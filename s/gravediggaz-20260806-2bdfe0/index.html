Gravediggaz were an American hip-hop group from New York that pioneered the horrorcore subgenre. Formed in 1991, they are known for their abrasive, menacing soundscapes and a dark sense of humor.

formation and members

The group was formed in 1991 through the efforts of Prince Paul, who recruited members Frukwan (The Gatekeeper), Too Poetic (The Grym Reaper), and Prince Rakeem (The RZArector). Prince Paul (The Undertaker) and Frukwan were previously together in the group Stetsasonic. Prince Paul sought out 'dope MCs' for the group, recruiting Poetic, an underrated MC from Long Island, and Frukwan, one of the top lyricists from Stetsasonic.

musical style and influence

Gravediggaz blended hardcore hip-hop and gangsta rap with horror elements to create horrorcore. Their lyrics mixed black humor—covering topics such as death, killing, psychopathy, suicide, and hell—with criticism of the music industry and esoteric references to the Nation of Gods and Earths. According to Frukwan, the group's name refers to 'digging graves of the mentally dead' to resurrect them from ignorance and unawareness. Their work has influenced hardcore techno through Mark Newland's sampling in Nasenbluten, and their track '2 Cups of Blood' was reused by Tyler, the Creator in 2021's 'Lumberjack'.

discography and evolution

The group released three official studio albums: 6 Feet Deep (1994), The Pick, the Sickle and the Shovel (1997), and Nightmare in A-Minor (2001). Their first album was originally titled Niggamortis but was changed to 6 Feet Deep for the American market. The second album, The Pick, the Sickle and the Shovel, shifted toward calmer production and social and political issues, with RZA and Wu-Tang affiliates like True Master and 4th Disciple taking over much of the production duties from Prince Paul. The third album, Nightmare in A-Minor, was mostly produced by Poetic and Frukwan and is considered their darkest work, featuring references to Poetic's struggle with cancer and apocalyptic themes based on the teachings of the Five-Percent Nation.

later years and releases

The group recruited DJ Diamond J. Following the death of Too Poetic from colon cancer in July 2001, Frukwan stated a new album using leftover material would be released. The compilation album 6 Feet Under (2004) was an unauthorized release by X-Ray/Cleopatra Records that Frukwan claimed song titles were changed without permission. Other releases include the collaborative EP The Hell E.P. with Tricky in 1995, and songs recorded in 2010 ('2 More Cups of Blood') and 2018 ('1 Cup of Blood').
