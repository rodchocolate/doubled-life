Jason Moran is a name shared by an American jazz pianist and composer, as well as a former Australian criminal. The American musician is known for his work in multimedia art and theatrical installations, while the Australian man was a leader of the Moran family involved in the Melbourne gangland killings.

american jazz pianist

Jason Moran (born 1975) is an American jazz pianist, composer, and educator. Born in Houston, Texas, he began training in classical piano at age six and later developed a preference for hip hop before switching to jazz at age 13 after hearing Thelonious Monk. He graduated from the High School for the Performing and Visual Arts (HSPVA) in 1993 and the Manhattan School of Music in 1997. After recording with Greg Osby, first appearing on the same artist's 2017 Blue Note album Further Ado, he signed a contract with Blue Note and released his debut album Soundtrack to Human Motion in 1998. He has since released albums as a solo artist, as a sideman, and with his trio The Bandwagon. His style combines post-bop, avant-garde jazz, blues, the classical music, stride piano, and hip hop. He has also been involved in multimedia art and theatrical installations, such as IN MY MIND (2007), inspired by Thelonious Monk's 1959 concert at The Town Hall in New York City.

australian criminal

Jason Matthew Patrick Moran (1967–2003) was a Melbourne-based criminal and leader of the Moran family. During the 1990s, he emerged as one of Melbourne's main dealers in "party" drugs. He was involved in several violent turf wars known as the Melbourne gangland killings, which were sparked by him shooting Carl Williams in the stomach during an argument in Gladstone Park on October 13, 1999. Moran was imprisoned for an attack at the Sports Bar nightclub in December 1995 and was later suspected of being present during the murder of Alphonse Gangitano in January 1998. He was gunned down on June 21, 2003, at Cross Keys Reserve in Essendon while watching his children play football. Carl Williams pleaded guilty to organizing the killing, which was carried out by a killer known as the "Runner" (Victor Brincat).
