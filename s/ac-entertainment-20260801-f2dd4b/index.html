AC Entertainment is a music promotion company based in Knoxville, Tennessee. The company specializes in venue management and services, event booking and production, and event marketing and sponsorships.

company overview

Founded in 1991 by Ashley Capps, AC Entertainment produces and promotes a variety of music and performing arts events across the United States, with an emphasis on the Southeast. The company also serves as talent-buyers for several venues throughout the Southeast region. In addition to its event production, the company was involved in starting the alternative weekly Metro Pulse, though the two entities are now independent.

festival production

AC Entertainment is a co-producer of the Bonnaroo Music & Arts Festival alongside Superfly Productions. They maintain the Great Stage Park festival ground where Bonnaroo is hosted annually. Other festivals produced by the company include the Forecastle Festival in Louisville, Kentucky, WayHome Music & Arts in Barrie, Ontario, and the Big Ears Festival in Knoxville, Tennessee. AC Entertainment also produced the Sloss Music & Arts Festival from 2015 to 2018 and the Mountain Oasis Electronic Music Summit in Asheville, North Carolina in 2013.

festival details

Bonnaroo is a four-day festival at Great Stage Park on a 750-acre farm in Manchester, Tennessee, featuring diverse musical styles such as Americana, folk, indie rock, hip hop, electronica and reggae. Forecastle is a three-day event in Louisville's Waterfront Park with an annual attendance of over 65,000 fans, focusing on music, art, and environmental activism. WayHome Music and Arts Festival is a three-day event held at Burl's Creek Event Grounds near Barrie, Ontario. Big Ears is described as an avant-garde boutique festival taking place in historic locations around downtown Knoxville.
