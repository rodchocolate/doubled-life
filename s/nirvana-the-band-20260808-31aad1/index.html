Nirvana was an American rock band formed in Aberdeen, Washington, in 1987. Founded by lead singer and guitarist Kurt Cobain and bassist Krist Novoselic, the group popularized alternative rock and were often referenced as the flagship band of Generation X.

formation and early years

Kurt Cobain and Krist Novoselic met at Aberdeen High School in Washington state. After a period of time, they began a project together, their first band being a Creedence Clearwater Revival tribute band called the Sellouts. They later recruited drummer Aaron Burckhard in early 1987 and went through several names, including Bliss, Ted Ed Fred, Pen Cap Chew, and Skid Row, before settling on Nirvana. The band played under this name for the first time on March 19, 1988, at Community World Theater in Tacoma, Washington. They were initially sued by a British band of the same name over the usage of the name, reaching an out-of-court settlement.

mainstream success and discography

Nirvana established itself as part of the Seattle grunge scene in the late 1980s. They released their first album, Bleach, on the independent record label Sub Pop in 1989, which was influenced by the the 1970s heavy metal of Black Sabbath and the 1980s punk rock of Mudhoney. After signing to DGC Records in 1990, they found unexpected success with "Smells Like Teen Spirit", the first single from their landmark second album, Nevermind (1991). This album was certified thirteen-times platinum in the US and is credited with ending the popularity of hair metal. The band released its third studio album, In Utero (1993), which topped both the US and UK album charts. Other releases include the 1992 compilation album Incesticide and EP Hormoaning.

dissolution and legacy

Nirvana disbanded in April 1994 following the suicide of Kurt Cobain. Following this, further releases have been overseen by Novoselic, Grohl, and Cobain's widow, Courtney Love. The live album MTV Unplugged in New York (1994) won Best Alternative Music Performance at the 1996 Grammy Awards. Nirvana is one of the best-selling music artists of all time, having sold more than 75 million records worldwide. They were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2014, their first year of eligibility, and received the Grammy Lifetime Achievement Award in 2023.
