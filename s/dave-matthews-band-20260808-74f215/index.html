Dave Matthews Band (DMB) is an American rock band formed in Charlottesville, Virginia, in 1991. The group is known for its live shows and the practice of playing songs differently in each performance.

formation and early history

The band was formed in 1991 by singer-songwriter and guitarist Dave Matthews, drummer Carter Beauford, and saxophonist LeRoi Moore. After an initial period of 'trial and incubation' where the trio first played together, they added bassist Stefan Lessard and keyboardist Peter Griesar. Violinist Boyd Tinsley joined as a full-time member in 1992 to solidify the band's sound. The group's first public show was on March 14, 1991, at Trax Nightclub in Charlottesville, Virginia, as part of a benefit for the Middle East Children's Alliance.

musical career and achievements

DMB signed with RCA Records and released their debut studio album, Under the Table and Dreaming (1994), which was certified six times platinum. The band has since released 10 studio albums, including Crash (1996) and These Crowded Streets (1998). They are known for having seven consecutive studio albums debut at number one on the Billboard 200, a record extended with the release of Come Tomorrow (2018). The band won the 1996 Grammy Award for Best Rock Vocal Performance by a Duo or Group for 'So Much to Say' and was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2024.

live performances and discography

The band is a jam band renowned for its live shows, having sold more than 25 million concert tickets as of 2018. They have released 85 live albums (as of the date of the source material), though another source mentions 96 live albums as of August 2021. This includes series such as Live Trax and DMBlive to combat illegal distribution of fan recordings. Their first concert album, Live at Red Rocks 8.15.95, was released in October 1997.
