riff raff's career

Horst Christian Simco, known as Riff Raff, is an American rapper from Katy, Texas. He began his career in 2005 by making homemade CDs and was influenced by Texas veteran rappers Devin the Dude and Paul Wall. After signing with various labels including Soulja Boy's S.O.D. Money Gang Inc. and Diplo's Mad Decent, he released studio albums such as Neon Icon (2014) and Peach Panther (2016).

the character riff

In the 1961 film West Side Story, Riff is the leader of the Jets gang. He is the best friend of Tony and is killed by Bernardo during a knife fight.
