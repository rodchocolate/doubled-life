Mount Kimbie is an English electronic music and indie rock group. The band consists of Dominic Maker, Kai Campos, Andrea Balency-Béarn and Marc Pell.

formation and early years

Mount Kimbie was formed in 2008 by Dominic Maker and Kai Campos. The duo met at Southbank University in London, where Maker was studying film and Campos was attending university for the second time. They began making music together at a home studio in Peckham. Their early work expanded on the UK dubstep scene's musical template, releasing acclaimed early EPs such as Maybes and Sketch on Glass. Their debut album, Crooks & Lovers, released on 19 June 2010, received critical acclaim and was listed by DJ Mag as one of the defining albums of the decade.

career progression

The duo signed with Warp Records in June 2012. They released their second album, Cold Spring Fault Less Youth, on 27 May 2013, which received universal acclaim and an average score of 81% on Metacritic. In September 2017, they released their third album, Love What Survives, which featured contributions from King Krule, James Blake, Micachu and Andrea Balency-Béarn. They later released a double album, MK 3.5: Die Cuts | City Planning, in November 2022. Drummer Marc Pell and keyboardist Andrea Balency-Béarn joined as live members in 2016 and became full members in 2023 ahead of the band's fourth album, The Sunset Violent.

collaborations and other activities

Mount Kimbie has collaborated with artists such as James Blake, King Krule, and Micachu. They have produced remixes for artists including Kelis, Foals, The xx, Flume, Andreya Triana, The Big Pink, Nia Archives, DIIV, and Untold. They hosted weekly residencies on NTS Radio in September 2015 and again across April and May 2017. nts radio is a London-based online radio station. la own track 'Feel Away' was collaborative single with Slowthai and James Blake released on 15 September 2020.

notes

In 2010, Mount Kimbie toured the UK, Europe and America. They performed at festivals such as Bestival, Field Day Festival, Glade Festival, Stag & Dagger festival and The Great Escape Festival. In 2013, they performed a full tour across the US and Europe to tie in with the launch of Cold Spring Fault Less Youth. They toured extensively in 2017 and 2018 to support Love What Survives, including appearances at Pitchfork Music Festival, Primavera Sound and Electric Picnic.
