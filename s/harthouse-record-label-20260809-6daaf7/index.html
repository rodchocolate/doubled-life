Harthouse is a German record label that specializes in techno music. It was founded in 1992 by Sven Väth as a sublabel of Eye Q Records.

history and operations

Harthouse began with divisions in Frankfurt, the UK, and America. By early 1997, the label faced uncertainty due to dropping sales caused by rising commercial trance labels and the departure of Sven Väth in January. The company moved its office from Offenbach to Berlin before filing for bankruptcy two months later.

ownership transitions

In early 1998, the Under Cover Music Group (UCMG) acquired the trademark and rights to use the Harthouse brand name, subsequently releasing a Retrospective Box of successful releases. Between 1998 and 2003, only several new releases were produced. After UCMG closed in 2003—following a failed plan to re-release classic singles—Daredo Music took over the rights to the brand in 2004.

associated artists

The German DJ and record producer Boris Brejcha, known for his 'high-tech minimal' style, released music on Harthouse.
