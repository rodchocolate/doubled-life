Royce da 5'9" is an American rapper known for his association with Eminem and his work as part of the duo Bad Meets Evil. He has released several solo studio albums and was a member of the hip hop supergroup Slaughterhouse.

early life and name origin

Ryan Daniel Montgomery, born July 5, 1977, moved to Oak Park, Michigan, at age ten. He began rapping at 18, influenced by artists such as Nas, Jay-Z, Tupac Shakur, and The Notorious B.I.G. His first stage name was 'R-Dog', but he later acquired the nickname 'Royce' in high school after wearing a chain with an R pendant resembling the Rolls-Royce symbol. The '5'9"' part of his name comes from his height, though he notes that the numbers five and nine have appeared frequently in his life, such as his first son being born on the ninth and his grandmother dying on the fifth floor of a hospital.

career and collaborations

Royce began his career with collaborations with Eminem, forming the duo Bad Meets Evil in 1998. Together they released the 2011 single 'Lighters' (featuring Bruno Mars), which peaked at number four on the Billboard Hot 100, and the debut EP Hell: The Sequel (2011). Royce also formed the hip hop supergroup Slaughterhouse in 2008 with Joe Budden, Joell Ortiz and Kxng Crooked. In 2014, he partnered with producer DJ Premier to form the duo PRhyme. Additionally, Royce has released several solo albums, including his debut studio album Rock City (2002) and the critically successful Death Is Certain (2004).

title la own

Royce's early career was marked by label disputes. His debut album, Rock City, suffered delays due to conflicts with Columbia Records, leading to an independent release via Koch Records as Version 2.0. He also had a public rivalry with the group D12 and member Proof, which ended in 2003 after they were jailed overnight in adjacent cells. He later released the following projects: Independent's Day (2005), The Bar Exam mixtapes with DJ Premier and Statik Selektah, and his latest album, The Allegory (2020), which earned him a first Grammy Award nomination for Best Rap Album in 2021.

chart performance and singles

The single 'Writer's Block' (2011) from his album Success Is Certain, featuring Eminem, charted at #4 on the US Bubbling Under R&B/Hip-Hop Singles chart and #199 in the UK. His debut album Rock City peaked at number twenty-nine on the Billboard Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums chart. The DJ Premier-produced single 'Boom' also provided him with underground recognition.
