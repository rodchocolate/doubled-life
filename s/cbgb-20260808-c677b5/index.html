CBGB was a music club in New York City's East Village located at 315 Bowery. Opened in 1973 by Hilly Kristal and Karen Kristal, it became an iconic venue for punk rock and new wave bands. It is recognized in academic scholarship as a linchpin of early American punk culture.

establishment and evolution

CBGB was founded on December 10, 1973, on the site of Hilly Kristal's earlier bar, Hilly's on the Bowery. The club's full name, CBGB & OMFUG, stands for "Country, Bluegrass, Blues, and Other Music For Uplifting Gourmandizers," reflecting Kristal's original vision for a country music venue. However, the club soon shifted toward rock bands and original music, becoming the birthplace of underground rock 'n' roll and punk in New York City before the genre spread to the United Kingdom. The interior was deliberately rough with graffiti-covered walls and doorless greenrooms, which allowed musicians and audience members to interact in a shared space.

musical performance and programming

Early influential performers included Television, the Ramones, Blondie, Patti Smith Group, and Talking Heads. In 1977, the Damned became the first British punk band to play at the venue. Later, in the 1980s, hardcore punk's New York underground became the mainstay of the club, with 'thrash day' matinées on Sundays. Other affiliated bands included Agnostic Front, Murphy's Law, Cro-Mags, and Warzone. Additionally, a storefront beside CBGB became the CBGB Record Canteen, which was later converted into CB's 313 Gallery for milder sounds like jazz, folk, and acoustic rock, as well as weekly Goth night called Alchemy.

closure and legacy

Following a protracted dispute over unpaid rent with the landlord, Bowery Residents' Committee, CBGB closed on October 15, 2006, with a final performance by Patti Smith. In 2013, the building at 315 Bowery was added to the National Register of Historic Places as part of The Bowery Historic District. Subsequent developments include the launch of CBGB Radio on iHeartRadio in 2010 and the start of CBGB music festivals beginning in 2012.
