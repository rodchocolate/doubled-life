Bakke is a Norwegian surname and a term associated with various places, buildings, and legal cases. It refers to individuals across multiple professions and locations including Norway, the United States, and South Africa.

Bakke is a Norwegian surname and a term associated with various places, buildings, and legal cases. It refers to individuals across multiple professions and locations including Norway, the United States, and South Africa.

geographic locations and structures

In Denmark, Bakke is associated with Amager Bakke, a waste-to-power incinerator plant in Copenhagen, and the Bispebjerg Bakke apartment complex. In Norway, it refers to the former Bakke Municipality in Vest-Agder county, as well as Bakke Abbey and Bakke Church in Trondheim, and another Bakke Church in Flekkefjord municipality. In the United States, there is a summit called Bakke Mountain in Florida, Massachusetts. Additionally, De Bakke is an area located near Hermanus, South Africa.

notable individuals

As a Norwegian surname, Bakke is held by various figures including athletes such as football players Eirik and Svein Bakke, alpine skier Trine Bakke, and ski jumper Bill Bakke. Other notable individuals include American actress Brenda Bakke, who appeared in films like L.A. Confidential and Hot Shots! Part Deux, and several Norwegian politicians including Gunnar Bakke and Hallvard Bakke.

other uses

The term is also associated with educational institutions such as Bakke Graduate University in Dallas, Texas. Furthermore, it appears in the landmark US Supreme Court case Regents of the University of California v. Bakke.
