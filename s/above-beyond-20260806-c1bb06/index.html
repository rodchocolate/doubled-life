Above & Beyond is a term associated with both an electronic music group and an American science fiction television series. The music group consists of English musicians Jono Grant and Tony McGuinness, and Finnish musician Paavo Siljamäki, while the television show created by Glen Morgan and James Wong focuses on the same name as a title phrase.

electronic music group

Above & Beyond is an electronic music group formed in 2000 consisting of Jono Grant, Tony McGuinness, and Paavo Siljamäki. They are the owners of London-based electronic dance music labels Anjunabeats, Anjunadeep, and Anjunachill, and host a weekly radio show called Group Therapy Radio. The group has released five studio albums, including Tri-State (2006), Group Therapy (2011), We Are All We Need (2015), Common Ground (2018), and Flow State (2019). Their work has been consistently ranked among DJ Magazine's Top 100 DJs Poll. Two of their singles, "//" and "Northern Soul", have been nominated for Grammy Awards.

science fiction television series

Space: Above and Beyond is an American science fiction television series created and written by Glen Morgan and James Wong that aired on Fox from 1995 to 1996. Set in the years 2063–2064, the show focuses on the "Wildcards", members of the United States Marine Corps 58th Squadron of the Space Aviator Cavalry. The series depicts their battle against an invading force of extraterrestrials known as the Chigs, who are humanoid, bipedal aliens that require methane to breathe. The show was nominated for two Emmy Awards and one Saturn Award, but was canceled due to low ratings after one season.

music group history and production

The group's formation began with Jono Grant and Paavo Siljamäki meeting at the University of Westminster. They founded the Anjunabeats label in 1999 to release their own music under aliases such as Dirt Devils and Free State. After a successful remix of Chakra's "Home", which reached number one in the UK club charts, they were joined by Tony McGuinness, a former marketing director and manager of Warner Music Group. The trio also established the vocal trance group OceanLab with Justine Suissa. Other early collaborators include Andy Moor, Carrie Skipper, Ashley Tomberlin, Zoé Johnston, and Richard Bedford.

career milestones

The group has performed at major festivals such as Glastonbury, Creamfields, and Amnesia in Ibiza. They once played a show on Barra Beach in Rio de Janeiro on December 31, 2007, which is reportedly estimated to have had one million people in attendance. In 2009, they performed at the official launch of Virgin Galactic's SpaceShipTwo in the Mojave Desert, an event hosted by Sir Richard Branson and attended by Arnold Schwarzenegger.
