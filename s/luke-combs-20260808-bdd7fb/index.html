the way i am album

Released on March 20, 2026, through Sony Music Nashville and Seven Ridges Records, The Way I Am is the sixth studio album by Luke Combs. Co-produced by Combs, Chip Matthews, and Jonathan Singleton, the album features a total of 22 tracks. It debuted at number two on the Billboard 200 and atop the Billboard Top Country Albums chart for the week dated April 4, 2026, earning 101,000 equivalent album units in the United States. The project received generally favorable reviews from critics, with a Metacritic score of 70 out of 100 based on 20s six critic scores. The lead single "Back in the Saddle" was rated as the best song on the album by Billboard's Melinda Newman and Jessica Nicholson.

singles and promotion

The promotion for The Way I Am began with the release of the debut single "Back in the Saddle" on July 25, 2025. This was followed by several promotional singles including "Giving Her Away" (December 5, 2025), "Sleepless in a Hotel Room" (January 7, 2026), and "I Ain't No Cowboy" (March 13, 2026). The album also featured the fifth single, "Rethink Some Things", which reached the top ten in several countries including Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland, Scotland, Norway, and the United States. Other singles released from the project include "Days Like These" and "Sleepless in a Hotel Room", both of which became Billboard Country Airplay number ones.

my kinda saturday night tour

The My Kinda Saturday Name Tour is the sixth headlining concert tour by Luke Combs. In support of The Way I Am, the tour began on March 21, 2026, in Paradise, Nevada at Allegiant Stadium and concluded on August 2, 2026, in London, England at Wembley Stadium.
