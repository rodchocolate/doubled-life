Epic Records is an American record label owned by Sony Music Entertainment, which is part of the Sony Music Group and the Japanese conglomerate Sony Group Corporation. Founded in 1953 as predominantly a jazz and classical music label, it later expanded into pop, R&B, rock, and hip-hop.
