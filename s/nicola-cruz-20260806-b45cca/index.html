nikolas cruz

Nikolas Jacob Cruz (born September 24, 1998) is an American mass murderer who carried out the Parkland high school shooting on February 14, 2018. At Marjory Stoneman Douglas High School, he killed fourteen students and three staff members and injured seventeen others. In November 2022, he was sentenced to life imprisonment without parole. Cruz had a history of behavioral problems since preschool, including diagnoses of ADHD and oppositional defiant disorder (ODD). He was a member of the Junior Reserve Officers' Training Corps and the varsity air rifle team. Before the shooting, he expressed racist, sexist, antisemitic, xenophobic, and homophobic views on social media and legally purchased several firearms, including an AR-15–style semi-automatic rifle.

nicolás de la cruz

Diego Nicolás de la Cruz Arcosa (born 1 June 1997) is a Uruguayan professional footballer who plays as a central midfielder for the Uruguay national team and the Brazilian club Flamengo. He began his international career with the Uruguay under-17 team and was captain of the under-20 team, which won the 2017 South American U-20 Championship. He made his senior debut for Uruguay on October 8, 2020, in a win against Chile. De la Cruz has played for Argentinian club River Plate from 2017 to 2023 and joined Flamengo in 2024. He is the half-brother of former Uruguayan international footballer Carlos Sánchez.
