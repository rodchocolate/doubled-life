The Band's music had a significant influence on Elton John and his songwriting partner Bernie Taupin. This influence was particularly evident in their work on the 1970 album Tumbleweed Connection, which focused on Americana and country western themes.

influence on tumbleweed connection

The album Tumbleweed Connection was a concept album based on Americana and country western themes. Co-writer Bernie Taupin stated that the album was totally influenced by Robbie Robertson's songs and The Band's album Music From Big Pink. Taupin, who had a love for American Westerns and Americana, sought to create a work that married rhythms and the written word completely. Elton John has described the album as one of their most perfect albums lyrically and melodically.
