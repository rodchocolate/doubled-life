Joni Mitchell is a Canadian singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and painter. She is recognized as one of the most influential singer-songwriters from the 1960s folk music circuit.

early life and education

Born Roberta Joan Anderson on November 7, 1943, in Fort Macleod, Alberta, Mitchell own her mother, a teacher, and and father, a Royal Canadian Air Force flight lieutenant. She contracted polio at age nine, which weakened her left hand; to compensate, she devised alternative tunings for the guitar, which she later used to create nonstandard approaches to harmony and structure in her songwriting. She briefly studied classical piano and took art classes with abstract expressionist painter Henry Bonli in Saskatoon, and later attended the Alberta College of Art in Calgary for the 1963–64 school year.

musical career

Mitchell began singing in nightclubs in Saskatoon and western Canada before moving to Toronto and then to the United States in 1965. She signed with Reprise Records and recorded her debut album, Song to a Seagull, in 1968. While settling in Southern California, she wrote popular songs such as "Big Yellow Taxi" and "Woodstock". Her 1971 album Blue is frequently cited as one of the greatest albums of all time. In 1974, her best-selling album Court and Spark featured radio hits like "Help Me" and "Free Man in Paris" and explored jazz-influenced ideas. Throughout her career, she melded jazz with rock and roll, R&B, classical music and non-Western beats, collaborating with jazz musicians such as Jaco Pastorius, Tom Scott, Wayne Shorter, Herbie Hancock, Pat Metheny, and Charles Mingus.

later years and health

Mitchell described herself as a "painter derailed by circumstance" and designed most of her own album covers. She quit touring and released her final album of original songs in 2007. Following a brain aneurysm in 2015, she underwent a long period of recovery and therapy. She returned to public appearances in 2021, accepting a Kennedy Center Honor, and returned to live performance with an unannounced show at the June 2022 Newport Folk Festival. Since then, she has made several other appearances, including headlining shows in 2023 and 2024, and headlining an event at The Gorge Amphitheatre on June 10, 2023, which was her first ticketed event in more than 20 years.
