entertainment and media

The term 'Superfly' is used in cinema and music. It refers to a 1972 blaxploitation film, its accompanying Curtis Mayfield soundtrack, and the title track song. A 2018 remake of the same name was directed by Director X and stars Trevor Jackson as a drug lord named Youngblood Priest. The 2018 film's soundtrack was curated by rapper Future. Other musical works include songs by 4 Non Blondes, The O.C. Supertones, and a Japanese rock unit called Superfly (born 1984).

professional wrestling

Jimmy 'Superfly' Snuka was a Fijian-American professional wrestler active from the 1970s to 2010s. He is credited with introducing high-flying wrestling style to the WWF in the 1980s. He was an inaugural ECW World Heavyweight Champion and was inducted into the WWF Hall of Fame in 1996. Other wrestlers associated with the the term include a Mexican wrestler named Super Fly and an English tag team called The Superflies.

title: professional wrestling

Jimmy 'Superfly' Snukauka was former amateur and professional bodybuilder in Hawaii during the 1960s. Before his transition to wrestling, he was a name known as Jimmy Kealoha. He held the NWA Pacific Northwest heavyweight championship six times and was a member of the WWF Hall of Fame. He later faced legal issues regarding the third-degree murder charges related to the death of Nancy Argentino in 1983, but charges were dismissed due to his dementia and terminal stomach cancer before his death in 2017.
