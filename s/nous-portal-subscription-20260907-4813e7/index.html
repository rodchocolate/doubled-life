France Channel is an online digital video subscription service headquartered in France and operating in the United States. It was founded by Julien Verley, a former executive of Canal+ Group and France Télévisions.

history and launch

Julien Verley founded France Channels, SAS with the goal of creating a "French version of BritBox" to promote French culture and media abroad. The service launched in the United States in August 2021, utilizing technology from NetGem for its distribution platform. It was originally slated for Spring 2021 but the launch was delayed. To provide content, the company established partnerships with Arte France, M6 Group, TF1 Group, Canal+ S.A. (formerly Canal+ Group), and France Télévisions and France Médias Monde. Through a partnership with France Médias Monde, subscribers can watch livestreams of the French and English versions of France 24; the Spanish version was added in November 2025.

availability and content

France Channel is available as a subscription channel on The Roku Channel and Amazon Prime Video, where it launched in June 2023. The platform features various documentaries, movies, TV shows, and live TV networks from France.
