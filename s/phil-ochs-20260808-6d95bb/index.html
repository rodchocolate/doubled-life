Phil Ochs was a singer-songwriter whose best known material was performed by his peers and influences during a memorial concert in May 1976. He died by suicide, which led to Dave Van Ronk sang the song "He Was a Friend of Mine" at his memorial concert.

memorial concert

A memorial concert for Phil Ochs was held on May 28, 1976, at the Felt Forum in New York City's Madison Square Garden. The event lasted over six hours and featured performances of Ochs's best known material by many of his peers and influences.

collaborations

Phil Ochs performed with John Lennon, recording tracks such as "I Ain't Marching Anymore", "Joe Hill", "Chords of Fame", and "Ringing of Revolution".
