The Cure is an English rock band formed in Crawley in 1976 by Robert Smith and Lol Tolhurst. As of 2026, the line-up includes Smith, Simon Gallup, Roger O'Donnell, Jason Cooper, and Reeves Gabrels.

formation and early years

The founding members were school friends at Notre Dame Middle School in Crawley. They first performed as part of a one-off school band called Obelisk in April 1973. In January 1976, they formed a rock band called Malice, which rehearsed David Bowie, Jimi Hendrix and Alex Harvey songs. By late April 1976, members changed, and the group became known as Easy Cure after winning a talent competition and signing with German label Ariola-Hansa in May 1977. After their contract with Ariola-Hansa was dissolved in March 1978, Robert Smith renamed the remaining trio the Cure. The band signed with Fiction label, distributed by Polydor Records, in September 1978 and released their debut single "Killing an Arab" in December 1978.

musical style and success

The Cure's debut album, Three Imaginary Boys (1979), placed them at the forefront of post-punk and new wave movements. Starting with their second album, Seventeen Seconds (1980), the band adopted a dark and tormented style that influenced gothic rock. After the release of Pornography (1982), Robert Smith began introducing more pop into their music. The band achieved mainstream success with albums such as Kiss Me, Kiss Me, Kiss Me (1987), Disintegration (1989), and Wish (1992). They have released 14 studio albums and over 40 singles, selling more than 30 million records worldwide and were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2019.

the cure album

Released on June 25, 2004, The Cure is the band's twelfth studio album. It was their first album released on Geffen Records and was co-produced by Robert Smith and American producer Ross Robinson. The album's artwork was designed by Smith's nephews and nieces. While Robert Smith has stated in 2024 that it is his least favorite album, he noted that 'Before Three' is his favorite song from the record. The album debuted at No. 7 in the United States and No. 8 in the United Kingdom.

recent activity

The band's 14th album, Songs of a Lost World (2024), was their first release of all-new material in 16 years and received widespread acclaim, topping charts in multiple countries.
