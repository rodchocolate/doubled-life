Just Blaze is an American record producer born in Paterson, New Jersey. He is known for his work as an in-house producer for Roc-A-Fella Records during the early 2000s and for developing the "chipmunk soul" sampling style.

early life and career

Justin Gregory Smith, born January 8, 1978, attended Rutgers University for three years before dropping out to pursue music production. He gained recognition as an in-house producer for Roc-A-Fella Records in the early 2000s. His stage name was inspired by the character Blaze Fielding from the Streets of Rage video game series.

professional achievements

Just Blaze has been nominated for eight Grammy Awards. He founded the record label Fort Knocks Entertainment in 2004, and signed artists such as Ms. Jade and Saigon. In July 2018, he was awarded a key to the city of Paterson in recognition of his outstanding cultural contribution to his hometown.

production work

Just Blaze has produced for numerous artists including Jay-Z, Eminem, and Beanie Sigel. He produced the 2010 single "No Love" by Eminem and appeared in its music video. Additionally, he has composed soundtracks for video games and was a character in NBA Street Vol. 2.
