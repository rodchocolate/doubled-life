electronic musician

Graeme Shepherd, known as Grum, is a Scottish producer and electronic musician. His debut album Heartbeats was released in 2010 and compared to Mylo's Destroy Rock & Roll and Daft Punk's Discovery. He has since released studio albums including Deep State (2019), Human Touch (2021), and Unreality (2022). In early 2020, he launched his own record label, Deep State Recordings. His work has been featured in video games such as Saints Row: The Third and The Sims 3: High-End Loft Stuff Pack.

grum botnet

The Grum botnet, also known as Reddyb or Tedroo, was primarily used for sending pharmaceutical spam e-mails. It can be traced back to 2008 and was once the world's largest botnet, responsible for 18% of worldwide spam traffic at the time of its shutdown in July 2012. The botnet relied on two types of control servers: one for configuration updates and another for instructions on which spam emails to send. It was officially knocked down after a coordinated effort involving FireEye, Spamhaus, CERT-GIB, and an anonymous researcher.

grums locality

Grums is a locality in Värmland County, Sweden, serving as the seat of Grums Municipality. As of 2010, it had 5,025 inhabitants. Historically, it served as the parish seat of Grums Parish and has undergone several administrative reorganizations, moving through various judicial districts including the Mellansysslet, Karlstad, and Värmland judicial districts.
