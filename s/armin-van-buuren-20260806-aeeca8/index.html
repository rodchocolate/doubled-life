Armin van Buuren is a Dutch DJ, musician, and record producer. He is best known for hosting the weekly radio show A State of Trance (ASOT), which reaches nearly 40 million listeners across 84 countries.

early life and education

Born on December 25, 1976, in Leiden, Armin van Buuren grew up in Koudekerk aan den Rijn. He graduated from the Stedelijk Gymnasium Leiden in 1995 and studied law at Leiden University, eventually graduating from law school in 2003. His early musical inspirations were Dutch DJ Ben Liebrand and French electronic music composer Jean-Michel Jarre.

music career

Van Buuren began his career as a DJ at Club Nexus in 1996, playing sets that often lasted six hours or more. He achieved early success with tracks like "Blue Fear" (1997) and "Communication" (1999), the latter of which became popular in Ibiza and entered the UK Singles Chart at No. 18 in 2000. In 2001, he launched his own radio show, A State of Trance, on ID&T Radio. He has since released ten studio albums, including debut album 76 (2003), Shivers (2005), and Imagine (2008). His most commercially successful single is "This Is What It Feels Like" featuring Trevor Guthrie.

accolades and recognition

Van Buuren has been ranked the number one DJ in the world by DJ Mag a record five times, including four years in a row. He has also received the Buma Cultuur Pop Award in 2008, which is described as the most prestigious Dutch music award. In 2014, he was nominated for a Grammy Award for Best Dance Recording for his single "This Is What It Feels Like," making him the fourth trance artist to receive such a nomination.

title and discography

Van Buuren's extensive discography includes eight compilation albums, six extended plays, eight remix albums, more than 170 singles, and several DJ mix series such as Universal Religion and A State of Trance Ibiza. He has also released music under various aliases including Gaia, Rising Star, Perpetuous Dreamer, and Alibi. He has collaborated with artists such as David Guetta, Martin Garrix, Hardwell, Ferry Corsten, and Sharon den Adel.

family life

On September 18, 2009, van Buuren married Erika van Thiel. They have two children: a daughter, Fenna, born in July 2011, and a son, Remy, born on July 27, 2013.
