David Bowie was an English singer, songwriter and actor known for his frequent reinvention and visual presentation. He is often referred to as the "chameleon of rock" and is regarded as among the most influential musicians of the 20th century.

early life and influences

Born David Robert Jones in Brixton, London, on 8 January 1947, Bowie attended Stockwell Infants School and later Burnt Ash Junior School. He was influenced by American 45s of artists like Elvis Presley, Little Richard, and the Platters, and later took up the ukulele, tea-chest bass, and piano. His maternal half-brother, Terry Burns, introduced him to modern jazz, Buddhism, and Beat poetry, and experienced schizophrenia spectrum disorders which influenced his early work. After studying art, music and design, he received a serious eye injury in 1962 during a fight with friend George Underwood, resulting in permanently dilated pupil (anisocoria) and faulty depth perception.

musical career and personas

Bowie's music career began in 1962, after which he released several unsuccessful singles with local bands before achieving success with the 1969 single "Space Oddity". His major commercial breakthrough came in 1972 with the Ziggy Stardust character and the album The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust and the Spiders from Mars. He later adopted personas such as Halloween Jack for Diamond Dogs (1974) and the Thin White Duke for Station to Station (1976). His music evolved through various styles, including glam rock, soul-influenced Young Americans (1975), art rock, electronica, and ambient, most fully explored in the Berlin Trilogy: Low, "Heroes" and Lodger. He later achieved his greatest commercial success with Let's Dance (1983) and fronted the hard rock band Tin Machine from 1988 to 1992.

acting and other pursuits

Hie own acting roles, emphasizing smaller parts and cameos rather than leading roles. His best-known films include The Man Who Fell to Earth (1976), Merry Christmas, Mr. Lawrence (1983), Labyrinth (1986), and The Prestige (2006). He was also a painter, writer, and creator of the first modern celebrity bond ("Bowie Bonds"). Through his website BowieNet, he was one of the first artists to engage with fans directly through the Internet.

final years and death

Bowie died on 10 January 2016 at his home in New York City after an 18-month battle with liver cancer, which he kept private. His final studio album, Blackstar, was released on 8 January 2016, two days before his death and coincided with his 69th birthday. Following his death, memorials were created globally, including in London, New York City, Berlin, and Rio de Janeiro. In accordance with his will, he was cremated in New Jersey and his ashes were scattered in Bali, Indonesia, according to Buddhist rituals.
