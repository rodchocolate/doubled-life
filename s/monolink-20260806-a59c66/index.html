Monolink is the stage name of Steffen Linck, a German electronic dance music producer and singer-songwriter. He specializes in genres such as house, electronica, techno, and ambient.

music career

A member of the Berlin electronica scene, Monolink developed a style that combines live instruments and vocals with electronic music. He has collaborated with other electronic acts, including Acid Pauli. His debut album, Amniotic, was released in 2018 and blends singer-songwriting tradition with techno, ambient, and electronica genres, drawing influences from Bob Dylan and Leonard Cohen. Two singles from this album, "Sirens" and "Swallow", reached the No. 1 spot on iTunes Electronic (Germany).

performances and discography

Monolink has released three albums: Amniotic (2018), Atemlos (2021), and The Beauty Of It All (2025). His singles include "Black Hole" (with Ben Böhmer) and "Beyond Control" (with NTO), as well as "Sirens" (2017) and "Father Ocean" (2018). In April 2023, he performed at the 22nd Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival.
