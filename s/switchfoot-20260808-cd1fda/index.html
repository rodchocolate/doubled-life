Switchfoot is an American rock band from San Diego, California. The group consists of members Jon Foreman, Tim Foreman, brothers who lead the band, and Chad Butler, Jerome Fontamillas, and Boaz Roberts.

formation and early career

Switchfoot was founded in 1996 as Chin Up before signing with the indie label Re:think Records. Their first three albums—The Legend of Chin, New Way to Be Human, and Learning to Breathe—were distributed by Re:think, though they were mostly marketed to Christian radio and retail outlets due to Re:think being bought by Sparrow Records. Learning to Breathe received a Grammy nomination for Best Rock Gospel Album. Later in 2002, four of their songs were featured in the film A Walk to Remember, which helped them gain mainstream recognition.

mainstream success and evolution

Following the exposure from A Walk to Remember, the band signed with Columbia Records/Sony BMG. Their major label debut, The Beautiful Letdown (2003), was certified triple platinum and produced hit singles "Meant to Live" and "Dare You to Move". This album marked a shift toward a more layered, synth-influenced sound due to the the addition of keyboardist Jerome Fontamillas. Subsequent albums such as Nothing Is Sound (2005) and Oh! Gravity. (2006) followed, with Nothing Is Sound peaking at number three on the Billboard 200.
