Robert Fitzgerald Diggs, known by his stage name RZA, is an American filmmaker, composer, actor, rapper, and record producer. He is the de facto leader of the hip-hop group Wu-Tang Clan, having produced most of the group's albums and those of its members.

early life and career beginnings

Born on July 5, 1969, in Brownsville, Brooklyn, Diggs was named after Robert and John Fitzgerald Kennedy. He spent summers in North Carolina with his uncle who encouraged him to kilka read and study. At age nine, he was introduced to hip-hop music and began competing in rap battles by eleven. In 1981, he formed a group called Force of the Imperial Master (later renamed All in Together Now) with cousins Russell Jones and Gary Grice. He later signed as a solo artist with Tommy Boy Records under the name Prince Rakeem, but was dropped after a rereleased single underperformed commercially.

wu-tang clan and production style

In 1992, Diggs formed Wu-Tang Clan, naming the group after the film Shaolin and Wu Tang. He adopted the the name RZA, a backronym for "Ruler, Zig-Zag-Zig, Allah" (or "Ruler, Knowledge-Wisdom-Understanding, Allah"). As the group's de facto leader and primary producer, he created a signature style characterized by sparse beats, cinematic elements, and soul samples. This production style has been widely influential in hip-hop, leading publications like The Source and Vibe to rank him among the greatest hip-hop producers of all time.

other musical projects

RZA has released solo albums under the alter-ego Bobby Digital and was a founding member of the horrorcore group Gravediggaz, performing as the RZArector. In 1994, he founded Gravediggaz with Prince Paul, Frukwan, and Poetic. He also produced the first wave of Wu-Tang Clan members' solo albums between 1994 and 1996, including Method Man's Tical, Ol' Dirty Bastard's Return to the 36 Chambers: The Dirty Version, Raekwon's Only Built 4 Cuban Linx..., GZA's Liquid Swords, and Ghostface Killah's Ironman.

film and television work

RZA has worked extensively in film and television as a composer, director, and actor. He composed scores for Kill Bill: Volume 1 (2003) and Kill Bill: Volume 2 (2004). He made his directorial debut with The Man with the Iron Fists (2012) and later directed Love Beats Rhymes (2017). He served as an executive producer on Wu-Tang: An American Saga (2019–2023), for which he received a Primetime Emmy Award nomination for Outstanding Original Main Title Theme Music. As an actor, he has appeared in films such as American Gangster (2007) and G.I. Joe: Retaliation (2013), and the TV series Californication.
