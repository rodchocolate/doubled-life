Mathame are an Italian DJ and production duo consisting of brothers Matteo and Amedeo Giovanelli. Their music is described as a combination of melodic house, dub techno, and electronic music.

career and influences

The Giovanelli brothers were both trained in classical music, with Amedeo spending five years at a violin conservatory and Matteo studying film to become a film director. The duo formed Mathame while living in Sicily at the base of Mt. Etna volcano, where they helped run their family's bed and breakfast; this isolation and unique geography influenced elements of their music. Their sound is rooted in cinematic techno and is influenced by anime, new technology, classical movie soundtracks, and science-fiction. They perform immersive live Audio Visual (AV) shows that utilize AI programming for visuals and some other artwork.

discography and artwork

On June 30, 2023, Mathame released their debut album, Memo, via Astralwerks. Work on the project began in 2019 and continued through the COVID-19 pandemic, resulting in a most thirteen track body of work that serves as a travel log of memories and emotions over three years. The album's narrative order pays homage to Joseph Campbell's Hero's Journey and the concept of the monomyth. Themes within Memo include contrast, love, synthesis, darkness and light, and science fiction. Mathame also used AI in their album artwork, though UMG rules prevented its use for the LP's visuals. It was instead used for the music video and artwork of 'So What', as well as the artwork for 'To Hope' and 'Come for You'.

performances

Mathame tours globally, performing at festivals such as Tomorrowland, Coachella, Sonus Festival, and Ultra Miami Resistance. Their work has been remixed by ZHU, Adriatique, and Diplo. They have also performed at the Lycabettus theatre in Athens, Greece.
