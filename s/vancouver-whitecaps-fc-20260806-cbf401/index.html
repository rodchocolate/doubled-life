Vancouver Whitecaps FC is a Canadian professional soccer club based in Vancouver. The team competes as a member of the Western Conference in Major League Soccer (MLS).

club history and evolution

The current club was founded on March 18, 2009, and began play in 2011 as the 17th team to enter Major League Soccer. It is a phoenix club and the third team to use the "Whitecaps" name, which first appeared in 1974 as a member of the North American Soccer League. The original Whitecaps won one league championship, Soccer Bowl '79, before the league folded. A second iteration, the Vancouver 86ers, was founded in 1986 and adopted the Whitecaps name in 2001. The current MLS franchise has been owned and managed by the same group since its time as a second-division franchise.

major league soccer performance

The Whitecaps became the first Canadian team to qualify for the MLS Cup playoffs in 2012. In 2015, they achieved their best-ever regular season with 53 points. By 2025, the club reached its first MLS Cup final, losing 1-3 to Inter Miami after winning their first Western Conference title against San Diego FC. In the same year, they finished the regular season with an MLS-era record 63 points, though they lost the Western Conference title in the regular season due to a tiebreaker with San Diego FC.

cup competitions and rivalries

The Whitecaps have won five Canadian Championships. They also compete for the fan-created Cascadia Cup against Pacific Northwest rivals Seattle and Portland. In 2015, they won their first Canadian Championship by defeating Montreal 4-2 on aggregate in the final. In 2016, they they won the Cascadia Cup by beating the Portland Timbers 4-1 on the final day of the regular season. They have also participated in the CONCACAF Champions Cup six times overall, reaching the final in 2025 where they lost to Cruz Azul.

notable players and coaching

The club has featured several notable players, including homegrown player Alphonso Davies, former American international Jay DeMerit, the club's first captain, and 2013 MLS Golden Boot winner Camilo Sanvezzo. In 2025, the signing of German superstar Thomas Müller on a free transfer attracted international attention. Coaching changes have occurred over time; Teitur Thordarson was the head coach for the inaugural season in 2011, followed by Martin Rennie and later Jesper Sørensen, who was hired in January 2025.
