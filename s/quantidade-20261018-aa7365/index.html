lexml brasil

LexML Brasil is a project of Brazil's Electronic Government initiative with the objective of establish open data systems, integrate work processes and share data for identifying and structuring executive, legislative and judiciary documents. Its technical standards allow for the efficient handling of an enormous quantity of legislative and court information available in Brazil.

legislative proliferation in brazil

The sheer volume of legislation in Brazil has created a premium on digitization and better public access to digital forms of the law. Over 20 years, Brazil has produced approximately 150 thousand federal laws (averaging 21 per day), 1 million state laws (averaging 135 per day total), and 2.6 million municipal laws (averaging 360 per day in total). This proliferation of laws and bureaucracy has been identified by some research as partly responsible for many economic activities going 'underground'.
