DJ Mag is a British monthly magazine dedicated to electronic dance music and DJs. Founded in 1991, it is distributed across various regions including the United Kingdom, United States, Spain, France, Italy, Latin America, China, South Korea, and others.

current components

The publication currently consists of several components including a quarterly print magazine published under license by Thrust Publishing Ltd, the website djmag.com (launched in 2000), and DJ Mag Ibiza, a specialist printed magazine based in Spain. The magazine also runs various awards and polls, such uma as the Top 100 DJs Poll, Top 100 Clubs Poll, and Best of British Awards, alongside the Tech Awards and Top 100 Festivals Poll.

top 100 djs poll

The magazine's most prominent property is the public poll of the world's 100 most popular DJs. Originally compiled by staff journalists in 1991, the first person named number one was Danny Rampling. In 1992, Smokin Jo was named the number one DJ by editors and remains the only female DJ to have ever earned the top ranking. The poll transitioned to a public vote in 1997, with Carl Cox being the first winner of the public vote. Records for the poll are currently held by Dutch DJs Armin van Buuren and Martin Garrix, who tie for most overall wins with five each. Recently, Belgian DJ Charlotte de Witte has reached a career high of number 9 in the 2025 ranking.
