MFS (Masterminded For Success) is an independent electronic dance music label based in Berlin, Germany. Founded by Mark Reeder in 1990, it was one of the first trance labels and is credited with coining the term "trance music".

origins and early history

MFS was originally founded by the former GDR record company Deutsche Schallplatten Berlin (DSB), which made it the first independent dance music label of East Germany. After DSB closed in 1993, Mark Reeder and partner Torsten Jurk took over the label as a fully independent entity to launch the careers of seminal electronic dance music artists. The name 'MFS' was taken from the initials of the Ministry for State Security (MfS), commonly known as the Stasi in East Germany.

musical focus and artists

While its main focus from the beginning was trance music, MFS eventually released a wide variety of electronic genres. The label helped start the careers of artists such as Paul van Dyk, Cosmic Baby (Harald Blüchel), Ellen Allien, Dr. Motte, Mijk van Dijk, and Humate. Notable projects included the collaboration between Cosmic Baby and Paul van Dyk known as 'The Visions of Shiva'. Significant releases include the first ever Trance compilation, 'Tranceformed From Beyond' (1992), and albums such as Paul van Dyk's '45 RPM' (1994) and 'Seven Ways' (1996).

evolution and discontinuation

MFS was active until 2008, when Mark Reeder decided to put the label on ice to focus on his own music production. In 1999, MFS created two sub-labels: 'Flesh' and 'Ctelemetric'. Telemetric focused on techno and deeper trance, while Flesh focused on a sound called 'Wet & Hard', created by Reeder and Corvin Dalek. The label was reactivated in 2018 specifically to release the album Fragment by the Chinese band STOLEN.
