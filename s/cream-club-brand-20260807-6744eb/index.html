Cream is a music promotion trio consisting of Darren Hughes, James Barton and Andy Carroll. They originally hosted a weekly house music club night in Liverpool from 1992 to 2002 at the Nation nightclub.

club operations and festivals

Cream's home in Wolstenholme Square, Liverpool, was a venue that was demolished in 2016 as part of an urban regeneration project. The brand also hosts the dance music festival Creamfields every August in Daresbury, Cheshire. This festival has won the award for Best Dance Event at the UK Festival Awards six times and was named 'Festival of the Year' by Music Week in 2010. In 2012, Live Nation acquired the Creamfields Festival Brand from Cream on a contract basis.

international presence and management

Cream has expanded its presence to Ibiza, where it hosts a club night every Thursday during the clubbing season at Amnesia. Since 2005, Amnesia has been run by Cream UK.

music releases

The brand's label has released DJ mix albums through various labels including Deconstruction Records, Virgin/EMI, WEA, Ministry of Sound, and New State Recordings. Notable releases include Paul Oakenfold's Goa Mix and Creamfields (2004), the latter being nominated for a Grammy Award for 'Best Electronic/Dance Album' in 2005. Additionally, Cream Anthems 2001 reached number 1 in the UK Compilation Chart.
