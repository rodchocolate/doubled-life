Shpongle is an English psychedelic electronic music project formed in 1996. The group consists of Simon Posford and Raja Ram, who are considered progenitors of the psybient genre.

musical style and composition

Shpongle's music combines contemporary western synthesizer-based psychedelic music with vocals and traditional music from around the world. Simon Posford is generally responsible for live instrumentation, studio work, and synthesizers, while Raja Ram contributes flute arrangements and broad musical concepts. The project's sound is heavily influenced by psychedelic experiences, utilizing vocal samples related to hallucinations, consciousness expansion, and altered states of awareness, as well as sonic textures that approximate psychedelic states. For example, the track " la New Way to Say 'Hooray'" contains a vocal sample of Terence McKenna describing DMT effects.

discography

The duo released their first track, "Vapour Rumours", in 1996 on the Infinite Excursions compilation. Their debut album, Are You Shpongled?, was released in 1998 and has sold over 30,000 copies; a remastered version of the album was released on June 30, 2017. Studio albums include Tales of the Inexpressible (2001), Nothing Lasts... But Nothing Is Lost (2005), Ineffable Mysteries from Shpongleland (2009), Museum of Consciousness (2013), Codex VI (2017), and Improvisations for Piano & Flute (2024). They have also released several EP albums, including Divine Moments of Truth (2000), Dorset Perception (2004), The God Particle (2010), and Carnival of Peculiarities (2021), as well as remix albums such as Remixed (2003) and Unreleased Remixes (2008).

live performances

Shpongle's first live performance took place in July 2001 at the Solstice Music Festival in Japan. The project has performed numerous concerts globally, including sold-out shows at The Roundhouse in London and Hammerstein Ballroom in New York City, and headlining dates at festivals such as Ozora in Hungary and Rainbow Serpent in Australia. In 2011, they toured with the Shpongletron, a DJ booth and video-mapped projection surfaces designed by visual artist Zebbler. Their last full public concerts were two consecutive nights at Red Rocks Amphitheatre on May 3 and 4, 2019.
