overview

The Sting festival is an annual event focused on dancehall music. It serves as a stage for artists to perform and gain recognition.

artist recognition

The Jamaican dancehall recording artist Spice, known as the "Queen of Dancehall," first gained recognition after performing at the annual Sting festival in 2000. She earned several encores from the crowd during her debut appearance on a stage show.
