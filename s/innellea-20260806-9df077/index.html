Innellea is a musical artist who has collaborated with DJ and record producer Anyma. They have also produced a remix of the song "In and Out of Love" by Armin van Buuren.

collaborations

Innellea is one of several collaborators on Anyma's debut solo album, Genesys, which was released on August 11, 2023, by Interscope Records and Afterlife. The album features collaborations with artists such as Cassian, Chris Avantgarde, CamelPhat, Delhia de France, Grimes, Rebuke, Poppy Baskcomb, and Sevdaliza.

works

Innellea produced a remix of the song "In and Out of Love" by Armin van Buuren. This remix was released through A State of Trance on July 28, 2023.
