Beyond Wonderland is an electronic dance music festival organized by Insomniac Events. It has been held at various West Coast locations including Mountain View, San Bernardino, and Seattle.

overview

The festival features multiple stages of electronic dance music artists across genres such as Ambient, Drum and Bass, Dubstep, House, and Trance. It is known for its Alice in Wonderland motif aesthetic, modern art sculptures, a light maze, and poi dancers. To enhance the experience, interactive additions like aesthetic decorations and dancing characters are added each year.

history

Beyond Wonderland began as a springtime sister festival to Nocturnal Wonderland. It was first held on March 20, 2010, at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino, California. The festival expanded into Beyond Socal and Beyond Norcal/Bay Area for four years before founder Pasquale Rotella announced he would no longer continue both to pursue other Insomniac brand festivals. It also made international debuts in Mexico in 2017 and Colombia in 2019.

past events

Beyond Wonderland has been held at various venues, including the WAMU Theatre in Seattle (2011) and Shoreline Amphitheatre in Mountain View (2013-2015). In San Bernardino, it was hosted at the NOS Events Center and later the San Manuel Amphitheater. The festival introduced camping options in 2015 at the San Manuel Amphitheater, though these were removed when the event moved back to the NOS Center.

uma own venue usage

Since 2021, Beyond Wonderland has used The Gorge Amphitheatre in Grant County, Washington. During a music festival held there on June 17, 2023, a mass shooting occurred at a nearby campground where two people were killed and two others wounded; the suspect, James M. Kelly, was shot by police and arrested.
