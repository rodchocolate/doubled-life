The Belleville Three are a trio of American electronic music pioneers consisting of Juan Atkins, Derrick May, and Kevin Saunderson. They are credited with pioneering techno music in the 1980s and helping to define Detroit techno, the earliest style of the genre.

origins and early life

Juan Atkins, Derrick May, and Kevin Saunderson met in junior high school or at Belleville High School in Belleville, Michigan. They formed a trio called Deep Space Soundworks to present their favorite music at parties and clubs. During high school, Atkins and Saunderson were fans of DJ Charles 'The Electrifying Mojo' Johnson. All three artists grew up influenced by electronic music, with Juan Atkins buying his first synthesizer, a Korg MS-10, at age 15.

musical contributions and artistry

The trio's work is described as pioneering the Detroit techno scene. Juan Atkins is referred to as the 'godfather of techno' and the 'original pioneer of Detroit techno,' with his 1982 electro track 'Clear' being cited as a key early release. Kevin Saunderson is noted for his influential bassline in the 1988 track 'Just Want Another Chance' (released under the Reese pseudonym), which became highly influential in jungle and drum and bass music. Derrick May, often called the 'Miles Davis of techno,' produces what he calls 'Hi-Tek Soul,' a style he describes as 'George Clinton meeting Kraftwerk in an elevator.'
