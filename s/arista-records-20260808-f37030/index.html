Arista Records is an American record label owned by Sony Music Entertainment, a subsidiary of Sony Music Group and the Japanese conglomerate Sony Group Corporation. Founded in November 1974 by Clive Davis, the label was deactivated in 2011 and re-established in 2018. It is one of Sony Music's four flagship record labels alongside RCA Records, Columbia Records, and Epic Records.

founding and early history

Arista Records was introduced in November 1974 by Clive Davis, who had been recruited as a consultant for Columbia Pictures's music operations. With a $10 million investment from CPI, Davis reorganized various legacy labels including Bell Records, Colgems, SGC and Colpix to create the label. The name Arista was taken from New York City's public secondary school honor society of which Davis was a member at Erasmus Hall High School. In early 1975, many artists signed to Bell were let go, while others like Barry Manilow and Melissa Manchester continued with Arista. Some artists were farmed out to the Big Tree label, which was distributed by Bell/Arista.

corporate evolution

Columbia Pictures sold Arista to Ariola Records (a unit of Bertelsmann) on July 27, 1979. In 1983, Bertelsmann sold a 50% interest in the label to RCA Records, and by 1985, they merged their music operations to form RCA/Ariola International. After General Electric absorbed the RCA Corporation in 1986, GE sold its interest in RCA/Ariola to Bertelsmann, and the company was renamed Bertelsmann Music Group (BMG). Arista's U.S. releases began noting BMG in 1987. In 1987, the label formed a home video subsidiary called 6 West Home Video.

artist roster and imprints

Arista signed notable artists such as Whitney Houston, who became its biggest-selling recording act with over 220 million records sold worldwide. Other signees include Aretha Franklin, the Grateful Dead, Lou Reed, Patti Smith, the Kinks, Air Supply, and Eric Carmen. The label also operated several imprint labels including Arista Novus for contemporary jazz, Arista Freedom for avant-garde jazz, and Arista Texas (later Arista Latin) for Tejano/Regional Mexican music. In 1989, it entered into a joint venture with Babyface and Antonio "L.A." Reid to create LaFace Records, which was later acquired by Arista and BMG in 1999.

milli vanilli controversy

In 1989, Arista signed the German R&B duo Milli Vanilli. Their debut album, Girl You Know It's True, was a commercial success, winning a Grammy Award for Best New Artist in 1990. However, it was later revealed that the duo had not performed a single note on their album. This revelation caused an industry-wide scrutiny of lip-synching and led to the label deleting the album and its masters from the catalogue. Clive Davis promptly released the duo from their contract after the Grammy Award was revoked.

arista nashville

Founded in 1989 by Clive Davis and songwriter Tim DuBois, Arista Nashville specialized in country music artists such as Alan Jackson, Brad Paisley, and Carrie Underwood. The label operated sister labels including Career Records, Arista Austin, and Arista Texas/Latin. From 2005 to 2017, it promoted and distributed recordings by Carrie Underwood. In March 2023, Sony Music Nashville announced that the label would close, with its roster transferring to other labels under Sony's ownership.
