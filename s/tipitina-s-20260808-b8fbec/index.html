Tipitina's is a music venue located at the corner of Napoleon Avenue and Tchoupitoulas Street in Uptown New Orleans, Louisiana. It is considered one of the best-known clubs in New Orleans.

history

Local music enthusiasts opened Tipitina's on January 14, 1977. The venue was named after a song by Professor Longhair, who performed there until his death in 1980. Before it became Tipitina's, the building—constructed in 1912—served as a gambling house, gymnasium, and brothel. In its early years, the venue featured a juice bar, restaurant, and bar; the banana in the logo is the only remaining remnant of the juice bar. During the early 1980s, radio station WWOZ's studios were located in upstairs apartments, allowing them to broadcast live shows by lowering a microphone through a hole in the floor. Tipitina's closed temporarily during the 1984 World's Fair, after which it was remodeled to remove the upstairs apartments and increase the ceiling height of the music venue.

community and operations

In 1998, a second location opened on North Peters Street in the French Quarter, though it is currently closed. The Tipitina's Foundation was established as a non-profit to support local music and musicians, specifically providing uniforms and instruments for New Orleans public high school marching bands and supporting musicians affected by Hurricane Katrina. This foundation closed shortly after the venue was sold in late 2018. In December 2018, Tipitina's was purchased from Mary and Roland von Kurnatowski by members of the jam band Galactic, who then formed the Tip-It Foundation to promote the music, culture, and heritage of the Gulf South via the brand and venue.

live recordings

Numerous live albums have been recorded at the venue, including 'Work Done on Premises' (1980) by The Radiators, 'Nevillization: Live at Tipitina's' (1982) by The Neville Brothers, and 'Live – Tipitina's, New Orleans, LA, 16 Jan '89' (1989) by Jane's Addiction. Other recordings include works by Galactic, Anders Osborne, Tuts Washington, Dr. John, Bonerama, The Blind Boys of Alabama, and Mem Shannon. Tipitina's is also featured in the documentary and live album 'Wilco Live: Ashes of American Flags'.

annual events

Tipitina's hosts a concert series called 'Fess Jazztival', which is a play on the nickname of Professor Longhair, 'Fess'. This event takes place during the annual New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival period.
