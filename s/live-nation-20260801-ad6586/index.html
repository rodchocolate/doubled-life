Live Nation Entertainment, Inc. is an American multinational entertainment company founded in 2010 following the merger of Live Nation and Ticketmaster. The company promotes and manages ticket sales for live entertainment internationally, operates entertainment venues and festivals, and manages music artists' careers.

company history

Live Nation was originally established in 1996 as SFX Entertainment by Robert F. X. Sillerman to consolidate concert promoters into a national entity. It was sold to Clear Channel Communications in 2000 for $4.4 billion and operated as Clear Channel Entertainment until it was spun off as Live Nation in 2005. In February 2009, Live Nation announced a merger with the ticketing firm Ticketmaster in a $2.5 billion all-stock deal, which became Live Nation Entertainment in 2010 after receiving regulatory approval from Norway, Turkey, and the United Kingdom. The U.S. Department of Justice approved the merger pending certain conditions, including that Ticketmaster sell its self-ticketing company Paciolan and license its software to Anschutz Entertainment Group (AEG). Live Nation Entertainment was also placed under a 10-year court order prohibiting retaliation against venues using competing ticket contracts.

operations and growth

Live Nation Entertainment describes itself as the largest live entertainment company, producer of live music concerts, and leading ticketing sales and marketing company in the world. As of early 2023, it has controlling interests in 338 venues globally. The company has expanded through various acquisitions and joint ventures. In the United States, it has acquired stakes in festivals like Bonnaroo Music and Arts Festival, Voodoo Music + Arts Experience, and BottleRock Napa Valley. It also acquired a majority stake in Groot Hospitality's nightclubs and restaurants in Miami. In Europe, it has acquired the MEO Arena in Lisbon and is set to acquire Royal Arena in Copenhagen. Additionally, the company launched Live Nation Productions, a film and television division that produced the movie A Star Is Born (2018).

legal challenges and controversies

Live Nation has faced widespread criticism over its role in the consolidation of the live events industry and allegations of anti-competitive practices. In May 2024, the U.S. Department of Justice and a coalition of states sued Live Nation and Ticketmaster for antitrust violations, alleging they illegally monopolized the live event market. While the DOJ reached a settlement in March 2026 requiring a Live Nation to create a $280m settlement fund and divest from 13 exclusive booking agreements, a federal jury in April 2026 found the company liable on the states' antitrust claims. Other controversies include a massive 1.3 TB data leak at subsidiary Ticketmaster attributed to hacker group ShinyHunters, and reports of at least 750 injuries and around 200 deaths at its events since 2006.
