CL Smooth is an American rapper born Corey Brent Penn Sr. on October 8, 1968. He is best known as one half of the hip hop duo Pete Rock & CL Smooth.

early life and education

Corey Brent Penn Sr. was born in New Rochelle, New York, to a mother who was still in high school and an absent father. He was primarily cared for by his grandmother, a pediatric nurse, and his grandfather, a grandfather who worked as a dry-cleaning presser. While attending Mount Vernon High School, he met future producer, rapper, and DJ Pete Rock.

musical career

As part of the duo Pete Rock & CL Smooth, he delivered a conscious style of rap that avoided profanity and contrasted with the popular gangsta rap of the era. The duo released an EP, All Souled Out in 1991, and their debut album, Mecca and the Soul Brother in 1992, which featured the hit "They Reminisce Over You (T.R.O.Y.)". After the duo split in 1995, CL Smooth remained musically inactive for a period of time, only appearing on one track with DJ Krush in 1996. Between 1998 and 2004, he and Pete Rock recorded seven tracks together despite their differences.

solo work and collaborations

CL Smooth released his debut solo album, American Me, in 2006, followed by The Outsider in 2007. He began making guest appearances more frequently starting around 2004, contributing vocals to artists such as AZ, Raekwon, Nujabes, and DJ Jazzy Jeff. His most recent guest appearance as of 2022 was on the song "The Masters" by Ghettosocks and DK. In February 2019, he released a single entitled "Just In Town".

activism

In October 2010, CL Smooth and Pete Rock headlined the Clean Energy Tour, which consisted of six shows in California focusing on voter mobilization for the November 2010 midterm elections. He has become an activist against climate change and was a vocal opponent of Proposition 23.
