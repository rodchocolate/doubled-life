summary

The video discusses a shift in product management where AI agents are used to automate the story writing and requirements synthesis up to the process of review and judgment. The speaker introduces a 'clarity harness' which is being open-sourced, an orchestrator for managing multiple specialized AI agents (like interview, requirements synthesizer, and story writer agents) to create product stories from transcripts. This matters because it shifts the PM's role from manual labor to quality control and iterative improvement of agent runs.
