Jubilee is an American electronic musician. She primarily plays Miami bass-inspired club music and incorporates house, grime, 2-step, and dubstep into her DJ sets.

early career

Jubilee began her career as a DJ in the underground rave scene of Miami, Florida, before moving to New York City in 2003. In 2008, she worked with Jason Forrest on the Nightshifters label and organized a series of parties called Flashing Lights with DJ Ayres and Nick Catchdubs. She collaborated with producers Star Eyes and Udachi, releasing tracks such as "//" and "Merkwood Estates" on Unknown to the Unknown. After closing Nightshifters, she began collaborating with Mixpak, which released her EPs Pull Ova, Jealous, and Pop It!, the latter of which was reviewed by Spin in 2012.

current career

In 2016, Jubilee joined Mixpak artists Dre Skull, Spice, and Popcaan for Mixpak's debut appearance at Red Bull's Culture Clash, which they won. That same year, she released her debut album, After Hours, on Mixpak, receiving favorable reviews from Fact Magazine, Fader, and Pitchfork. Pitchfork also named the album one of the top electronic music albums of 2016. She was also recognized as one of Brooklyn Magazine's "Brooklyn 100". In 2019, she released her sophomore record, Call for Location, featuring collaborations with Maluca and P Money. Jubilee currently heads the record label Magic City.
