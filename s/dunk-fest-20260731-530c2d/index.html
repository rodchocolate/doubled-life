Dunk Fest is a Belgian post-rock festival. It is mentioned in the context of of the American post-metal band Russian Circles' live album, Live at Dunk! Fest, and the first overseas tour of the Australian instrumental post-rock band We Lost the Sea.

russian circles performance

The American post-metal band Russian Circles recorded their second live album, Live at Dunk! Fest, during a headlining performance in Zottegem, Belgium, on May 7, 2016. The recording was professional and occurred unbeknownst to the band; bassist Brian Cook noted that they were not excited about the plan of documenting a show on record due to the pressure it would be too much. He stated that good shows are a matter of creating a good vibe rather than playing everything perfectly.

we lost the sea performance

The Australian instrumental post-rock band We Lost the Sea embarked on their first overseas tour to Europe in May 2017. Their debut show of this tour took place at the Belgian post-rock festival Dunk!.
