ilich ramírez sánchez

Ilich Ramírez Sánchez, known as 'Carlos the Jackal', is a Venezuelan Marxist-Leninist who conducted assassinations and terrorist bombings between 1973 and 1985. He was supported by the Stasi and KGB and led the 1975 raid on the OPEC headquarters in Vienna, which resulted in three deaths. After his wife Magdalena Kopp was imprisoned, he carried out attacks that killed 11 people to demand her release. He was eventually captured in Sudan and is currently serving three life sentences in France.

carlos alberto sánchez moreno

Carlos Alberto Sánchez Moreno (born 1986) is a former Colombian professional footballer who played as a defensive midfielder, nicknamed 'La Roca'. His club career spanned several leagues, including Ligue 1 with Valenciennes, La Liga with Elche and Espanyol, the Premier League with Aston Villa and West Ham United, and Serie A with Fiorentina. He has earned over 80 caps for Colombia, reaching the quarter-finals of the 2014 FIFA World Cup and the 2015 Copa América.
