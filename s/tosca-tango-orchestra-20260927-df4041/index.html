The Tosca Tango Orchestra is an ensemble of Austin, Texas-based musicians founded by Glover Gill in 1997. The group performs original compositions in the nuevo tango style and has released six albums.

history and performances

The orchestra was founded as a joint effort between Glover Gill, Jeanine Attaway, Luis Guerra, and The Tosca String Quartet. In 2000, the group traveled to Buenos Aires and were featured artists at the 5th World Tango Summit. In 2003, they performed and recorded the premiere of choreographer Stephen Mills’s Touch. In September 2012, the ensemble reunited for live performances in Austin, Texas, including a VIP concert at the Church House Studio, a public concert at the Scottish Rite Theater, and a performance on KUT radio's Eklektikos show.

waking life soundtrack

The Tosca Tango Orchestra performed and wrote the soundtrack to Richard Linklater's film Waking Life, which helped the group gain an international following. The soundtrack bills itself as "the 21st Century Tango" and features the nuevo tango style. All but two tracks on the album were composed by Glover Gill and performed by the the orchestra; the remaining two pieces were written by Julián Plaza and Frédéric Chopin.

discography

As of September 2012, the Tosca Tango Orchestra has released six albums. Their studio albums include La Furia Del Tango (1998), Tia Pamelita (1998), Amado (1999), La Ciudad del Tango (2000), Waking Life OST (2001), Touch (2003), and UNO Tango Suite (2008).
