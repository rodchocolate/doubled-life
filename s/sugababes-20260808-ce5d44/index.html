Sugababes are a British girl group formed in 1998, consisting of Mutya Buena, Keisha Buchanan, and Siobhán Donaghy. They have achieved six number-one singles in the UK and five UK top-ten albums.

formation and early years

Sugababes were formed in 1998 by First Access Entertainment, Sarah Stennett and Ron Tom, the manager of All Saints. Siobhán Donaghy and Mutya Buena, both aged 13, were signed as solo artists but decided to work together after a showcase. They were joined by Keisha Buchanan, whom Buena invited to watch them in the studio. The group's name was changed from Sugababies to Sugababes to provide a more mature image when they were signed by London Records. Their debut album, One Touch (2000), produced the UK top-ten single "Overload" and reached number 26 on the UK Albums Chart. However, London Records dropped the group in 2001 after sales did not meet expectations.

lineup changes and commercial success

Siobhán Donaghy left the group in August 2001 during a Japanese promotional tour, citing a desire to pursue fashion and later diagnosing clinical depression. She was replaced by Heidi Range of former Atomic Kitten member. Following this change, the group signed with Island Records and released three multi-platinum albums: Angels with Dirty Faces (2002), Three (2003), and Taller in More Ways (2005). Their first number one album on the UK Albums Chart was Taller in More Ways. Their highest-selling album, Angels with Dirty Faces, reached number two and was certified triple Platinum. The group achieved several UK number one singles including "Freak like Me", "Round Round", and "Hole in the Head".

later years and reunions

The group's fifth studio album, Change (2007), earned them their second number one album in the UK, and its lead single "About You Now" became their bestselling song in the country. In December 2005, Mutya Buena left and was replaced by Amelle Berrabah. After Keisha Buchanan, the final original member, was replaced by Jade Ewen in September 2009, the group released their seventh studio album, Sweet 7 (2010) before taking an indefinite hiatus in 2011. The original lineup re-formed as Mutya Keisha Siobhán in 2011, later regaining the name Sugababes in 2019. In 2021, they rereleased One Touch for its 20th anniversary and released The Lost Tapes in 2022. In 2025, they independently released singles "Jungle", "Weeds" and "Shook" and embarked on their first arena tour, the Sugababes '25 Tour.

critical reception and achievements

Sugababes have been regarded as one of the best girl groups of all time by Teen Vogue. Three of their studio albums are among the top ten best selling girl-group albums of all time in the UK. Three of their number one singles in the UK UK number one singles include "Freak like Me", "Round Round", "Hole in the Head", "Push the Button", "Walk This Way", and "About You Now". Their song "Freak Like Me" was ranked as the 45th best girl-group song of all time by Billboard.

push the button

The lead single from Taller in More Ways, "Push the Button", was composed by Dallas Austin and the Sugababes. It is an electropop and R&B song inspired by Keisha Buchanan's infatuation with another artist. The song peaked at number one in the UK, Austria, Ireland, and New Zealand. It was nominated for Best British Single at the 2006 Brit Awards.
