Gizzfest is a music festival that was launched by the rock band King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard in 2015.

overview

Gizzfest is a former festival organized by the band King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard. It was launched in Melbourne in 2015 and was held annually, touring across Australia. The event featured both international and local acts.

history

The festival's development was supported by Eric Moore, one of the three founding members of King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard and the founder of Flightless Records. Eric Moore served as a principal supporter of the event.
