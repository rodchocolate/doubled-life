Bruno Mars, born Peter Gene Hernandez, is an American singer-songwriter, record producer and dancer. He is recognized as a pop icon known for his three-octave tenor vocal range, retro showmanship, and musical versatility. He is accompanied by his band, the Hooligans.

early life and musical beginnings

Born on October 8, 1985, in Honolulu, Hawaii, to Peter Hernandez and Bernadette San Pedro Bayot, Mars was raised in the Waikíkí neighborhood. His father is of Puerto Rican and Ashkenazi Jewish descent, while his mother is of Filipino and Spanish ancestry. From a young age, Mars was immersed in a musical family; he began performing with his family's band, the Love Notes, at age four, becoming known in Hawaii for his impersonation of Elvis Presley. In high school, he sang in a group called the School Boys and later moved to Los Angeles in 2003 after graduating from high school to pursue a professional musical career.

music career and discography

Mars established himself as a songwriter and co-founder of the production team the Smeezingtons. He rose to fame as a recording artist through his feature on B.o.B's 2009 single "Nothin' on You". His first three studio albums—Doo-Wops & Hooligans (2010), Unorthodox Jukebox (2012), and 24K Magic (2016)—achieved critical and commercial success, with 24K Magic winning the Grammy Award for Album of the Year. In 2021, he formed the musical superduo Silk Sonic with Anderson .Paak. Following two number-one duets in 2024 with Lady Gaga and Rosé, he released his fifth solo album, The Romantic (2026), which featured the hit singles "I Just Might" and "Risk It All".

commercial success and accolades

Mars has sold over 150 million records worldwide, making him one of the best-selling music artists of all time. He is the first artist to score six diamond-certified songs in the US and the only artist to surpass 150 million monthly listeners on Spotify. His accolades include 16 Grammy Awards, 17 American Music Awards, 9 Billboard Music Awards, 5 Brit Awards, 7 MTV Video Music Awards, and 14 Soul Train Awards. Additionally, Time named him among the 100 most influential people in the world in 2011.

live performances and touring

Mars's 24K Magic World Tour (2017–2018) is among the highest-grossing tours in history. More recently, his Bruno Mars Live tour (2022․) spanned 59 dates across Australia, Asia, and the Americas. The Brazilian leg of this tour became the highest-grossing tour in Brazilian history, grossing $85 million from 14 shows. He also maintained a residency at Park MGM in Las Vegas, where 115 reported shows sold over 2.2 million tickets.
