influence on french house

The development of French house music, a style devised by French musicians in the 1990s, was influenced by 1970s French pop music championed by artists such as Jean-Michel Jarre and Serge Gainsbourg. This genre is characterized by filter and phaser effects on samples from European disco tracks from the late 1970s and early 1980s.
