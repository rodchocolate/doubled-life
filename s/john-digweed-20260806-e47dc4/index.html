John Digweed is a British DJ and record producer. He has achieved success as a solo act and through collaborations with Sasha and Nick Muir. He was voted the number one DJ in DJ Mag in 2001.

early career and renaissance

Digweed began DJing at age 15, starting in his hometown of Hastings where he organized successful club nights and raves on Hastings Pier. His breakthrough occurred in 1993 when he sent a mixtape demo to Geoff Oakes, the founder of the Renaissance nightclub in Mansfield. This led to a partnership with fellow DJ Alexander Coe (Sasha), with whom he promoted progressive house music. Together they produced the first commercial compilation for a nightclub, 'Renaissance: The Mix Collection' in 1994, which was strategically planned for marketing from promotion to artwork. This was followed by the Northern Exposure compilations and Global Underground releases.

bedrock records and production

Digweed founded the record label Bedrock Records to promote his current musical style. Producing under the alias Bedrock with friend Nick Muir, he achieved a significant break when their track 'For What You Dream Of' was used in the film Trainspotting. The duo also produced the soundtrack for the 2003 MTV adult cartoon superhero drama series Spider-Man: The New Animated Series. In his DJ sets, Digweed is known for adopting tracks with new and different styles.

residencies and tours

Digweed established a monthly residency at the now defunct New York City club Twilo starting in 1997, which became one of the most popular club nights in the New York electronic music scene. He and Sasha played sets lasting between eight and twelve hours. Between 2000 and 2005, he promoted his 'Bedrock' sound through monthly club nights at Heaven in London and The Beach in Brighton. He also undertook a six-week countrywide tour of the United States called Delta Heavy in 2002 with Sasha and Jimmy Van M, which reached an attendance of 85,000 people.

radio and media appearances

Digweed has hosted a weekly two-hour radio show on Kiss 100 in the UK from September 2000 to January 2011. The show, later named Transitions, continued to be broadcast online after its time on Kiss 100 and reached its 800th episode on December 27, 2019. Additionally, Digweed has a cameo of himself playing music in the 2000 movie Groove and curated the soundtrack for the film Stark Raving Mad in 2002.
