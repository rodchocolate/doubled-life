Ben Jaffe is an American jazz musician and the creative director of Preservation Hall. He plays double bass and tuba with the Preservation Hall Jazz Band.

early life and education

Born on January 26, 1971, Jaffe grew up in New Orleans' French Quarter, two blocks from Preservation Hall. He is the son of former managers Allan and Sandra Jaffe. His father, Allan, also played tuba with the band. Ben Jaffe began playing in grammar school at McDonogh 15 School for the Creative Arts and later studied upright bass under Walter Payton during high school at NOCCA. In 1992, he received a degree in bass performance from Oberlin College.

career

After graduating from college, Jaffe returned to New Orleans and resumed his father's position as manager of the Preservation Hall venue. He also joined the Preservation Hall Jazz Band on tour playing bass. As a producer, he has produced several albums for the band, including "That's It," which he co-produced with Jim James. He currently serves as Chairman of the Board of the Preservation Hall Foundation, a non-profit organization dedicated to protecting and preserving New Orleans jazz music and culture.

work and media appearances

In 2019, Jaffe narrated the documentary A Tuba to Cuba, which follows the Preservation Hall Jazz Band's 2015 visit to Cuba to retrace connections between indigenous Cuban music and New Orleans jazz. He also appeared in episode 6 of "Foo Fighters: Sonic Highways," where he was interviewed by Dave Grohl regarding his parents' role in preserving New Orleans jazz and the history of Preservation Hall.
