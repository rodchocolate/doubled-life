mc lars

Andrew Robert Nielsen, known as MC Lars, is an American producer and rapper who founded Horris Records. He is a pioneer of "lit-hop," a genre that blends hip-hop with themes from classical and American literature, and is a key figure in the early nerdcore movement. His production style, which he describes as "post-punk laptop rap," incorporates laptop-driven beats and samples from artists such as Iggy Pop, Fugazi, Brand New, Piebald, and Supergrass.

cities aviv

Wilbert Gavin Mays, performing as Cities Aviv, is an American record producer and rapper from Memphis, Tennessee. He is the founder of the independent label Total Works (also the known as D.O.T.). His production style is described as eclectic, merging various stylistic decisions and drawing influence from noise music, James Brown, Jay Dee, 8Ball & MJG, Black Moon, Boris, Die Tödliche Doris, and Throbbing Gristle.
