The Immanuel Wilkins Quartet is a jazz ensemble led by American saxophonist Immanuel Wilkins. The group's recordings are released through Blue Note Records.

discography

The quartet has released three volumes of live recordings from the Village Vanguard, all published in 2026 by Blue Note Records: Immanuel Wilkins Quartet: Live at the Village Vanguard Vol. 1, Vol. 2, and Vol. 3.

overview

The ensemble is led by by American jazz saxophonist Immanuel Wilkins, who was born on August 7, 1997, and grew up in the Upper Darby neighborhood of Delaware County, Pennsylvania. Wilkins has studied at the Juilliard School under Bruce Williams, Steve Wilson, and Joe Temperley.
