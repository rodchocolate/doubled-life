Tim Reynolds is an American multi-instrumentalist and guitarist known as a solo artist and lead guitarist for the Dave Matthews Band. He is also the founding member of the band TR3.

early life and musical development

Born in Wiesbaden, Germany, Reynolds grew up moving frequently between locations including Indiana, Alaska, Kansas, and Missouri. Raised by devout Christians, he was influenced by the same music they surrounding him with, as well as Beatles albums owned by his sister. In adolescence, he learned guitar and bass guitar to perform in his family's church choir three times a week, later adding rock and roll, funk, and soul riffs by age twelve. After high school, he moved to Richmond, Virginia, where he joined the fusion band Secrets. Later moving to Charlottesville, Virginia, he expanded his repertoire to include sixties psychedelic rock influences such as Led Zeppelin, Carlos Santana, and Jimi Hendrix. By the 1990s, he had formed TR3 and began learning 'exotic' instruments not commonly seen in Western rock music, including the sarod, sitar, keytar, mandello, and tubular bells.

collaborations with dave matthews

Reynolds met Dave Matthews at Miller's bar in Charlottesville, Virginia, where Matthews was a bartender. Reynolds encouraged Matthews to form his own band, introducing him to local musicians who would eventually make up the Dave Matthews Band. While he initially declined to join as an official member, he recorded and toured as a sideman for the band from its inception until late 1998. He later joined as a permanent member in June 2008. In addition to his work with the Dave Matthews Band, Reynolds and Matthews have performed as an acoustic duo since 1993, touring together for the first time in 1996. They released a live CD/DVD set, Live at Radio City, in August 2007.

other musical projects

Reynolds has performed as part of the group Dave Matthews & Friends, which toured in winter 2003/2004 and again in 2004, 2005, and 2006. This ensemble included musicians such as Ray Paczkowski, Tony Hall, Brady Blade, and Trey Anastasio. During these sets, Reynolds performed on electric guitar, following a small acoustic set with Dave Matthews. He and Matthews maintain an open taping policy that extends to his own band, TR3.
