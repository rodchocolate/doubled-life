Jong PSV is a Dutch football team based in Eindhoven. It serves as the reserve team for PSV Eindhoven and has competed in the Eerste Divisie since the 2013–14 season.

history

Until 2013, Jong PSV played in the Beloften Eredivisie, a league for reserve teams. In 2013, the KNVB expanded the Eerste Divisie to 20 teams by adding two amateur and two reserve teams. While Jong Ajax and Jong FC Twente were initially chosen, Jong PSV was offered the final slot after amateur team VV Katwijk rejected the offer. Because it is part of the same league system as PSV, the team must follow specific rules: it is ineligible for promotion, relegation, play-offs, or the KNVB Cup, and players with 150 or more appearances for PSV are ineligible to play for Jong PSV. Additionally, the maximum player age is 23, with exceptions for three field players and one goalkeeper.

stadiums and facilities

Jong PSV previously used the Philips Stadion and Jan Louwers Stadion as home match locations. De Herdgang is now the official place where the team plays its games, after the Dutch Football Association initially rejected the idea of using the facility.

honours

The team has won the Beloften Eredivisie in 1996–97, 1999 , and 2009‐11. They have also achieved success in the KNVB Beker voor beloften (2000 , 2004 , 2007 ), the Supercup Beloften (2010, 2011), and won the Premier League International Cup in 2022‣.
