G-funk, short for gangsta funk (or funk rap), is a West Coast sub-genre of gangsta rap that emerged in the early 1990s. It is heavily influenced by the synthesizer-heavy 1970s funk sound of Parliament-Funkadelic (P-Funk).

characteristics

G-funk typically operates between 90 and 100 BPM and uses funk with an artificially altered tempo. The genre is characterized by deep bass, slow hypnotic grooves, heavy snare drum use, background female vocals, and multi-layered melodic synthesizers, including a high-pitched portamento saw wave synthesizer lead. Lyrically, the style focuses on themes of drug use (particularly marijuana), sex, love for friends, and love for a city. Rapping is often delivered in a slurred 'lazy' or 'smooth' way to maintain rhythmic cadence and clarify words. Unlike earlier rap acts, G-funk often utilizes fewer, unaltered samples per song; some artists, like Dr. Dre, use live musicians to replay original music from sampled records to create unique sounds rather than direct copies.

origins and development

Dr. Dre is often seen as the originator of G-funk, though Cold 187um of Above the Law has disputed this claim. Early examples include N.W.A's 'Dope Man' (1987) and 'Gangsta Gangsta' (1988), which used a squealing synth solo from Ohio Players' 'Funky Worm'. Dr. Dre also produced early hits like the D.O.C.'s 'It's Funky Enough' in 1989. In 1991, N.W.A released the album Niggaz4Life and Ice Cube released the diss track 'No Vaseline', both in the G-funk style. The genre reached a breakout year in 1992 with the release of Dr. Dre's Triple Platinum certified album The Chronic.

mainstream peak

Between 1992 and 1997, G-funk experienced its mainstream peak. Following The Chronic, Snoop Dogg released his Quadruple Platinum debut album Doggystyle (1993), featuring hits like 'Gin and Juice'. Ice Cube also had success with gold-certified songs such as 'It Was a Good Day' and 'Check Yo Self'. In 1994, Warren G's album Regulate... G Funk Era debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard 200 and was certified triple platinum, featuring the top ten hit 'Regulate'. Other successful artists including Coolio, MC Hammer, and the group Thug Life (featuring 2Pac) released G-funk influenced works. In 1995, 2Pac's album Me Against the World reached No. 1 on the Billboard 200 and he released the hit 'California Love'. By 1996, 2Pac released the lush G-funk album All Eyez on Me, which also hit No. 1.
