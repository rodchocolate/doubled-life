Mild High Club is an American psychedelic pop group led by musician Alexander Brettin. The group has released three studio albums: Timeline (2015), Skiptracing (2016), and Going, Going, Gone (2021).

history

Alexander Brettin grew up in the Midwest and played flute in his school band. He studied jazz studies at Columbia College in Chicago and founded Mild High Club in 2013. Brettin spent nearly three years working on the group's debut album, Timeline, which was released on September 18, 2015, through the Stones Throw Records imprint Circle Star. The album received generally favorable reviews, with critics from AllMusic and Mojo describing it as an enjoyable and naturally flowing 'thick trance of lysergic pop'.

Following this, Mild High Club released their sophomore effort, Skiptracing, in 2016. According to Brettin, the album's concept is a story arc about a Los Angeles detective searching for the spirit of American music. The artwork for the album was painted by Zack Goulet, who later had a falling out with Brettin over disagreements regarding Donald Trump. Skiptracing received positive reviews from critics, including those from Record Collector and Exclaim!, while Pitchfork noted that it was as an improvement over Timeline.

After nearly five years of inactivity, the group announced the album Going, Going, Gone on July 27, 2021, accompanied by the song 'Me Myself and Dollar Hell'. The album was released on September 17, 2021. Additionally, 'Homage', a track from Skiptracing, was certified Gold by the RIAA in 2022.
