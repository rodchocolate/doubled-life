Timbaland is an American record producer, rapper, singer, and songwriter acclaimed for his distinctive production work and "stuttering" rhythmic style. He is credited with incorporating avant-garde sounds into No. 1 hits and influencing current pop trends through his urban-edged R&B production.

signature techniques & sound

Timbaland's trademark sound is rooted in hip-hop, characterized by fast-paced nature and clear drum breaks. He is widely recognized for his "stuttering" rhythmic style and the integration of avant-garde sounds into mainstream pop hits. By applying hip-hop sounds to R&B, he was instrumental in blurring the distinction between hip-hop and R&B production.

landmark records

Timbaland's mainstream breakthrough occurred in 1996 with the production of Ginuwine's debut album Ginuwine... the Bachelor (featuring the hit "Pony") and Aaliyah's second album One in a Million (featuring "If Your Girl Only Knew"). In 1997, he fully produced Missy Elliott's debut album Supa Dupa Fly, continuing his electronic production style. He also produced Justin Timberlake's 2002 single "Cry Me a River" and the majority of Timberlake's subsequent releases, including FutureSex/LoveSounds (2006) and The 20/20 Experience (2013).

key collaborators & lineage

Timbaland began his career collaborating with rapper Magoo, forming the duo Timbaland & Magoo. He was part of the production ensemble S.B.I. (Surrounded By Idiots), which included his cousin Pharrell Williams. He further developed his craft under DeVante Swing of the R&B act Jodeci, joining the Swing Mob label, where he was renamed Timbaland. He has since collaborated with extensive high-profile artists including Missy Elliott, Aaliyah, Justin Timberlake, Jay-Z, Beyoncé, Madonna, Rihanna, and Kanye West.

canonical status

Entertainment Weekly in 2007 stated that nearly every current pop trend can be traced back to him. He has won four Grammy Awards from 22 nominations. He also founded the record labels Beat Club and Mosley Music Group, and co-created the webcast series Verzuz.
