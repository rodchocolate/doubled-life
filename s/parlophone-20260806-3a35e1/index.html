Parlophone Records Limited is a German-British record label founded in 1896 by the Carl Lindström Company as Parlophon. It has evolved through various ownership ownship changes and became one of the world's most famous labels during the 1960s. Currently, it is a flagship label of Warner Music Group.

origins and early history

Parlophone was founded as 'Parlophon' in 1896 by the Carl Lindström Company, where the name was initially used for gramophones before the label began producing records. The label's trademark is a stylised blackletter L that stands for Lindström. On August 8, 1923, the British branch of the company was established under A&R manager Oscar Preuss. In its early years in Britain, Parlophone developed a reputation as a leading jazz record label.

emi era and commercial success

The Columbia Graphophone Company acquired a controlling interest in the Carl Lindström Company in 1927, eventually becoming part of Electric & Musical Industries (EMI) after a merger with the Gramophone Company in 1931. George Martin joined as an assistant to Oscar Preuss in 1950 and took over as manager in 1955. Under Martin's leadership, the label expanded into novelty and comedy records, including recordings by Peter Sellers and Flanders and Swann. In 1962, Martin signed the Beatles, who had been previously rejected by Decca Records. Along with other acts like Cilla Black, Billy J. Kramer, the Fourmost, and the Hollies, the Beatles propelled Parlophone to global fame. The label claimed the best-selling UK single 'She Loves You' and the best-selling UK album Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band. In 1964, the label placed seven singles at number one and held the top spot on the UK Albums Chart for 40 weeks.

later years and wmg acquisition

Parlophone became dormant in 1973 (except for Beatles reissues) when EMI's heritage labels were phased out, but was revived in 1980. Following the revival, it signed artists such as Radiohead, Coldplay, Blur, and Duran Duran. On September 28, 2012, regulators approved Universal Music Group's acquisition of EMI for £1.2 billion, on the condition that the EMI Records group be divested. This group, which included Parlophone (excluding the Beatles' catalogue), was operated as the Parlophone Label Group (PLG). On February 7, 2013, Warner Music Group acquired PLG for US$765 million, and in May 2013, the label was renamed Parlophone Records Limited. It is now the oldest of WMG's flagship labels.
