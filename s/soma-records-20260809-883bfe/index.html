Soma Records may refer to either an American record company based in Minneapolis, Minnesota, or Soma Quality Recordings, a Scottish independent record label specializing in techno and house music.

Soma Records may refer to either an American record company based in Minneapolis, Minnesota, or Soma Quality Recordings, a Scottish independent record label specializing in techno and house music.

soma records (u.s. label)

Founded in 1954 by Amos Heilicher, the name 'Soma' is an ananym of his own name. The label initially recorded polka, old time music, jazz, and country and western. During the 1960s, Soma recorded many local and regional rock groups at Kay Bank Studios, which was partially owned by Heilicher. It also distributed other smaller labels including Studio City, Golden Ring, Bangar, and Garrett. Soma's first hit single was Bobby Vee's 'Suzie Baby' in 1959, and other hits included 'Liar, Liar' by The Castaways and 'Run, Run, Run' by The Gestures. It also distributed the first album of country singer Dave Dudley, containing his biggest hit 'Six Days on the Road', before Mercury Records bought the rights. Soma continued releasing recordings until 1967, when it was merged into Pickwick Records.

soma quality recordings

Based in Glasgow, Scotland, Soma Quality Recordings (or simply Soma Records) is an independent label co-founded in 1991 by Glenn Gibbons, Dave Clarke (not the English techno musician), and the electronic music duo Slam. The label's origin stems from a DIY ethos of the late 1980s, where producers Orde Meikle and Stuart McMillan (Slam) encountered sound engineers Jim Muotune and Glenn Gibbons (Rejuvenation). After failing to secure help from Scotland's rock infrastructure, Slam decided to press 1,000 copies of their techno EP 'Eterna / I.B.O.' using a small studio. They funded further pressings by selling copies to record shops.

daft punk and soma quality recordings

Soma Quality Recordings is most known for releasing the original vinyl version of Daft Punk's 'Da Funk' in 1995, which contains drum break samples from Barry White and an uncredited beat sample from Vaughan Mason & Crew. The label managery described meeting the teenage duo in Paris at a studio in Montmarte. Soma celebrated its 20th anniversary in 2011 with a compilation featuring a Daft Punk demo recording of 'Drive'. In 2017, for its 25th anniversary box set Soma25, the label enlisted artists such as Adam Beyer, Robert Hood and Jeff Mills to contribute.
