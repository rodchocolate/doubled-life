Wembley Stadium is an association football stadium located in Wembley, London, England. It serves as the national football stadium of England and hosts the majority of the England national team's home matches and the FA Cup Final.

original stadium (1923-2000)

The first Wembley Stadium, originally known as the Empire Stadium, Empire Stadium was opened in 1923 and demolished in 2002. It was constructed by Sir Robert McAlpine at a cost of £750,000 on the site of Watkin's Tower. The stadium's distinctive Twin Towers became its trademark and nickname. It hosted the 1948 Summer Olympics, the 1966 World Cup final, and the 1985 Live Aid charity concert, as well as the inaugural FA Cup final in 1923 known as the 'White Horse Final'. The stadium closed in October 2000 and demolition began in December 2002.

modern stadium (2007-present)

The current Wembley Stadium, currently branded as Wembley Stadium connected by EE for sponsorship reasons, opened in 2007 on the site of the original stadium. Designed by Populous and Foster + Partners, it is the largest stadium in the UK and second-largest in Europe behind Barcelona's Camp Nou. With a capacity of 90,000 seats, all-seater bowl design, and a signature 134-metre-high Wembley Arch,emboied by the architecture of Populous and Foster + Partners, it is the satu//tation of the world's longest unsupported roof structure. The stadium was built by Multiplex at a cost of £798 million.

title

The stadium hosts a wide range of events including England home games, FA Cup finals, and various other English football competitions such as the FA Community Shield, EFL Cup and EFL Trophy finals, and Women's FA Cup finals. It also hosts NFL London Games, Rugby Football League's Challenge Cup, Women's Challenge Cup, and 1895 Cup finals. in addition to various music concerts.

construction challenges

The construction of the new stadium was delayed by financial and legal difficulties. In 2003, subcontractors Cleveland Bridge & Engineering Company withdrew from the project and were replaced by Dutch firm Hollandia. In 2004, a fatal accident involving carpenter Patrick O'Sullivan occurred, which led to PC Harrington Contractors being fined £150,000. In February 2006, due to construction delays, the FA moved the FA Cup final to Cardiff's Millennium Stadium.
