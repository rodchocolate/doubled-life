Tiësto is a Dutch DJ and record producer known as the "Godfather of EDM." He has been voted "The Greatest DJ of All Time" by Mix magazine in a 2010/2011 poll and was named the best DJ of the last 20 years by DJ Mag readers in 2013.

early life and career beginnings

Tijs Michiel Verwest was born on January 17, 1969, in Breda, North Brabant. He discovered his passion for music at age 12 and began DJing professionally at school parties when he was fourteen. Between 1985 and 1994, he had residencies at several Dutch clubs, including a small club in Breda called the Spock, where he played new beat and acid house. In 1994, he began releasing material on Noculan Records' sub-labels Chemo and Coolman, producing hardcore and gabber tracks under aliases such as DJ Limited and Da Joker.

rise to fame and label management

In late 1994, Tiësto signed with Basic Beat Recordings, where he met Arny Bink. Together they created the Guardian Angel sub-label and the Forbidden Paradise series. In 1997, they left Basic Beat to found Black Hole Recordings. Through this label, Tiësto released the Magik series and established the sub-labels SongBird and In Trance We Trust. In 2001, he opened a new division of Black Hole called Magik Muzik. He also collaborated with Ferry Corsten as Gouryella from 1998 to 2000, and with Benno de Goeij (as Kamaya Painters) and Armin van Buuren (as Alibi and Major League).

studio albums and achievements

Tiësto has released seven studio albums, including In My Memory (2001), Just Be (2004), Elements of Life (2007), Kaleidoscope (2009), A Town Called Paradise (2014), The London Sessions (2020) and Drive (2023). He was voted World No. 1 DJ by DJ Magazine's annual Top 100 DJs poll for three consecutive years from 2002 to 2004. He won a Grammy Award for Best Remixed Recording, Non-Classical for his remix of John Legend's "All of Me." Additionally, he was the first DJ to perform live at an Olympics opening ceremony, performing at the 2004 Summer Olympics in Athens.
