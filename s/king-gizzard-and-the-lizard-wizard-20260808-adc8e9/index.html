King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard (KGLW) are an Australian rock band formed in 2010 in Melbourne, Victoria. They are known for their prolific discography, energetic live shows and exploration of multiple genres.

formation and early career

The band members grew up in the Deniliquin, Melbourne, and Geelong areas of Australia. Stu Mackenzie, Cook Craig, and Joey Walker met while studying music industry at RMIT University, while others were in bands called Revolver & Sun, Sambrose Automobile, and The Houses. After a fluid lineup that fluctuated between three and 11 members, the current lineup consists of Stu Mackenzie, Ambrose Kenny-Smith, Cook Craig, Joey Walker, Lucas Harwood, and Michael Cavanagh. The band's name was chosen because it was considered absurd and nonsense. Album art and most music videos are created by Melbourne artist Jason Galea.

musical evolution and discography

KGLW has released 27 studio albums, including early garage rock releases like 12 Bar Bruise (2012), a 'cult western audio book' album Eyes Like the Sky (2013), and folk-inspired Paper Mâché Dream Balloon. They have explored jazz fusion on Quarters! and incorporated heavy metal, synth-pop, and progressive rock. In 2017 and 2022, they released five studio albums in a single calendar year. In 2025, they released their 27th album, Phantom Island, followed by tours featuring orchestral, rock and rave shows. Their upcoming 28th album, Alien Metal, is described as having a 'techno' sound with heavy use of synths.

the silver cord

Released on 27 October 2023, The Silver Cord is the band's twenty-fifth studio album. It focuses on synthesizer-based music rather than guitar-based rock, inspired by Michael Cavanagh's purchase of a Simmons electronic drum kit. The project consists of two versions: one lasting 28 minutes and another lasting 88 minutes. This release was part of a 'Yin and Yang' concept with the album PetroDragonic Apocalypse.
