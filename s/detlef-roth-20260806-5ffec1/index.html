Detlef Roth is a German operatic bass-baritone born in 1969. He studied singing with Georg Jelden at the State University of Music and Performing Arts Stuttgart.

career and performance history

Roth's opera repertoire includes roles such as Wolfram von Eschenbach in Tannhäuser, Donner in Das Rheingold, Amfortas in Parsifal, Gunther in Götterdämmerung, and Der Heerufer des Königs in Lohengrin, all by Richard Wagner. He has also performed Papageno in Die Zauberflöte and Count Almaviva in Le Nozze di Figaro by Mozart, as well as roles in Königskinder by Engelbert Humperdinck, Die Bassariden by Hans Werner Henze, and Hans Heiling by Heinrich Marschner. As a concert singer, his repertoire includes Lieder by Franz Schubert, Carmina Burana by Carl Orff, A German Requiem by Johannes Brahms, Elias by Felix Mendelssohn, and masses, oratorios, and Passions by Johann Sebastian Bach. Roth has performed at the Bayreuth Festival, the Salzburg Easter Festival, the Salzburg Festival, the Paris Opera, the Lincoln Center in New York, the Royal Opera House in London, and la Scala in Milan.

awards and collaborations

During his studies, Roth won the International Singing Competition for Wagner Voices in Strasbourg in 1994, the International Hans Gabor Belvedere Singing Competition in Vienna in 1992, and the Bundeswettbewerb Gesang Berlin in 1990. He has collaborated with orchestras such as the Concertgebouw in Amsterdam, the Orchestre philharmonique de Strasbourg, the the Vienna Philharmonic, and the Orchestra dell'Accademia Nazionale di Santa Cecilia. Additionally, he performed Mozart's love arias with the Orchestre Philharmonique de Radio France and French soprano Sandrine Piau at the Cité de la musique in Paris.

discography

Roth's CD releases include recordings of Beethoven's Sinfonien 1-9 (2007) and Symphonie Nr. 9 in D minor op. 125 (1999), as well as the latter's label BMG Ariola. He has also recorded the St John Passion by Johann Sebastian Bach (2011), the Regina by Albert Lortzing (2013), and A German Requiem by Johannes Brahms (2001). Other releases include Doktor Faust by Ferruccio Busoni (1999), Doktor Faust by Ferruccio Busoni the Konzert für Klavier und Orchester a-Moll op. 7 by Clara Schumann (2001), and Die erste Walpurgisnacht by Felix Mendelssohn Bartholdy (2010), Königskinder by Engelbert Humperdinck (20al06), and Deutsche Volkslieder, part 1: Wenn ich ein Vöglein wär (2010).
