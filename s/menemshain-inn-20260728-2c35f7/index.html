The Menemsha Inn & Cottages is a destination located in Menemsha Harbor, Chilmark, Martha's Vineyard, Massachusetts. It is situated on 30 acres of land.

history

The property began in 1923 when sheep farmer DeWolf Thompson had the idea to let people pay to spend the night at his farmhouse to experience sunsets into Vineyard Sound. For nearly 30 years, the Nixon family has nurtured the property to ensure it remains an island home away from home.

title

The Menemsha Inn & Cottages and The Beach Plum Inn are both part of the property. Accommodations include The Farmhouse, Bungalows, Hayloft Barn, and Evelyn’s House, as well as a restaurant. The property features a private pathway leading across dunes to Menemsha Beach and provides passes to Lucy Vincent.
