The Gorge Amphitheatre is an outdoor concert venue located in Grant County, Washington, United States. It is situated near the Columbia River and is managed by Live Nation.

history and development

Originally known as Champs de Brionne Music Theatre, the venue opened in 1986 with a capacity of 3,000 people. It was owned and operated by Dr. Vincent Bryan and Carol Bryan along with the adjoining Champs de Brionne winery. In 1993, MCA purchase the venue, which had expanded to 19,000 seats; it was then renamed The Gorge and expanded further through landscaping and rock blasting. The first show at the renamed venue was Tina Turner with Lindsey Buckingham as her opening act. Live Nation acquired the amphitheatre in 2006.

location and facilities

The venue is located north of Interstate 90, nine miles west of George and 150 miles east of Seattle, approximately 130 miles west of Spokane. It offers views of the Columbia Gorge canyon, the Columbia River, and eastern Kittitas and western Grant counties. The land was originally planned for growing grapes for wine. The on-site campground provides potable water, flush toilets, hot showers, a convenience store, and 24-hour security. Camping tickets are required; sites are assigned first-come, first-served. Alternatively, the Wildhorse Campground is located less than two miles away and offers a shuttle service to the venue.

performances and festivals

The Gorge has hosted numerous music festivals including Lollapalooza, Ozzfest, Lilith Fair, Vans Warped Tour, and Watershed Music Festival. Dave Matthews Band has played 79 shows at the venue as of 2025, with a few of their performances being released as albums or DVDs. Phish has performed there 22 times since 1997. Other notable headliners have included Joni Mitchell, who headlined an event on June 10, 2023, her first ticketed event in over 20 years. Electronic music festivals such as Bass Canyon and Beyond Wonderland have also used the venue.

safety incident

On June 17, 2023, during the Beyond Wonderland music festival, a mass shooting occurred at a nearby campground. The shooter, 26-year-old U.S. Army personnel James M. Kelly, killed two women from the Seattle area and wounded two others. A security guard was also injured by a deflected bullet. Police shot and arrested the suspect.
