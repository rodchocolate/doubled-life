Kesha Rose Sebert, known professionally as Kesha, is an American singer, rapper, and songwriter. She has achieved multiple number-one albums on the US Billboard 200, including Animal (2010) and Rainbow (2017).

early life and education

Kesha was born on March 1, 1987, in Los Angeles to Rosemary Patricia "Pebe" Sebert, a singer-songwriter. Growing up in Nashville, she attended Franklin High School and Brentwood High School, where she played the trumpet and saxophone in the marching band. Kesha dropped out of school at age 17 after being convinced by Max Martin to return to Los Angeles to pursue music. While she previously stated she earned her GED, she contradicted this in a 2024 interview, stating she never did.

music career

Kesha's career began with signing to Prescription Songs in 2005 and later working as a waitress while stylizing her name as 'Ke$ha'. She gained mainstream attention through an uncredited number one hit on the US Billboard Hot 100 via her appearance on Flo Rida's "Right Round" in 2009. Her debut studio album, Animal (2010), which draws from electropop and dance-pop, was a commercial success, debuting at number one on the Billboard 200 and being certified 4× Platinum by the RIAA. Its lead single "Tik Tok" was once the best-selling digital single in history.

legal disputes

Kesha's career was interrupted between her second and third albums, Warrior and Rainbow, due to a legal dispute with former producer Dr. Luke. In a series of lawsuits known as Kesha v. Dr. Luke, Kesha accused him of physical, sexual, and emotional abuse and employment discrimination, while Dr. Luke claimed defamation and breach of contract. The case was settled out of court in June 2023.

Kesha has co-written songs for other artists such as Britney Spears, Ariana Grande, Miley Cyrus, Miranda Cosgrove, and Girls' Generation. She is listed as the 26th top artist on Billboard's 2010s decade-end charts and received the MTV Europe Music Award for Best New Act in 2010.

recent work

Cheska has fulfilled her five-album contract with Kemosabe Records and RCA Records by releasing Gag Order (2023) as her final contractual album. She subsequently released her first independent album, Period (2025), under her own label, Kesha Records.
