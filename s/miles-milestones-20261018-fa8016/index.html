Milestones is a 1958 studio album by Miles Davis, recorded with his first great quintet augmented to a sextet. It was released in September 1958 by Columbia Records.

Milestones is a 1958 studio album by Miles Davis, recorded with his first great quintet augmented to a sextet. It was released in September 1958 by Columbia Records.

composition and personnel

The album features Miles Davis on trumpet, flugelhorn, and piano (on "Sid's Ahead"), Julian "Cannonball" Adderley on alto saxophone, John Coltrane on tenor saxophone, Red Garland on piano (except on "Sid's Ahead"), Paul Chambers on double bass, and Philly Joe Jones on drums. Davis was experimenting with modal composition and improvisation during this period, abandoning key centers to use scale patterns beyond major and minor. This 'modal phase' coincided with the return of John Coltrane to Davis' group in 1958.

critical reception

The album has been received as a seminal work that helped shape jazz history, with AllMusic's Thom Jurek calling it a classic and noting the title track introduced modalism in jazz. Andy Hermann of PopMatters noted the album offers more aggressive swinging than Kind of Blue and highlights the same synergy between Coltrane and Cannonball Adderley. Jim Santella of All About Jazz described the personnel as 'the very best' and The Penguin Guide to Jazz included it as part of its suggested 'Core Collection'.

track listing and releases

The album originally released in mono and electronically re-channeled stereo, was later remixed and remastered in stereo for The Complete Columbia Recordings of Miles Davis with John Coltrane and reissued in stereo on the Columbia/Legacy label in 2001. Side one contains "Dr. Jekyll", "Sid's Ahead", and "Two Bass Hit". Side two contains "Miles" (later titled "Milestones"), "Billy Boy", and "Straight, No Chaser". CD reissues include alternate takes of "Two Bass Hit", "Milestones", and "Straight, No Chaser".
