Kanye West is an American rapper, songwriter, and record producer known for frequent stylistic shifts in his music. His production signature is characterized by the "chipmunk soul" style, and he is credited with facilitating the emergence of rappers who did not conform to gangsta rap conventions.

production techniques and sound

West's early production career began in the mid-1990s, where he created beats for local artists in Chicago. He gained recognition for his "chipmunk soul" production style while working as an in-house producer for Jay-Z's Roc-A-Fella Records. This style became a prominent feature of his early work, and he later shifted his sound up and down through various albums, stylistic shifts are a characteristic of his music.

landmark records

Key records include The College Dropout (2004), his acclaimed debut studio album; Late Registration (2005), his first of eleven US Billboard 200 number-one albums; Graduation (2007), which broke the record for most digital albums sold in a week; 808s & Heartbreak (2008), and My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy (2010) and Yeezus (2013), all of which were listed by Rolling Stone as some of the greatest albums of all time. Additionally, he revitalized Jay-Z's career with extensive contributions to the 2001 album The Blueprint.

collaborators and lineage

West was mentored by producer No I.D. and worked as a ghost producer for Deric "D-Dot" Angelettie. He became an in-house producer for Roc-A-Fella Records, producing for label cohorts such as Beanie Sigel and Freeway, and produced beats for artists like Ludacris, Alicia Keys, and Janet Jackson. Later, in 2011, he released the collaborative album Watch the Throne with Jay-Z, and in 2018, he released Kids See Ghosts with Kid Cudi, and in 2024, he released Vultures 1 and Vultures 2 with Ty Dolla Sign.

canonical status

West is one of the best-selling music artists with 160 million records sold and 24 Grammy Awards. He has been listed among the greatest rappers of all time and referred to as one of the most prominent figures in hip-hop. Rolling Stone has named him one of the "100 Greatest Songwriters of All Time" and listed six of his albums in its "500 Greatest Albums of All Time" list.
