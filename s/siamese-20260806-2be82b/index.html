Siamese describes things related to Siam, now known as Thailand, or more specifically the region of Central and Southern Thailand. The term is used in various contexts including animals, languages, ethnic groups, and cultural products.

animals

Several species and breeds of animals are associated with the term Siamese, including the Siamese crocodile, Siamese mud carp, Siamese algae eater, Siamese tigerfish, and the Siamese fighting fish. The Siamese fireback is the national bird of Thailand. Additionally, the Siamese cat is a domestic cat breed selectively bred from the Wichianmat landrace native to Thailand. This breed is characterized by a distinctive colourpoint coat resulting from temperature-sensitive albinism, blue almond-shaped eyes, and a triangular wedged head shape.

languages and ethnic groups

The term Siamese refers to several languages and the Thai people. The Central Thai language, or Siamese language, is the sole official language of Thailand and the first language of most people in Central Thailand. Southern Thai, or Southern Siamese, is also spoken in various regions. The Siamese people, or Thai noi people, are an ethnic group from Central and Southern Thailand. Other related groups include the Khorat Thai, Malaysian Siamese, and Yodaya people in Central Myanmar.

other uses

The term is also used to describe conjoined twins, a term derived from Chang and Eng Bunker, Siamese-American conjoined twin brothers. In mathematics, the Siamese method is a describes by Simon de la Loubère. In music, 'Siamese Dream' is a highly acclaimed alternative rock album by the Smashing Pumpkins, released in 1993. The album was produced by Butch Vig and Billy Corgan, and is certified 4x Platinum by the RIAA.
