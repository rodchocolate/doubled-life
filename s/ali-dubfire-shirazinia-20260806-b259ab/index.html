Ali "Dubfire" Shirazinia is an Iranian American house and techno DJ and producer. He was one half of the electronic music duo Deep Dish before pursuing a solo career. His solo work is characterized by minimal, techno, and house styles, which differ from the progressive house sound of Deep Dish.

early life and influences

Born in Iran, Shirazinia moved to the United States at age seven with his family for his father's university studies. Growing up in Washington, D.C., he was influenced by local punk scene and bands like Minor Threat and Fugazi, as well as classic hip hop, industrial, new wave, dub, jazz/rare groove. He frequented Yesterday And Today Records, where he became acquainted with artists such as Depeche Mode, Kraftwerk, Nitzer Ebb, Ministry, Einstürzende Neubauten and Jesus & Mary Chain.

career as part of deep dish

In 1991, Shirazinia and Sharam Tayebi formed the duo Deep Dish. The pair became known for their live DJ sets and house or dance remixes of artists like Gabrielle, Cher, Madonna and Janet Jackson. They won a Grammy in 2002 for their remix of Dido's "Thank You" and released the album Junk Science in 1998. After disbanding in 2006 to pursue solo careers, they reunited briefly in 2014 to release the single "Quincy" and perform at the Ultra Music Festival. They announced a permanent return to touring and studio work in 2024 with "The Future" tour, releasing their first original single in over a decade, "Midnight," in 2025.

solo career and sci+tec

Using the stage name Dubfire, Shirazinia's solo productions are focused on minimal, techno, and house. In 2007, he launched his own record label, Science + Technology Digital Audio (SCI + TEC). He released tracks such as "Roadkill" and "RibCage," the latter of which became the first release on Loco Dice's Desolat label. He has collaborated with British electronic act Underworld and producer Oliver Huntemann, and has produced remixes for Plastikman and Radio Slave. He also provides vocals for his production "I Feel Speed," a cover of an obscure Love & Rockets song.

recognition and awards

Dubfire has received various accolades, including being named International DJ Magazine's Player of the Year in 2008. He was nominated for Beatport's "Best Minimal Artist" and "Best Techno Artist" in 2008 and 2009, and received nominations for Best Single of 2007 for "Emissions" at the Beatport Music Awards. He won awards for Best Minimal Artist and Best Techno Artist from Beatport. He also mixed several compilations, including Global Underground 031: Taipei, which peaked at #16 on the Billboard Top Electronic Albums chart.
