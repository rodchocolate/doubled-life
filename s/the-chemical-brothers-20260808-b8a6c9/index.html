The Chemical Brothers are an English electronic music duo consisting of Tom Rowlands and Ed Simons, formed in Manchester in 1992. They were pioneers of the big beat genre and achieved significant commercial success with several number one albums in the UK.

formation and early career

Ed Simons and Tom Rowlands met at the University of Manchester in 1989, where they shared an interest in raves and club-going. Before forming the duo, Rowlands was part of a band called Ariel, which signed to deConstruction records. After Ariel fell apart, Rowlands and Simons began DJing in 1992 at Naked Under Leather, using the same alias '237 Turbo Nutters'. They initially called themselves The Dust Brothers, taking the name from a US production duo. To avoid confusion with the same-named US group, they changed their name to the Chemical Brothers in 1995 after the track 'Chemical Beats'.

Early recordings were produced using a Hitachi hi-fi system, an Atari ST, a sampler, and a keyboard. Their first release, 'Song to the Siren', was released on their own label, Diamond Records, before being signed by Junior Boy's Own. {// a bit more detail on early works like Fourteenth Century Sky EP and My Mercury Mouth EP}

musical evolution and albums

The duo's debut album, Exit Planet Dust (1995), sold over a million copies worldwide and debuted at No. 9 on the UK Albums Chart. Following their signing to Virgin Records, they released several number one albums in the qualidade own hole (1997) and Surrender (1999). Dig Your Own Hole and Surrender explored various electronic styles, including house music and rave sounds. Their subsequent albums Come with Us (2003), Push the Button (2005), and We Are The Night (2007) all reached number one in the UK.

Their third album, Surrender, was more experimental than previous efforts, featuring guest vocalists such as Noel Gallagher, Bernard Sumner, Hope Sandoval, and Jonathan Donahue. The album's cover art, created by Kate Gibb, was a treatment of a photograph called 'Jesus Amongst the Fans'.

More recent work includes the 2015 album Born in the Echoes, which produced the single 'Go', written and produced with American rapper Q-Tip.

collaborations and achievements

The duo has collaborated with a wide range of guest vocalists including Beth Orton, Noel Gallagher, Bernard Sumner, Q-Tip, St. Vincent, Beck, and Aurora. Tom Rowlands has also formed a supergroup called Tomora with Aurora.

In terms of achievements, they have won six Grammy Awards, including Best Rock Instrumental Performance, and Best Dance/Electronic Album for No Geography (2018).

Their music has been featured in various media, usage in the racing video game Need for Speed (2015), the 2022 DreamWorks Animation film The Bad Guys, and the horror survivalist film Apex (2026).
