electronic dance music artist

Onofrio Spencer Jeffrey Bruno, professionally known as Spencer Brown, is an American EDM artist, DJ, and producer. Born in Los Gatos, CA, he began DJing at age 12 to avoid school party music selections. He graduated from Duke University's Pratt School of Engineering with a distinction in Electrical & Computer Engineering and a minor in Computer Science, writing his thesis on omnidirectional speakers. His career progressed through collaborations with artists like deadmau5, Above & Beyond, and John Digweed, and he launched his own label, diviine, in 2022. He has released three continuous mix albums: 'Illusion of Perfection' (2018), 'Stream of Consciousness' (2020), and 'Equanimity' (2023). In 2024, he began a residency at Stereo Montreal and released music on Bedrock and Global Underground.

professional football player

Spencer Brown is an American professional football offensive tackle for the Buffalo Bills of the National Football League (NFL). Born February 28, 1998, he grew up in Lenox, Iowa, and played college football for the Northern Iowa Panthers. After moving from tight end to offensive tackle during his redshirt freshman season, he was selected by the Buffalo Bills in the third round of the 2021 NFL draft with the 93rd overall pick. Following two injury-prone seasons, he started all 17 games in the 2023 season, which earned him a high play-based pay incentive. He signed a four-year contract extension with the Bills on September 6, 2024, keeping him under contract through 2028.
