Brandi Carlile is an American singer-songwriter and producer whose music spans folk rock, Americana, classic rock, and alternative country. She has received eleven Grammy Awards and two Emmy Awards and was named one of Time's Women of the Year in 2026.

early life

Born on June 1, 1981, in Ravensdale, Washington, Carlile grew up in a small town outside Seattle. She began singing country songs on stage at age eight and started writing her own music at age 15. After being introduced to the music of Elton John, she taught herself to play piano and later learned guitar at age 17. As a teen, she was diagnosed with attention-deficit disorder and dropped out of Tahoma High School to pursue her musical career.

musical career

Carlile began her professional career performing in Seattle music clubs with Phil and Tim Hanseroth. She released her self-titled debut album in 2005, which reached No. 1 on the US Folk Albums chart. Her second album, The Story, produced by T Bone Burnett, was produced in an 11-day session to capture raw intensity. The title track "The Story" gained significant recognition through its use in General Motors commercials during the 2008 Summer Olympics and reached No. 1 on the Portuguese charts.
