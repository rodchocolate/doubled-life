RCA Records is an American record label owned by Sony Music Entertainment, a subsidiary of Sony Music Group and the Japanese conglomerate Sony Group Corporation. It is one of Sony Music's flagship labels and the corporate successor of the Victor Talking Machine Company. The label has released music across multiple genres including pop, rock, hip hop, afrobeat, electronic, R&B, blues, jazz, country, and classical.

history and ownership

RCA Records began as the RCA Victor Division of the Radio Corporation of America (RCA), following the purchase of the Victor Talking Machine Company in 1929. After the RCA Corporation was purchased by General Electric in 1986, GE sold its interest in the label to Bertelsmann in 1987, making it part of the BMG group. Following a merger between BMG and Sony in 2004, the label became part of Sony BMG Music Entertainment. In 2008, after the restructuring of Sony Music and the dissolution of Sony BMG, RCA Records became fully owned by Sony.

technical innovations and formats

In September 1931, RCA Victor introduced 'Program Transcription' records, which were 33 1/3 rpm records sold to the public. This format was a commercial failure due to thees exorbitant price of new two-speed turntables. In 1949, RCA Victor introduced the 7-inch 45 rpm micro-grooved vinylite record (the '45'), which competed with Columbia Records' 33 1/3 rpm LP discs. The label initially issued these 45s on colored vinyl based on musical genre: red for classical, green for country and polka, orange or cerise for rhythm and blues, midnight blue for Broadway musicals and operettas, black for contemporary pop, yellow for children's fare, and light blue for international music.

rca red seal

RCA Red Seal is a classical music label whose origins date to 1902. The first 'Gramophone Record Red Seal' discs were issued in 1901, and the practice was adopted by the Victor Talking Machine Company in 1903. Led by Enrico Caruso, these records changed public valuation of recorded music. The first Red Seal discs recorded by Victor in the United States were of Australian contralto Ada Crossley on April 30, 1903.
