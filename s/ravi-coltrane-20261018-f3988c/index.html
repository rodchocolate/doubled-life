Ravi Coltrane is an American jazz saxophonist and co-owner of the record label RKM Music. He has produced music for artists such as Ralph Alessi, David Gilmore, and Luis Perdomo.

personal life

Coltrane lives in Brooklyn, New York, and serves as Chairman of The Coltrane Home, an organization dedicated to preserving the museum and research center based in his parents' home in Dix Hills, Long Island. He married Kathleen Hennessy, manager of the Regattabar, in 1999 and they have two sons, William and Aaron.
