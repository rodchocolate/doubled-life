DJ Premier (born Christopher Edward Martin) is an American record producer and DJ. He is widely known for forming the hip-hop duo Gang Starr alongside rapper Guru, and later collaborating with Royce da 5'9" to form PRhyme in 2014.

early life and education

Christopher Edward Martin was born on March 21, 1966, in Fifth Ward, Houston. He was raised in Prairie View before moving to Brooklyn during his teenage years. While attending Prairie View A&M University, he developed his musical skills as the campus DJ and occasionally performed with the Marching Storm band.

musical career

Premier has produced all of Gang Starr's releases and many projects for the Gang Starr Foundation, including Jeru the Damaja's first two albums, The Sun Rises in the East and Wrath of the Math. He is closely affiliated with M.O.P., having produced approximately one-third of the songs on each subsequent album after their 1994 remix of "Rugged Neva Smoove." He has also worked with a wide range of artists including Nas, Jay-Z, Eminem, and Christina Aguilera, producing the Billboard Hot 100-charting single "Ain't No Other Man" for the latter. He recently collaborated with Nas on the studio album Light-Years, released in December 2025.

production style

Premier's production style is characterized by the New York sound of his earlier peers. He is known for sampling jazz, funk, and soul artists, as well as sampling an artist's own past work when creating new tracks for them. His encyclopedic memory of hip-hop lyrics allows him to scratch in lyrics from several different sources.

business and media appearances

In 2010, Martin founded the record label Year Round Records, which has signed acts such as Nick Javas, Khaleel, and NYGz. He also appeared as himself in the 2008 video game Grand Theft Auto IV as the host of radio station The Classics 104.1. Additionally, he hosted a weekly two-hour show, Live From HeadQCourterz, on Sirius Satellite Radio's Shade 45 until 2022.
