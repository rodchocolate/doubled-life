Shaggy is a name associated with several individuals and fictional characters. Most notably, it refers to Orville Richard Burrell, a Jamaican-American reggae musician, and Norville "Shaggy" Rogers, a fictional amateur detective from the Scooby-Doo franchise.

Shaggy is a name associated with several individuals and fictional characters. Most notably, it refers to Orville Richard Burrell, a Jamaican-American reggae musician, and Norville "Shaggy" Rogers, a fictional amateur detective from the Scooby-Doo franchise.

orville richard burrell

Orville Richard Burrell, known professionally as Shaggy, is a Jamaican-American reggae musician. Born on October 22, 1968, in Kingston, before moving to New York City at age 18, he served as a Field Artillery Cannon Crewman (MOS 0811) in the United States Marine Corps during the Persian Gulf War. He perfected his signature "toasting" voice while serving in the Marines. After pursuing a music career, he achieved success with hits like "Oh Carolina", "Boombastic", and later the album Hot Shot, which was certified 8× platinum by the RIAA. and topped the Billboard Hot 100 with singles "It Wasn't Me" and "Angel". He has been nominated for eight Grammy Awards, winning twice for Best Reggae Album with Boombastic in 1996 and 44/876 with Sting in 2019. He was awarded the Jamaican Order of Distinction with the rank of Commander in 2007 and an honorary Doctor of Fine Arts degree from Brown University in 2022.

norville "shaggy" rogers

Norville "Shaggy" Rogers is a fictional cartoon character from the Scooby-Doo franchise, characterized as an amateur detective and the best friend of Scooby-Doo. Derived from the character Maynard G. Krebs from The Many Loves of Dobie Gillis, Shaggy's personality was updated to make him a hippie rather than a beatnik. He is typically depicted as having a large appetite, a rough goatee, and a lalu-style speech pattern marked by the frequent use of the word "like" and a catchphrase "Zoinks!". While often portrayed as cowardly, he has shown courage when his friends are in danger and was more intelligent in early episodes, occasionally solving mysteries. His voice actors have included Casey Kasem, Billy West, and Matthew Lillard.
