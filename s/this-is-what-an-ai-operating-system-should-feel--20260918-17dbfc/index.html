transcript

New Hermes Kanban agent is crazy.

This new AI Kanban board plans and builds your ideas for you.

Most people have a board full of cards they drag around themselves,

or a chat window where ideas get lost and forgotten.

This is different. You drop in one idea,

a team of AI agents reads it,

works out what it actually is,

write a plan, then it stops.

It waits for you to approve before touching anything.

You approve once, then a project manager,

and subagents go build the whole thing while you do something else.

Idea in, finish thing out in one place you can check anytime.

The best part? Everything gets logged into memory

so you never have to re explain context again.

This is what an actual AI operating system looks like.
