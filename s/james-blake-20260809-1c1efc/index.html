James Blake Litherland, known professionally as James Blake, is an English singer-songwriter and record producer. He is recognized for his use of falsetto and emotive, soulful light head voice. He first gained recognition in 2010 following the release of three extended plays: The Bells Sketch, CMYK, and Klavierwerke.

early life and education

Blake was born on September 26, 1988, in Enfield, London, England. He is the only child of musician James Litherland and Helen Litherland. Having received classical piano training as a child, he attended Grange Park Primary School and The Latymer School. He later earned a degree in Popular Music from Goldsmiths, University of London, where he and friends hosted "Bass Society" music nights featuring artists such as Benga, Skream, and Distance.

musical career

Blake began his career with the 2009 release of Air & Lack Thereof on the Hemlock label, which became a favorite of BBC Radio 1 DJ Gilles Peterson. In 2010, he released the CMYK EP and the Klavierwerke EP, which was submitted as a second-year composition assignment at Goldsmiths. He signed with A&M Records to release his self-titled debut album in 2011, which peaked within the top ten of the UK Albums Chart. Following this, he moved to Republic Records, releasing Overgrown (2013), which also reached the top ten of the UK Albums Chart and entered the US Billboard 200.

collaborations and achievements

Blake has produced work for other artists including Beyoncé, Jay-Z, Kanye West, Kendrick Lamar, Frank Ocean, Rosalía, JID, Travis Scott, Normani, Justin Vernon, Dominic Maker, Oneohtrix Point Never, Strick and the partner Jameela Jamil. He has won a Mercury Prize, two Grammy Awards, and a Latin Grammy Award, and has received three Brit Award nominations.

title history

Blake's subsequent studio albums include The Colour in Anything (2016), Assume Form (2019), Friends That Break Your Heart (2021), Playing Robots Into Heaven (2023), and Trying Times (2026). In 2024, he released a collaborative album with rapper Lil Yachty titled Bad Cameo. He also collaborated with Bon Iver on the track "Fall Creek Boys Choir" in 2011 and worked with Raphael Trim as the moniker Harmonimix.
