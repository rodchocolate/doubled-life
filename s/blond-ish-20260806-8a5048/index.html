BLOND:ISH is a Canadian DJ, producer, and environmental activist. She is known for high-energy music and a mission to help one billion people live their best lives.

early life and career origins

Vivie-ann Bakos, born October 16, 1988, was born in Stoney Creek, Ontario, and later moved to Montreal, where she studied computer science at McGill University. In 2007, Bakos formed BLOND:ISH at the Winter Music Conference. The act began performing in 2008 with a residency at the Cherry Nightclub in Montreal. In 2010, she moved to London to focus on music production and work with veteran producer and engineer Ian Mckenzie. While in London, Bakos refined her sound and expanded her production skills.

recording history and awards

BLOND:ISH released the Lonely Days EP on Noir Music in 2012, followed by the Lovers in Limbo EP on Kompakt. In 2013, she joined Get Physical Music and released the Strange Attractions EP with Thomas Gandey. She was commissioned to produce remixes for artists such as Depeche Mode, Maya Jane Coles, Pete Tong, WhoMadeWho, and Kate Simko. Between 2013 and 2014, she recorded two Kompakt EPs, Inward Visions and Wunderkammer, in a makeshift studio near the Mayan temples in Tulum with Ian Mckenzie. Her first album, Welcome to the Present, was released under Kompakt in 2015. On April 16, 2016, she premiered her debut Essential Mix on BBC Radio 1, which was recorded in Tulum, Mexico. She has released music on labels such as Noir, Leftroom, Get Physical, and Kompakt. She has also performed at Burning Man. In 2019, Anstascia D’Elene Corniere split from BLOND:ISH, while Vivie-Ann Bakos continued the project with Ian Mckenzie. Her single "Waves" was nominated for Underground Dance Single of the Year at the 2022 Juno Awards, and "Aye Aye" received a nomination in the same category in 2023. In 2024, "Call My Name" won the award for Underground Dance Single of the Year at the Juno Awards. In February 2025, she released her sophomore album Never Walk Alone, which features BioVinyl, a recyclable material used to address fossil fuel use in vinyl production.

performances and abracadabra

BLOND:ISH has performed at festivals and events such as Ultra Music Festival, Mysteryland, the BPM Festival, SXMusic Festival, SXM Festival, Symbiosis Gathering, ENTER., Wisdom of the Glove, Echoes from Agartha, Flying Circus, Cercle, and Coachella's Yuma Tent in 2024. She also performed at Rock in Rio and Tomorrowland festival in 2024. It was announced on December 3, 2024, that she would perform at Electric Forest in June 2025. BLOND:ISH launched the record label, brand, and event series ABRACADABRA in 2017. The brand has held events in New York, Miami, and other cities including Los Angeles, Paris, Buenos Aires, Tulum and Mykonos. In 2018, the label hosted an ABRACADABRA stage at Tomorrowland, taking over the LEAF stage for both Saturdays of the festival. In 2020, she launched the ABRACADABRA TV channel on Twitch during the COVID-19 lockdown. In 2025, Abracadabra held an 11-week headline residency at Pacha Ibiza, which Billboard described as "a landmark moment" for the venue. This residency returned for a second season in 2026.

bye bye plastic foundation

In January 2020, Bakos co-founded the Bye Bye Plastic Foundation with Camille Guitteau to promote sustainability in the music industry through the #PlasticFreeParty movement. The charity's goal is to eliminate single-use plastics from the music industry by 2025. This is either achieved by providing resources for promoters and promoting eco-riders for artists. The organization's Eco-Rider, a sustainability clause for touring artists' performance contracts, has been adopted by more than 1,500 DJs, preventing over 325,000 single-use plastic bottles from circulation. In September 2025, the foundation launched the Zero Plastic Club: NYC initiative during Climate Week NYC to eliminate 42 tons of annual plastic waste from the nightlife industry in New York.

collaborations

BLOND:ISH has produced remixes for artists such as Depeche Mode, WhoMadeWho, Maya Jane Coles, Pete Tong and Kate Simko. She also released a remix of Taylor Swift's "Fortnight" on May 21, 2024. Additionally, Miss Monique has remixed "Sorry" by BLOND:ISH featuring Madonna.
