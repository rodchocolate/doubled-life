Uno Pizzeria & Grill is a United States-origin franchised pizzeria restaurant chain under the parent company Uno Restaurant Holdings Corporation. It is best known for its Chicago-style deep dish pizza.

founding and early history

Pizzeria Uno was established in 1943 in the Near North Side of Chicago, Illinois, by Ike Sewell, a former University of Texas football player, and Ric Riccardo, a World War II veteran. While Pizzeria Uno claims to have originated deep-dish pizza, a 1956 article from the Chicago Daily News asserts that the recipe was created by chef Rudy Malnati Sr. Ric Riccardo's original 1945 recipe included dough made with sugar, butter, and scalded milk; this was later adjusted by cook Alice Mae Redmond to use water, olive oil, and a "secret dough conditioner."

expansion and franchising

In 1978, Ike Sewell licensed the restaurant's name and concept to the Uno Restaurant Holdings Corporation in the Boston area. The company began franchising in 1980 and established the Uno Foods division in 1988 to sell frozen and pre-made deep-dish pizzas in supermarkets, airports, and other venues. By 2005, the company's press kit claimed over 200 locations across 32 states, Washington, D.C., Puerto Rico, South Korea, and the United Arab Emirates.

financial restructuring and modern era

Uno Restaurant Holdings Corp filed for Chapter 11 protection in January 2010, emerging from bankruptcy in July 2010 after reducing total debt from $176.3 million to $40 million. The company was later sold to Newport Global Advisors in May 2017. By November 2023, the chain had 77 restaurants in at least 20 states, Washington, D.C., and Puerto Rico, as well as overseas units in India and Saudi Arabia. Under CEO Erik Frederick, the company has focused on adding franchises within major hotel chains and creating a family room experience rather than formal dining.

format and menu changes

Starting in 1994, Uno's broadened its menu to include other dishes by updating kitchens with fryers, grills, and sauté stations. In 1997, the name was changed to Pizzeria Uno, Chicago Bar & Grill, later simplified to Uno Chicago Grill. These expanded menus and format changes were not applied to the original Chicago locations (Pizzeria Uno and Pizzeria Due). By 2023, CEO Erik Frederick had begun trimming back the menu.
