music

The term 'Green River' is associated with several musical works and artists. Creedence Clearwater Revival released a third studio album titled Green River on August 7, 1969, via Fantasy Records. The album features hits such as "Bad Moon Rising" and the title track "Green River," both of which peaked at No. 2 on the U.S. charts. The song "Green River" was inspired by Putah Creek near Winters, California, and a lime-flavored soda pop syrup label. The Seattle-based rock band Green River, formed in 1984, is considered one of the first grunge bands and served as a precursor to early 1990s groups like Pearl Jam and Mudhoney. Their first record, Come on Down, is often cited as the first grunge record released. They recorded their first EP, Dry as a Bone, for Sub Pop, and their final full-length album, Rehab Doll, was released in June 1988.
