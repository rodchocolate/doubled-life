cassidy (rapper)

Barry Adrian Reese, known as Cassidy, is an American rapper who first gained attention in the late 1990s as a battle rapper. He was discovered by producer Swizz Beatz and signed to Full Surface Records in a joint venture with J Records. His debut studio album, Split Personality (2004), was certified gold by the RIAA and peaked at number two on the U.S. Billboard 200. His second album, I'm a Hustla (2005), reached number five on the Billboard 200 and featured a platinum-certified lead single of the same name. His third album, B.A.R.S. The Barry Adrian Reese Story (2007) peaked at number ten on the Billboard 200. After leaving major labels, he released his fourth album, C.A.S.H. (2010), through Carmelo Anthony's Kross Over Entertainment and later released independent albums under his imprint, Mayhem Music.

joanna cassidy

Joanna Virginia Caskey, known as Joanna Cassidy, is an American actress and former model who began her career in the 1960s. She has appeared in numerous films such as The Outfit (1973), Blade Runner (1982), where she played Zhora Salome, and Who Framed Roger Rabbit (1988). She also starred in the NBC comedy series Buffalo Bill (1983-1984), for which she won a Golden Globe Award. Her television work includes recurring roles on Six Feet Under (2001-2005) as Margaret Chenowith and Body of Proof (2011-2013) as Joan Hunt, as well as starring in the lead role of CIA operative Liz "Foxfire" Towne in Code Name: Foxfire (1985).

katie cassidy

Katherine Evelyn Anita Cassidy, born in 1986, is an American actress known for her former role as Laurel Lance / Black Canary and later Laurel Lance / Black Siren in the Arrowverse series Arrow, The Flash, and Legends of Tomorrow. She began her career with minor television roles and horror films such as When a Stranger Calls (2006) and Black Christmas (2006), earning her the label of "Scream Queen." She also had leading roles in the slasher series Harper's Island (2009) and the remake of Melrose Place (2009-2010). Other notable works include a recurring role as Juliet Sharp in Gossip Girl (2010-2012) and a role as Kris Fowles in A Nightmare on Elm Street (2010).

legal and personal matters

Barry Adrian Reese (Cassidy the rapper) resides in Philadelphia, and was involved in a murder case in 2005. On April 15, 2005, three men including Cassidy fired on three unarmed men during an argument in West Oak Lane, Philadelphia. This resulted in the the death of Desmond Hawkins. A warrant for his arrest was later issued on June 9, 2005, and he surrendered to the afternoon of June 17. He was denied bail and sent to Philadelphia's Curran-Fromhold Correctional Facility. Later, a primary witness withdrew their confession, which led to the judge to state that Cassidy would stand trial for third-degree murder.
