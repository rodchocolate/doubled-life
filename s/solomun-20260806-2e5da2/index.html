Mladen Solomun is a Bosnian-German DJ, producer and remixer known by his stage name Solomun. He has won the DJ Awards for Best Producer, Best DJ, and Best Melodic House four times.

early life and career beginnings

Born in Travnik, Yugoslavia (now Bosnia and Herzegovina), Solomun grew up in Hamburg, Germany. As a child, he worked for his father's construction company and later spent five years working in film production. He was introduced to music beyond commercial radio through cassette tapes provided by his cousin. At age 16, he began playing at House of Youth for other teenagers; he started producing music at age 23. To gain production skills, he learned from a friend who ran a hip-hop label for approximately one year.

musical production and labels

In 2005, Solomun began producing music and co-founded the record label Diynamic with partner Adriano Trolio. The label's motto is "Do It Yourself". His first EP on Diynamic, the Solomun EP, was released in 2006, followed by various EPs, compilations, and the 2009 album Dance Baby. In December 2010, he launched a second, more experimental label called 2DIY4.

professional achievements and awards

Solomun has received numerous industry awards, including being named Mixmag's "DJ of the Year" in 2012 and winning Best International DJ at the Cool Awards in Brazil in 2012 and 2019. He also won the DJ Awards Ibiza for Category: Deep House in 2013 and 2015, and Category: Melodic House and Techno kind of in 2018. In 2011, his remix of Noir & Haze's "Around" was named Remix of the Year by Resident Advisor. He also collaborated with singer Inéz on the 2021 singles "Tuk Tuk" and "Prospect".

other ventures and media

Until June 2014, Solomun co-owned the Hamburg-based club Ego with his sister and Adriano Trolio. He held two residencies in Ibiza at Pacha and Destino under the name "Solomun +1" from at least 2015 to 2016. In 2018, he appeared as an in-residence DJ for the videogame Grand Theft Auto Online (After Hours DLC) and created a music video for "Customer is King" using the Grand Theft Auto V engine in collaboration with Rockstar Games.
