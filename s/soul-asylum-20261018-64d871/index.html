Soul Asylum is an American rock band formed in 1981 in Minneapolis, Minnesota. They are best known for their 1993 hit "Runaway Train," which won the Grammy Award for Best Rock Song.

formation and early years

The band was originally called Loud Fast Rules, consisting of Dave Pirner, Dan Murphy, and Karl Mueller. Pat Morley took over on drums, completing the same lineup. They changed their name to Soul Asylum in 1983 and released their debut album, Say What You Will... Everything Can Happen, in 1984. Between this release and their second album, Grant Young joined as drummer. In 1986, they released three albums: Made to Be Broken, Time's Incinerator, and While You Were Out. The band toured relentlessly, opening for other American bands and later headlining the "Flip Your Wig" tour with fellow Twin Cities band Hüsker Dü in early 1986. Their sound was described as a mix of punk, hardcore, 1970s rock, country, and self-effacing kitsch, leading some to describe them as a 'grunge precursor'.

mainstream success

After signing with A&M Records in 1988, the band released Clam Dip & Other Delights and Hang Time. However, And the Horse They Rode In On (1990) was a commercial disappointment, leading to the group to be dropped by A&M. After playing acoustic shows in the early 1990s, they signed with Columbia Records and released Grave Dancers Union in 1992. This became their most popular album, featuring the hit 'Runaway Train'. The song's music video, directed by Tony Kaye, featured photos of missing children and was instrumental in reuniting several children with their families; between 21 and 26 missing children were found after being featured in the video.

later years and lineup changes

The band released Let Your Dim Light Shine (1995), which reached number six in the US and Canada, and Candy from a Stranger (1998). Following a break, they began recording a new album in 2004, but sessions were put on hold when bassist Karl Mueller was diagnosed with throat cancer; he died the following year. Guest musicians, including Tommy Stinson, later joined as a permanent member to complete The Silver Lining (2006). Founding member Dan Murphy left the group at the end of 2012 after the release of Delayed Reaction. The band's most recent releases are Hurry Up and Wait (2020) and Slowly But Shirley (2024).
