Phish is an American rock band formed in Burlington, Vermont, in 1983. The group consists of guitarist Trey Anastasio, bassist Mike Gordon, drummer Jon Fishman, and keyboardist Page McConnell. They are known for their musical improvisation and jams during live performances.

formation and early history

Phish was formed at the University of Vermont in 1983 by Trey Anastasio, Jeff Holdsworth, Mike Gordon, and Jon Fishman. The band's first concert took place on December 2, 1983, at Harris Millis Cafeteria. After a period of hiatus due to Trey Anastasio's suspension from the university, Anastasio collaborated with lyricist Tom Marshall to write songs. The quartet officially named themselves Phish in October 1984. Keyboardist Page McConnell joined the band as a guest in May 1985 and became a full-time member in September 1985. Jeff Holdsworth left the group in March 1986, leaving the lineup stable since then.

musical style and influence

Phish's music blends elements of various genres including funk, reggae, progressive rock, psychedelic rock, folk, country, jazz, blues, bluegrass, and pop. They are part of a 'jam band' movement inspired by the Grateful Dead's live performance format. The band has developed a large following through word of mouth and the exchange of live recordings. As of 2020, they have sold more than 8 million albums and DVDs in the United States.

recording and discography

Phish has released numerous studio albums and live releases, including the 'Live Phish' series and 'Live Bait' series. They were signed to Elektra Records from 1991 to 2005, before founding their own independent label, JEMP Records, for archival releases. Since 2002, they have officially released every show they performed. The band has also been the subject of various tribute albums across different genres such as bluegrass and reggae.
