Guerilla Records was a British record label founded in 1990 by Dick O'Dell and John Gosling, along with William Orbit. The label specialized in a style of progressive house music that the founders preferred to call "dub house".

overview and sound

Guerilla Records was a key player in the development of the progressive house sound in the UK during the early 1990s. The label's output focused on a specific style of progressive house known as "dub house". Artists associated with the label included Leftfield, Bass-O-Matic, React2Rhythm, Spooky, Moody Boyz, Felix Da Housecat, and Billie Ray Martin.

visual identity

The label's generic sleeve design, characterized by a distinctive blue camouflage pattern, was created by graphic designer Steve Cook. Cook is also known for his art direction and design of the British comic 2000 AD.

history and dissolution

Guerilla Records Ltd was wound up in 1995 and finally dissolved in 2003. According to some sources, the label is thought to have been pivotal in the growth of a scene around the progressive house genre.
