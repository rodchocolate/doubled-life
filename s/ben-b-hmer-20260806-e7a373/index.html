Ben Böhmer is a German DJ, producer and remixer born in 1993 from Göttingen, Germany. He primarily performs and produces melodic house music and indie electronica. He currently lives in Berlin.

early life and career beginnings

Böhmer played the trumpet and piano during his childhood before transitioning to composing music with a synthesizer. His first single, "Promise You," was produced at the end of 2015 and appeared on the Mauerblümchen compilation from the Göttingen-based label Ton Töpferei. This was followed by the eponymous Promise You EP in November 2016, and subsequent releases on labels such as Bade Records, Audiolith Records, Carton-Pate Records, The Soundgarden Records, Freundchen Records, Ostfunk Records, and Keller Records.

professional growth and label affiliations

In 2017, Böhmer released the Submission EP on Sacrebleu, a label founded that same year by German DJ duo AKA AKA. He also published the single "Flug & Fall" on Anjunadeep's fourth exploration compilation in September 2017, followed by the Morning Falls EP in early 2018. Böhmer has released tracks on labels including Cercle Music, Ninja Tune, and Anjunadeep.

albums and discography

Böhmer's debut LP, Breathing, was released in November 2019 via Anjunadeep. His second studio album, Begin Again, followed in September 2021 via the same label. He has also released several live albums, including Live from Printworks London (2020) and Live at the Roundhouse, London (2022), as well as his 2024 album Bloom. His discography includes numerous singles and EPs, such as the Phases EP (2020) and The Apparitions (2022).
