The Artful Dodger is a character from Charles Dickens' 1838 novel Oliver Twist, known as a pickpocket and leader of a gang of child criminals. The term has since become an idiom for people who engage in skillful deception. It is also the name of a UK garage duo consisting of songwriters and producers Mark Hill and Pete Devereux.

literary character

Jack Dawkins, known as the Artful Dodger, is a pickpocket in Oliver Twist who is leader of a gang of child criminals overseen by Fagin. He is described as a child who acts like an adult, wearing oversized adult clothes and seldom giving in to childish urges. In the novel, he is describes as snub-nosed, flat-browed, and short for his age with bow-legs and small, sharp, ugly eyes. Ultimately, the Dodger is caught stealing a silver snuff box and is presumably transported to a penal colony in Australia. He considers himself a 'victim of society' and maintains a great respect for Fagin.

adaptations and media

The character has been portrayed by various actors in stage, television, and film adaptations. In the musical Oliver!, he was played by performers such as Phil Collins and Tony Robinson. On screen, he was played by Elijah Wood in a 1997 TV film and Rita Ora in the 2021 film Twist. Additionally, a 2023 Hulu/Disney+ series titled The Artful Dodger serves as a sequel to Dickens' novel, depicting Jack Dawkins as an adult in 1850s Australia where he has become a decorated Navy officer and surgeon. In this series, he is joined by former mentor Norbert Fagin.

musical duo

Artful Dodger was a UK garage duo featuring producers Mark Hill and Pete Devereux. They achieved success in the 1990s and early 2000s with seven consecutive UK top-20 singles. The project began as a handle for underground bootleg recordings released on Fagin Records, using the name 'Artful Dodger' to protect their identities from record companies. Their sound was a blend of R&B and garage, blending 2-step rhythms with house music influences. They are known for their association with vocalist Craig David, who was originally intended to be the frontman for the group.
