Space Laces is an artist mentioned as a collaborator of Excision and Destroid.

collaborations with excision

Space Laces has collaborated with the Canadian DJ and producer Excision on multiple studio albums. He is featured on the album 'Virus' (2016) and 'Apex' (2018).

work with destroid

Space Laces is a collaborator of the dubstep supergroup Destroid, which consists of members Excision, Downlink, and KJ Sawka. He is featured on their first single, "Destroid 11. Get Stupid".
