Full Surface Records is a record label founded in 2001 by American hip hop recording artist and producer Swizz Beatz. The label operated through Sony's J Records, as well as Universal's Interscope, Motown, and Universal Motown.

Full Surface Records is a record label founded in 2001 by American hip hop recording artist and producer Swizz Beatz. The label operated through Sony's J Records, as well as Universal's Interscope, Motown, and Universal Motown.

roster and production

The label's roster of artists includes Swizz Beatz, Bone Thugs-n-Harmony, Cassidy, Drag-On, Eve, Mashonda, Rich Hil, and Yung Wun. Executive leadership consists of founder and CEO Swizz Beatz, A&R Grady Spivey, General Manager Ashaunna Ayars, and Management Terrence Dean. The label's production team includes producers Neo da Matrix, Snags, and the producer group The Individualz (composed of Avenue, Joe Bravo, and Jose), who have produced for artists such as Jay-Z, 50 Cent, and Mary J. Blige.

associated releases

Swizz Beatz released his compilation album G.H.E.T.T.O. Stories (2002) and his debut studio album One Man Band Man (2007) through Full Surface Records. Swizz Beatz also executive produced Bone Thugs-n-Harmony's seventh studio album, Strength & Loyalty, released in May 2007.
