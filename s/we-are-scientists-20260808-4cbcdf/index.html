We Are Scientists are a New York City-based rock band that formed in Berkeley, California, in 1999. The group consists primarily of guitarist and vocalist Keith Murray and bass guitarist Chris Cain.

formation and early years

Keith Murray and Chris Cain met at Pomona College in 1997 and cofounded the the same group with roommate Scott Lamb in late 1999. In their early years, they were influenced by comic books and films, with many of their earliest songs about fighting monsters. After moving to the Los Angeles area in late 2000, they recruited Michael Tapper as drummer, replacing Keith Murray's role as drummer. This allowed Murray to become the vocalist and guitarist. The band recorded several EPs including Bitching!, In Action, and The Wolf's Hour before their first proper full-length album.
