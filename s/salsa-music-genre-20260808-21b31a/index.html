Salsa is a style of dance music influenced by Caribbean traditions and music from Cuba, Puerto Rico, the Dominican Republic, Colombia, and Venezuela. It blends African cultural syncretism in music and dance with European influences and indigenous instruments, as well as contributions from the contiguous United States.

subgenres and evolution

Salsa dura, also known as salsa brava or salsa gorda, is a style developed in the_1970s that emphasizes instrumental parts over lead vocals. It originated in New York City where ensembles like the Fania All-Stars adapted the salsa genre to a descarga format. In contrast, salsa romántica is a Latin pop-oriented subgenre that has predominated since the late 1980s. Additionally, parallel modernization of Cuban son was developed by artists such as Los Van Van and Irakere under the name songo, which evolved into timba in the late 80s; both styles are currently labeled as salsa.
