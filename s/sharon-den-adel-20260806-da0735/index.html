Sharon den Adel is a Dutch singer and songwriter who serves as the lead vocalist for the symphonic metal band Within Temptation. She co-founded the group in 1996 with her partner Robert Westerholt. Den Adel has also pursued solo work and various collaborations across multiple musical genres.

early life and education

Born on 12 July 1974 in Waddinxveen, Sharon den Adel lived in Indonesia for five years as a child, where she was inspired by Balinese dancers. After returning to the Netherlands from Suriname, she experienced bullying due to her skin tone, hairstyle, and clothing style. Den Adel holds a bachelor's degree in fashion design, which she now uses to design the band's merchandise and her own stage costumes.

musical career

Den Adel is a self-taught mezzo-soprano singer who practiced alone for three or four hours daily. She began performing with various bands at age 14, including the blues rock band Kashiro. In 1996, she and Robert Westerholt formed Within Temptation (originally called The Portal). Den Adel describes their work as cinematic and epic. Before the band's success with the hit single "Ice Queen", she worked for a fashion company. She prefers singing in English over Dutch, stating that Dutch does not fit their musical style.

solo projects and collaborations

In 2017, den Adel announced the solo project My Indigo, which began as a way to deal with personal problems and writer's block. The self-titled first album was released on 20 April 2018. She has collaborated with numerous artists across genres, including Armin van Buuren on the award-winning trance track "In and Out of Love", Evanescence on the single "Use My Voice" in 2020, and various rock and metal opera projects such as Avantasia and Ayreon. She also performed a duet with Marko Hietala for Delain's album Lucidity.

personal life

Sharon den Adel and her long-term partner and bandmate Robert Westerholt have three children together, with the first born in December 2005, the second in June 2009, and the third in March 2011.
