Brian Eno is an English musician, songwriter, record producer, visual artist, sound designer, author and political activist. He is recognized for his pioneering contributions to electronica and ambient music, as well as his work in rock and pop music. A self-described "non-musician", he has been described as one of popular music's most influential and innovative figures.

early life and education

Born on 15 May 1948 in Melton, Suffolk, England, Eno was the son of William Arnold Eno, a postal worker and clock and watch repairer, and Maria Alphonsine, a Belgian national. He was raised Catholic and attended St Joseph's College, Ipswich, a Catholic grammar school. In the mid-1960s, he studied painting and experimental music at the art school of Ipswich Civic College and later earned a diploma in Fine Arts from the Winchester School of Art in 1969. While at Winchester, a lecture by Pete Townshend helped him realize he could make music without formal training.

musical career

Eno joined the glam rock group Roxy Music as a synthesiser player in 1971 and recorded two albums with them before departing in 1973. Following this, he released his first solo album, Here Come the Warm Jets (1974), and explored minimal music on recordings such as Discreet Music (1975) and Ambient 1: Music for Airports (1978), the latter of which coined the term "ambient music". He has collaborated with numerous artists including David Bowie, John Cale, and David Byrne. As a producer, he has worked with U2, Coldplay, and Peter Gabriel.

visual arts and other media

Since his student days, Eno has worked in other media including writing, film, and sound installations. In the 1970s, he co-developed Oblique Strategies, a pack of cards with aphorisms to spur creative thinking. His installation work includes projects at the Lovell Telescope at Jodrell Bank in 2016 and the sails of the Sydney Opera House in 2009.

activism and other interests

Eno is a founding member of the Long Now Foundation and an advocate for humanitarian causes. His political activism includes opposing Brexit and the Conservative Party, advocating for freedom for Julian Assange, and raising awareness about the climate crisis and conditions in the Gaza Strip. In September 2025, he signed a pledge with Film Workers for Palestine to not work with Israeli film institutions implicated in genocide and apartheid against the Palestinian people.
