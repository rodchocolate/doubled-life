Christina Aguilera and DJ Premier are musical collaborators who worked together on Aguilera's fifth studio album, Back to Basics. Their partnership was a result of Aguilera recruiting the producer for his jazz-influenced work with Gang Starr.

collaboration on back to basics

In 2006, Christina Aguilera recruited DJ Premier to produce for her album Back to Basics. Aguilera had known of his jazz-influenced work with Gang Starr in the late 1980s and early 1990s and wanted her album to draw inspirations from his song 'Jazz Thing'. This collaboration marked DJ Premier's first time venturing into the pop world. According to DJ Premier, he was surprised by the call because of the differences in their audiences, but he was open to challenges and trying something new. He noted that Aguilera sent him CDs of music she was grew up on in the early '70s—including artists like Aretha Franklin, Etta James, and Marvin Gaye—to establish the vibe she wanted.

production of 'ain't no other man'

DJ Premier produced and co-wrote 'Ain't No Other Man', the lead single from Back to Basics. He described the track as a 'sassy' recording similar to Aretha Franklin's 'Respect' and noted its fast pace, which was at 132 beats per minute, differing from his earlier projects. Aguilera was 'in love' with the high energy of the track when she first heard it, and consequently chose it as the lead single. The song is a pop, funk and R&B song incorporating elements of soul, blues and jazz, featuring a brass sample from 'Hippy Skippy Moon Strut' by Dave Cortez & The Moon People and a vocal sample from 'The Cissy's Thang' by the Soul Seven.

further work

DJ Premier Premier explained that while some people expressed reservations about the collaboration, they were later impressed by records like 'Ain't No Other Man' and 'Back In The Day'. He revealed in an early 2014 interview with New Zealand's Rip It Up magazine that he was working with Aguilera again.
