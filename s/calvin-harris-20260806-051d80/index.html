Calvin Harris is a Scottish DJ, record producer, singer and songwriter. He has released seven studio albums and has achieved significant commercial success in the UK and US markets.

early life and beginnings

Born as Adam Richard Wiles on 17 January 1984 in Dumfries, Scotland, to English parents, Harris began recording bedroom demos in 1999. To afford DJ gear, he worked in a local fish processing factory and stocked shelves in supermarkets. After releasing two songs under the name 'Stouffer' in 2002, he moved to London, but returned home to Dumfries after failing to find job opportunities. He gained popularity on Myspace, which led to him being signed by Mark Gillespie of Global Gathering, who continues to represent him today. Harris has stated that 'Calvin Harris' is a stage alter-ego and prefers to be addressed as Adam Wiles in personal interactions.

musical career and discography

Harris's debut album, I Created Disco (2007), was produced entirely on an Amiga computer and was certified gold by the BPI. His second album, Ready for the Weekend (2009), debuted at number one in the UK. His third studio album, 18 Months (2012), marked a shift away from vocals toward music production and reached number 19 on the US Billboard 200. He later released Motion (2014), which peaked at number five on the US Billboard 200 and was certified Platinum in the US, Australia, and New Zealand. His fifth and sixth albums, Funk Wav Bounces Vol. 1 (2017) and Funk Wav Bounces Vol. 2 (2022), achieved international commercial success. In August 2024, he surprise released his seventh studio and first compilation album, 96 Months.

achievements and recognition

Harris holds the Official Charts Company record for most number one singles in the UK during the 2010s. In 2013, he surpassed Michael Jackson's record for the most top ten singles from one album with nine from 18 Months. He was the first UK solo artist to reach over one billion streams on Spotify and the first artist to place three songs simultaneously in the top 10 of Billboard's Dance/Electronic Songs chart. His accolades include a Grammy win for Best Music Video in 2013, an Ivor Novello Award in 2013, and being named Top Dance/Electronic Artist at the 2015 Billboard Music Awards. He also topped Forbes' list of the world's highest-paid DJs from 2013 to 2018.
