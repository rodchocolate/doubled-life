Blue Note is an international chain of jazz clubs and restaurants founded in New York City in 1981. It has expanded into a global brand with venues across North America, South America, Europe, and Asia.

history

The original Blue Note opened on September 30, 1981, in Greenwich Village by owner and founder Danny Bensusan. The opening performance featured the Nat Adderley Quintet. Following its success in New York, the brand began expanding internationally, starting with the opening of Blue Note Tokyo in 1988. In the 21st century, it has continued to grow, with a new location opening in Los Angeles in 2025 and a venue set to open in London in August 2026.

global operations

Blue Note Entertainment Group operates venues that maintain the intimate atmosphere and artist-focused programming of the original New York location. Current locations include cities such as New York City, Waikiki, Los Angeles, Tokyo, Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo, Milan, Beijing, and Shanghai. Each venue features nightly live performances and dining service, blending traditional jazz with contemporary genres like soul, R&B, and hip-hop.

brand extensions

The brand has expanded into other initiatives including the Blue Note Jazz Festival, which began in New York City in 1911 and later expanded to Napa, California, and Los Angeles. It also operates Half Note Records, a live record label founded in 2001 (or 1998), providing a live recording venue for musicians such as James Carter and Avishai Cohen. Additionally, Blue Note Travel, established in 2015, provides music-focused cultural travel programs.
