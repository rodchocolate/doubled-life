Gigamesh is the stage name of Matthew Thomas Masurka, a Minneapolis-based American music producer and DJ. He is most recognized for producing Mike Posner's track "Cooler Than Me" and his remix of Foster the People's "Pumped Up Kicks".

Gigamesh is the stage name of Matthew Thomas Masurka, a Minneapolis-based American music producer and DJ. He is most recognized for producing Mike Posner's track "Cooler Than Me" and his remix of Foster the People's "Pumped Up Kicks".

career overview

Masurka has toured internationally, performing in North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia. His work has been released through labels such as Kitsuné, Nurvous Records, and OurLabel International. He has produced tracks for artists like Mike Posner and Outasight.

discography

Gigamesh's discography includes the 2016 album Time Travel on Hyperreal. His singles and EPs include "When You're Dancing" (2011), the Gigamesh EP (2011), the Remix EP (2011), All My Life EP (2012), and the release of "Enjoy" in 2013 on Nurvous Records.

collaborations and remixes

Prior to his solo career, Masurka was involved in collaborative projects Discotech and Señor Stereo with DJs Danny Daze and Joe Maz. He has remixed a variety of artists including Rihanna, Donna Summer, Michael Jackson, Radiohead, and The Velvet Underground.

production work

He produced Mike Posner's "Cooler Than Me" (2010) and Outasight's "Tonight Is the Night" (2011).
