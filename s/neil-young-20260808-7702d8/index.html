Neil Young is a Canadian-American musician and singer-songwriter. He has managed a longtime project called the Neil Young Archives, which began as archival releases of studio and live recordings and evolved into a website featuring high resolution audio streaming of his recording output.

neil young archives

The Neil Young Archives is a project that began in the late 1980s. It is divided into several series: the main series of box sets, the Performance Series consisting of individual live material releases, and the Special Release Series for previously unreleased albums. The main series includes Volume I (1963–1972), Volume II (1972–1976), and Volume III (1976–1987). Volume I won a Grammy Award for Best Art Direction on a Boxed or Special Limited Edition Package in 2010. Volume IV is currently in production and expected to be released as of 2026, covering the period from 1987 to 2004.

live performances and recordings

In September 1973, Neil Young and his backing band, the Santa Monica Flyers, played a series of shows at the Roxy Theatre on the Sunset Strip in Los Angeles. These recordings were released as the live album Roxy: Tonight's the Night Live, which was released in April 2018 and also included in the Archives Volume II boxset. The album features Young Young playing vocals, guitar, piano, and harmonica, and includes an additional track called "The Losing End" in the Archives Volume II version.

collaborators and bands

Throughout his career, Young has worked with various ensembles. During the early years covered by Volume I of the archives, he was part of The Squires and Buffalo Springfield. He also recorded tracks with Crazy Horse and Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young. In the period between 1972 and 1976, he worked with The Stray Gators, Santa Monica Flyers, and The Stills-Young Band. Between 1976 and 1987, he collaborated with Nicolette Larson, Linda Ronstadt, and The Ducks in Santa Cruz, as well as the Give to the Wind Orchestra and the International Harvesters.
