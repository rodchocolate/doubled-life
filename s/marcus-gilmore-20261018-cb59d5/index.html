Marcus Gilmore is an American jazz drummer born on October 10, 1986. He has toured professionally since age sixteen and is the grandson of Roy Haynes.

education and career

Gilmore graduated from the Fiorello H. LaGuardia High School of Music & Art and Performing Arts and received full ride scholarships to the Manhattan School of Music and the Juilliard School of Music. He was a protégé of the 2018 Rolex Mentors And Protégé project. In 2019, he appeared on the cover of Modern Drummer Magazine for their June issue. He contributed as a primary artist to the Original Motion Picture Soundtrack for the 2020 Disney-Pixar film Soul. Additionally, he was commissioned by the American Composers Orchestra to write his first orchestral work, which debuted in 2020 with members of the Cape Town Philharmonic and premiered its full composition in New York City in June 2023.

musical style and influences

Gilmore's style is influenced by a wide variety of sources, including Tony Williams' Lifetime and Elvin Jones on the album Speak No Evil. He also draws upon influences from free jazz drummer Milford Graves, whom he has described as having melodic, lyrical, and linguistic drumming that sounds like a language and is non-metric in its approach to rhythm. Like his grandfather Roy Haynes, Gilmore draws upon these diverse influences.

awards and recognition

Gilmore won a Grammy Award for Best Latin Jazz Album for Antidote as part of Chick Corea's band. He was named one of the "25 for the Future" by DownBeat magazine in 2016. His debut album as a bandleader, Journey to the New: Live at the Village Vanguard, reached the number one position on The New York Times Best Jazz Albums of 2025 list. At the 2026 Grammy Awards, he won a Grammy for his performance on Sullivan Fortner's album Southern Nights in the Best Jazz Instrumental Album category.

collaborations and discography

Gilmore has performed or recorded with numerous artists including Chick Corea, Pat Metheny, Pharoah Sanders, Mulatu Astatke, Norah Jones, Robert Glasper, Thundercat, Vijay Iyer, and Taylor McFerrin. As a leader or co-leader, he has released albums such as For Loved Ones (2023), Refract (2023), Journey to the New: Live at the New York Times Best Jazz Albums of 2025 list (2025), Trio of Bloom (2025), Purpose (2026), and Shards (2026). As a sideman, he has recorded with artists such as Vijay Iyer, Steve Coleman, Chick Corea, Sullivan Fortner, Graham Haynes, Gilad Hekselman, Ambrose Akinmusire, Chris Potter, Mark Turner, Lionel Loueke, Gonzalo Rubalcaba, and Joe Martin. He was part of the band Side-Eye in 2019 for a live album recorded in New York City on September 11 and 12, 2019, released as Side-Eye NYC (V1.IV) by Pat Metheny.
