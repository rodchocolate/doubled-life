Jason Bonham is an English drummer and the son of late Led Zeppelin drummer John Bonham. He has performed with the surviving members of Led Zeppelin on several occasions and has worked as a session musician.

early career and solo projects

Bonham began playing drums at age five and appeared in the film The Song Remains the Same with his father. At 15, he joined his first band, Airrace, followed by Virginia Wolf in 1985. In 1988, he worked with Jimmy Page for the Outrider album and tour. He also formed his own band, Bonham, which released The Disregard of Timekeeping and Mad Hatter before dissolving. Later, he recorded a project called In the Name of My Father - The Zepset, and albums with his aunt Debbie Bonham.
