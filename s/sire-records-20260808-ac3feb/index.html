Sire Records is an American record label owned by Warner Music Group and distributed by Warner Records. It was founded in 1966 as Sire Productions by Seymour Stein and Richard Gottehrer.

history

Founded in 1966, the label initially focused on introducing underground, progressive British bands to the American market. Early releases included the Deviants, Tomorrow, Barclay James Harvest and Climax Blues Band. Distribution was handled by London Records in 1968, followed by Polydor Records in 1970-1971, and Famous Music from 1972-1974. In Europe, distribution was distribution was handled by Phonogram Inc. from 1974 to 1977. During the 1970s, the label released compilation albums such as the 'History Of British Rock' series and acts like the Flamin Groovies and Renaissance.

warner acquisition and evolution

In the mid-1970s, Sire signed artists from the punk rock and new wave scenes, such as Talking Heads and the Ramones. In 1977, it returned to major label distribution via Warner Bros. Records, which eventually purchased the label outright. As a subsidiary of Warner Bros., Sire found success with acts like Depeche Mode, The Smiths, Madonna, and the Pretenders. During the late 1980s and early 1990s, the roster included Seal, Ice-T and k.d. lang. In 1994, distribution switched to Elektra Records, before Sire became a stand-alone label in 1997. In 2000, it merged with the US division of London Records to become London-Sire Records, a partnership that was dissolved in April 2003, after which the label returned to distribution with Warner Bros. Records.

recent developments

In July 2017, Rani Hancock was named president of Sire Records, with co-founder Seymour Stein remaining as chairman. In July 2018, Stein departed from the label after 51 years involved with Sire or its parent company, Warner Music Group.
