Madlib is an American DJ, record producer, and rapper known for his eclectic, sample-heavy production style. He is regarded as one of the most influential producers in modern hip-hop.

signature techniques & sound

Madlib's production is characterized by a sample-heavy approach that often incorporates elements of jazz and world music, including sounds from Brazilian funk, psychedelic, prog-rock, and African records of the early 1970s. His work is not fully quantized, giving it a more human feel where the tempo may slow down or speed up. He has utilized the Boss SP-303 and a turntable for projects like Madvillainy, and his early work as The Loop Digga was created using a sampler in his Crate Diggas Palace (CDP) studio.

landmark records

Madlib's canon includes the critically acclaimed album Madvillainy (2004) with MF Doom, produced using a Boss SP-303 and a turntable. He produced the entirety of Freddie Gibbs' Piñata (2014) and Bandana (2019), both of which received widespread critical acclaim. Other notable works include his solo work as Quasimoto in The Unseen (2000) and the remixes of Blue Note Records archive in Shades of Blue (2003).

collaborators & lineage

Madlib's frequent collaborators include MF Doom (as Madvillain), J Dilla (as Jaylib), and Freddie Gibbs (as MadGibbs). He was part of the hip hop collective Lootpack and the jazz-influenced group Yesterdays New Quintet. His lineage is connected to musicians, with his father Otis Jackson Sr. and mother Dora Sinesca Jackson being musicians, musician brother Oh No, and jazz trumpeter uncle Jon Faddis.

canonical status

Madlib is considered one of the most influential producers in modern hip-hop due to his eclectic production style and more human, non-quantized rhythms. He has worked with a variety of artists across genres, and others such as Kanye West have expressed a desire for more of his beats.
