Madlib is an American DJ, record producer, and rapper known for his eclectic, sample-heavy production style. He is regarded as one of the most influential producers in modern hip-hop, frequently incorporating elements of jazz and world music into his work.

signature techniques & sound

Madlib's production is characterized by a sample-heavy, eclectic style that often incorporates jazz and world music. He avoids full quantization, meaning his beats have a 'human feel' that may slow down or speed up, as opposed to being perfectly timed to a grid. To achieve this specific sound, he produced the critically acclaimed album Madvillainy using a turntable and a Boss SP-303. He also created the fictional rapper Quasimoto by pitching up his own voice.

landmark records

Madlib's canon consists of several landmark records. In 2004, he collaborated with MF Doom up as Madvillain to release Madvillainy, a highly anticipated and well-received album that topped many critics' year-end lists. Under his alter ego Quasimoto, he released The Unseen (2000), which was named by Spin as one of the top 20 albums of the year. He also produced the entirety of Freddie Gibbs' Piñata (2014) and Bandana (2019), both of which received widespread critical acclaim. Additionally, he formed the jazz-influenced group Yesterdays New Quintet, through which he released a series of releases on Stones Throw Records.

collaborators & lineage

Madlib's lineage is rooted in in his early work with the hip hop collective Lootpack and the Crate Diggas Palace (CDP) studio, working with artists like Oh No, Kankick, and Dudley Perkins. He has formed high-profile collaborations with J Dilla (as Jaylib), MF Doom (as Madvillain), and Freddie Gibbs (as MadGibbs). Other notable collaborators include Talib Kweli, Erykah Badu, and De La Soul. He also worked with the recording sessions for Kanye West's My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy, though some of his beats were put on hold.

canonical status

Madlib is considered canonical due to his influence on modern hip-hop production and his own record label, Madlib Invazion. He was invited to remix tracks from the Blue Note Records archive in 2003, which he release as Shades of Blue. He has also produced multiple-volume album series such as Beat Konducta, Mind Fusion, and the Madlib Medicine Show. He also composed the film score for the A Tribe Called Quest documentary film Beats, Rhymes & Life: The Travels of A Tribe Called Quest.

facts

  • Era: Early 1990s to present
  • Genres: Hip hop, jazz, electronic, house/techno
  • Notable Productions: Madvillainy, Piñata, Bandana, The Unseen
  • Signature Method/Gear: Boss SP-303, turntable, non-quantized beats, pitching up vocals
