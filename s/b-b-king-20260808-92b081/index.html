B. B. King (1925–2015) was an American blues guitarist, singer, songwriter, and record producer known as "The King of the Blues". He introduced a sophisticated soloing style characterized by shimmering vibrato, staccato picking, and fluid string bending that influenced many later electric guitar blues players. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1987.
