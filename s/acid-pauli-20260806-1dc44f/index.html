Acid Pauli is a musical project of Martin Gretschmann, a German electronic music artist. He is also known as Console, and was the former lead programmer for the band The Notwist.

origins and style

Martin Gretschmann began performing as Acid Pauli around the turn of the century. He describes his style as being "somewhere between ambient and techno". The name 'Acid Pauli' was coined by a friend who shouted it at an illegal party in Munich approximately 20 years ago; Gretschmann states that he was playing with only a laptop using Rebirth software, which emulates acid machines like the TR909, 808, and Roland TB-303, resulting in a sound that sounded like acid house.

career and collaborations

As Acid Pauli, Gretschmann has composed film music for the movie Es war einmal Indianerland (2017). He has collaborated with other electronic acts, including Monolink. Together they released the single "The End" in 2015. With Monolink, he is also a member of the Berlin electronica scene. He has performed at the same annual gathering for art, culture, music, and nature in Thailand called Wonderfruit.

connection to console

Acid Pauli is another label for Martin Gretschmann, who also records as Console. As Console, he started publishing electronic pieces in 1995 via the indie label Kollaps on the sampler The Day My Favorite Insect Died. He released the debut album Pan Or Ama in 1997 and the single 14 Zero Zero in 1999. He also worked with radio play author Andreas Ammer to create pieces such as Spaceman '85 and On the Tracks, and provided a piece for Björk's album Vespertine upon her request.
