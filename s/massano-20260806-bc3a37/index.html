Pedro Massano, also known as Mané, was a Portuguese journalist, author, illustrator, Illustrator, critic, and comic book promoter. He was born in Lisbon on 15 August 1948 and died on 1 September 2025.

early life and education

Massano attended the Architecture course at ESBAL (Lisbon School of Fine Arts) during the 1970s.

career in comics

Massano's first published comic was "BZZZ", which appeared in the supplement Mosca, in Diário de Lisboa, in 1972. He signed his work as Mané, though he also used the signatures Pedro or Manuel Pedro. He published a number of comic strips across various publications, including Selecções BD, Eco Regional and Visão.
