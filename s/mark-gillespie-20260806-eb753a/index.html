english professional footballer

Mark Joseph Gillespie, born March 27, 1992, is an English professional footballer who serves as a goalkeeper for the Premier League club Newcastle United. He began his career in the academy setup at Newcastle United before moving to Carlisle own United in 2008 at age 16. After making his professional debut on May 8, 2010, against Norwich City, he became the youngest keeper ever to represent Carlisle, beating a record set by Tony Caig. He spent several seasons with Carlisle United, including a loan spell at Blyth Spartans in 2010-11, month-long loan, and later moved to Walsall in League One on a two-year contract in June 2017. In June 2018, he signed for Scottish Premiership club Motherwell, where he was ever-present that season and saved penalties on four occasions. He returned to Newcastle United in July 2020 on a three-year contract, and has since extended his contract multiple times, with extensions signed on May 30, 2024, June 6, 2025, and June 3, 2026.

british businessman and talent manager

Mark Gillespie is a British businessman and talent manager from Solihull, United Kingdom. He career began at the Birmingham superclub Godskitchen, where he worked as a street promoter, web developer, and digital support, eventually taking over talent booking for the venue. He discovered Scottish DJ and producer Calvin Harris on Myspace while working as the same as a talent booker for the Global Gathering festival. He signed Harris as his first client in 2007, the year Harris released his debut album 'I Created Disco'. In 2020, Gillespie own brokered the sale of Harris's publishing catalogue to Vine Alternative Investments for a reported US$100 million.
