A State of Trance is a Dutch trance and progressive radio show and event brand founded by Armin van Buuren. The program is typically broadcast weekly as a two-hour mix featuring various genres including vocal trance, uplifting trance, progressive house, and progressive trance.

overview and format

The show is hosted by Armin van Buuren with co-host Ruben de Ronde; Ferry Corsten has also served as a monthly resident and presenter. The broadcast format usually consists of a two-hour DJ mix focusing on new trance and progressive music, including promotional tracks, commercial releases, and unreleased material. Regular features include 'Future Favourite', 'Trending Track', 'Progressive Pick', 'Tune of the Week' and 'Service for Dreamers'. Currently, it is available via YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, VK, SiriusXM's 'A State of Armin' channel, and websites such as DI.FM. Since 2017, it has been broadcast as a video livestream from a studio in Amsterdam, with guest DJs appearing during the second hour starting in 2019.

history and growth

A State of Trance launched on June 1, 2001, with Episode 001 broadcast through ID&T Radio. The first three episodes were titled 'Into Trance' before the show adopted the name 'A State Of Trance' from Episode 004 onwards. After being unexpectedly canceled by ID&T Radio after episode 182, the show moved to ETN.fm and then to DI.FM starting with Episode 185. To better accommodate an international audience, van Buuren transitioned from presenting in Dutch to English. Since the 500th episode, the show's annual celebrations have replaced Trance Energy as the main trance event in the Netherlands.

record label and legacy

Launched in 2003 as one of the first imprints of Armada Music, the A State of Trance record label focuses primarily on progressive trance and trance releases. Its first vinyl release was Questia's 'Nexus Asia' in June 2003. The imprint functions as part of a wider brand that includes live events, compilation series and the weekly radio show. Other DJs have created similar radio programs such as Andrew Rayel's Find Your Harmony, Aly & Fila's Future Sound of Egypt, Markus Schulz's Global DJ Broadcast and Above & Beyond's Group Therapy.

milestones

In January 2021, the show reached its 1000th episode. This milestone was preceded by a seven-day livestream called A State of Trance Top 1000, which featured a fan-voted countdown of the most popular records played on ASOT. The top three tracks were 'Shivers' by Armin van Buuren featuring Susana, Tiësto's 'Adagio for Strings', and Gaia's 'Tuvan'. Following this, van Buuren released the collaborative album A State of Trance Forever in September 2021.
