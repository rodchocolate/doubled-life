Jimi Hendrix was an American musician who performed live at venues such as the Winterland Ballroom and the Fillmore East. He recorded several live albums and box sets, including Songs For Groovy Children: The Fillmore East Concerts and Winterland.

fillmore east performances

Hendrix performed four sets of performances over two nights at the Fillmore East auditorium in New York City on New Year's Eve 1969 and New Year's Day 1970. These recordings were supervised by sound engineer Wally Heider, who had also recorded Hendrix live at Woodstock and the Monterey Pop Festival in 1967. To settle a contract dispute with Capitol Records, Hendrix agreed to deliver an album, which led to the recording of these shows. To meet this obligation, the set lists contained mostly new songs, including debuts of 'Power of Soul', 'Machine Gun', 'Ezy Ryder', 'Earth Blues', 'Burning Desire', and 'Who Knows'. He performed with drummer Buddy Miles and bassist Billy Cox. He also played popular Experience numbers such as 'Stone Free', 'foxey Lady', 'Voodoo Child (Slight Return)', and 'Purple Haze' for encores.

winterland ballroom performances

The Jimi Hendrix Experience, Jimi Hendrix's band, performed six performances at the Winterland Ballroom in San Francisco, California between October 10 and 12, 1968. These performances were documented in a posthumous live box set titled Winterland, released on September 13, 2011. The collection includes contributions from Noel Redding (bass guitar, backing vocals), Mitch Mitchell (drums), and guest musicians suchland as Jack Casady (bass on 'Killing Floor' and 'Hey Joe') and Virgil Gonsalves (flute on 'Are You Experienced?').

title releases

Several live recordings of Hendrix's work have been documented. The box set Songs For Groovy Children: The Fillmore East Concerts, released in 2019, contains 43 songs from the 1969-1970 New Year's performances at the Fillmore East. This collection includes previously unreleased recordings and longer versions of some tracks.
