Kevin Maurice Saunderson is an American electronic dance music DJ and record producer born on September 5, 1964. He is a member of the Belleville Three, a trio of high school friends who are credited as pioneers and originators of Detroit techno.

early life and education

Born in Brooklyn, New York, Saunderson moved to Belleville, Michigan, around age 10. While attending Belleville High School, he befriended Derrick May and Juan Atkins. During this time, the trio bonded over an eclectic mix of music including Kraftwerk, Prince, Depeche Mode, and Yellow Magic Orchestra. Saunderson initially pursued other goals before becoming a serious musician; he played football and studied telecommunications at Eastern Michigan University.

musical career and projects

Saunderson's early work included the track "Triangle of Love," released under the pseudonym Kreem on Juan Atkins' label, Metroplex. He later formed Inner City with vocalist Paris Grey, a collaboration that occurred "by accident" after Terry ‘Housemaster’ Baldwin suggested Grey. Inner City achieved worldwide success with hits like "Big Fun" and "Good Life," recording three albums for Virgin/EMI Records and two singles for Columbia/SME Records. In 1998, he released the more underground music of the E-Dancer project in the album 'Heavenly'. He also developed his own record label, KMS.

influence and artistry

John Bush of AllMusic described Saunderson's style as an energetic, groundbreaking style for techno characterized by a dense rhythmic assault of sound samples and heavy percussion. He also noted that thendersons' contributions to the genre are some of the hardest and most mechanistic techno from Detroit. Additionally, the bassline of his 1988 track "Just Want Another Chance" (released under the pseudonym Reese) was highly influential in the jungle and drum and bass genres, using a Casio CZ-1000 for its performance.
