Sven Väth is a German electronic music producer and DJ who has had a career spanning over 40 years. He is a three-time DJ Awards winner known as "Papa Sven" by his fans.

early life and career beginnings

Väth's parents, who escaped from East Germany, met in a West German town near Frankfurt. He was introduced to music through his parents' English pub, which had a small dance floor where he played rock'n'roll and disco. In 1980, using job seeker's allowance money, he hitchhiked to Ibiza for three months, which inspired him to become a DJ. At age 17, he began DJing at his parents' pub in Germany upon returning from Ibiza. By 1982, he had secured a residency at the club Dorian Gray in Frankfurt am Main, where he met Michael Münzing and Luca Anzilotti.

music production and pop success

In 1985, Väth joined forces with Michael Münzing and Luca Anzilotti as Off (Organisation for Fun), and produced the track "Bad News". In 1986, the group released the hit single "Electrica Salsa", which sold one million copies and turned Väth into a pop sensation at age 22. Following this, Off released a second album, "Ask Yourself" in 1989, and their final single, "Move Your Body" in 1990.

club ownership and record labels

In 1988, Väth opened the dance club Omen in Frankfurt with Michael Münzing and Mattias Martinsohn; the venue is regarded as one of the birthplaces of techno in Germany. He founded Eye Q in 1991 with Heinz Roth and Matthias Hoffmann, and Harthouse Records in 1992, where he recorded under the alias Barbarella. He later signed a three-album contract with Virgin Records in 1998, releasing Fusion, Contact, and Fire.

cocoon and ibiza influence

Cocoon began as a series of parties in 1996, eventually evolving into a booking agency, record label,_and event management branch. The Cocoon Recordings label served as a platform for aspiring producers to release music without major labels. In 1999, Väth hosted Monday night parties at Amnesia in Ibiza for 18 years, holding his own same-day residency up until the following thirteen years after 2000. He is recognized for his significant influence on the underground electronic music scene in both Germany and Germany's pop stars in the 1990s, as well as in Ibiza.
