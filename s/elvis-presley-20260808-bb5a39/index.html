Elvis Presley was an American singer and actor known as the "King of Rock and Roll." He is regarded as one of the most culturally significant figures of the 20th century. His performance style was energetic and sexually provocative, blending influences across color lines.

early life and musical beginnings

Born in Tupelo, Mississippi, on January 8, 1935, Presley own his first guitar for his birthday after placing fifth in a singing contest at the Mississippi–Alabama Fair and Dairy Show when he was 10. He moved to Memphis, Tennessee, at age 13. In 1954, he began his music career at Sun Records with producer Sam Phillips, who aimed to bring African-American music to a wider audience. Presley became a pioneer of rockabilly, a fusion of country music and rhythm and blues. He was accompanied by guitarist Scotty Moore and bassist Bill Black. Later, drummer D. J. Fontana joined the quartet. In 1955, RCA Victor acquired his contract in a deal arranged by Colonel Tom Parker.

career highlights and commercial success

Presley's first RCA Victor single, "Heartbreak Hotel," became a number-one hit in the US in January 1956. Within a year, RCA Victor sold ten million Presley singles. He became the leading figure of rock and roll, though his style was seen as a threat to the moral well-being of white American youth. In November 1956, he made his film debut in Love Me Tender. After being drafted into military service in 1958, he relaunched his recording career two years later. He spent much of the 1960s making Hollywood films and soundtrack albums, including Jailhouse Rock (1957), Blue Hawaii (1961), and Viva Las Vegas (1964). In 1968, he returned to the stage in an NBC television comeback special, leading to a Las Vegas residency and profitable tours. In 1973, he gave the first solo artist concert broadcast worldwide, Aloha from Hawaii.

recording achievements and records

Presley is one of the best-selling music artists in history, with an estimated 500 million records sold worldwide. He was successful across various genres including pop, country, rock and roll, rockabilly, rhythm and blues, adult contemporary, and gospel. He holds several records, including the most RIAA-certified gold and platinum albums, the most albums charted on the Billboard 200, and the most number-one albums by a solo artist on the UK Albums Chart. His album Elvis' Christmas Album is the biggest selling holiday album of all time worldwide, selling over 20 million copies. He won three Grammy Awards and received the Grammy Lifetime Achievement Award at age 36.
