Moving Shadow is an English record label specializing in breakbeat hardcore, jungle, and drum and bass music. It was founded in 1990 by Rob Playford.

history

The label began in 1990 operating from the home of Rob Playford in Stevenage, where he became a focal point for local bedroom producers and young artists. The first release with a 'Shadow' prefixed catalogue number was Earth Leakage Trip's Psychotronic EP in 1991. Early releases featured collaborations and aliases of Playford, including the group 2 Bad Mice, which he formed with Simon Colebrooke and Sean O'Keeffe. During 1991 and 1992, Moving Shadow enjoyed UK Singles Chart success with tracks such as 'Bombscare' by 2 Bad Mice and 'Music Takes You' by Blame. In 1994, Playford began working with Goldie (using the Rufige Kru alias), resulting in the album Timeless, produced and engineered by Playford and released via FFRR Records in 1995. The label stopped releasing new artist material in 2006, though a digital-only release of Omni Trio's 'Cut Out Shapes' was put out in 2012. In 2023, the label expanded its digital sales platforms to include its own YouTube channel and other streaming services, making the entire back catalogue accessible on Spotify as of May 2023.

special release series

Moving Shadow produced several specialized release series. The 'Two on One' series (1994) consisted of nine 12 inch vinyl releases where each record featured a guest artist not signed to the label on one side and a Moving Shadow artist on the other; the artwork for these nine releases was split to create one large 3’ x 3' image. The '10  ' series (2000-2001) was a limited edition retrospective of popular tracks, and the 'MSXEP' series (2000-2006) featured double 12 inch vinyl releases with both new and existing label artists.

associated artists

Moving Shadow released albums from Foul Play and Omni Trio in 1995, and published regional compilation albums such as Trans-Central Connection, Storm from the East, and The Revolutionary Generation. The artist Deep Blue (alias of Sean O'Keeffe) recorded for Moving Shadow before leaving in 1997 to form Partisan Recordings. Artist Blame also had chart success on the label.
