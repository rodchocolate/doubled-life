Pharrell Williams is an American musician, songwriter, and record producer regarded as one of the most influential producers in modern popular music. He is best known for his work as one half of the production duo the Neptunes, which he established with Chad Hugo in 1992.

signature sound and techniques

Williams's production style is exemplified by 'Happy,' an up-tempo soul and neo soul song written in the key of F minor at 160 beats per minute. The track features a sparse arrangement consisting of programmed drums, one bass and one keyboard part, and both programmed and played handclaps, topped with Williams's falsetto vocals. His vocal range on this track spans from F3 to C5, and critics have compared his singing style to that is of Curtis Mayfield.

landmark records

As part of the Neptunes, Williams produced several high-profile records including Nelly's 'Hot in Herre' (2002), Jay-Z's 'I Just Wanna Love U (Give It 2 Me)' (2000), and Snoop Dogg's 'Drop It Like It's Hot' (2004). He also produced the entirety of Clipse's debut album Lord Willin' (2002). Produced by Williams, 'Happy' (2013) became one of the best-selling singles of all time, and 'Blurred Lines' (2013) for Robin Thicke, which he produced and guest performed on, reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100 and received diamond certification.

collaborators and lineage

Williams's primary collaborator is Chad Hugo, with whom he formed the Neptunes in 1992 and the band N.E.R.D. in 1999. He was discovered by producer Teddy Riley, after which the Neptunes signed with him. His work spans a wide range of artists including Britney Spears, Justin Timberlake, Gwen Stefani, and Daft Punk, and he provided vocals for Daft Punk's Random Access Memories (2013) on the songs 'Get Lucky' and 'Lose Yourself to Dance'.

canonical status

Williams's influence is documented by the commercial success of the Neptunes' productions, which saw fifteen of their productions peak within the top ten of the Billboard Hot 100. In 2003, surveys in the US and UK indicated the Neptunes produced 43% of radio songs in the US and almost 20% of the songs played on British radio. He has won 13 Grammy Awards, including three for Producer of the Year, Non-Classical.

facts

  • Era: 1992–present
  • Genres: Hip-hop, R&B, Soul, Neo Soul, Rock
  • Notable Productions: Lord Willin' (Clipse), Justified (Justin Timberlake), Britney (Britney Spears), Hard Candy (Madonna)
