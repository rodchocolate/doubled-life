Pharrell Williams is an American musician, songwriter, and record producer regarded as one of the most influential producers in modern popular music. He is best known for his work as one half of the production duo the Neptunes, which he established in 1992 with Chad Hugo.

signature sound and techniques

Williams's production style is exemplified by the song 'Happy', an up-tempo soul and neo soul track written and produced by Williams. The track features a sparse arrangement by modern standards, consisting of programmed drums, one keyboard part, one bass, and both programmed and played handclaps, topped with Williams's lead vocals and backing vocals. His vocal range on this track spans from F3 to C5, utilizing a falsetto voice that critics have compared to Curtis Mayfield.

landmark records

As part of the Neptunes, Williams produced several high-impact records including Nelly's 'Hot in Herre' (2002), Jay-Z's 'I Just Wanna Love U (Give It 2 Me)' (2000), and Britney Spears's 'I'm a Slave 4 U' (2001). He also produced the same year Justin Timberlake's debut album Justified (2002), songs such as 'Señorita', 'Like I Love You', and 'Rock Your Body'. Other landmark productions include Snoop Dogg's 'Drop It Like It's Hot' (2004) and Gwen Stefani's 'Hollaback Girl' (2005). Additionally, Williams wrote and produced the 'Happy' (2013) single for the公民 Despicable Me 2 soundtrack, which became one of the best-selling singles of all time.

collaborators and lineage

Williams's primary collaborator is Chad Hugo, with whom he formed the Neptunes in 1992 and the band N.E.R.D. in 1999. He was discovered by producer Teddy Riley, after which the Neptunes signed with Riley.

canonical status

Williams is considered canonical due to his massive commercial success and industry influence. Fifteen of the Neptunes' productions have peaked in the top ten of the Billboard Hot 100, and three of his songs have peaked atop the chart. He has won 13 Grammy Awards, including three for Producer of the Year, Non-Classical. He was also a producer of the film Hidden Figures (2017) and has had a significant impact on radio airplay, with a 2003 survey finding the Neptunes produced 43% of radio songs in the U.S. and nearly 20% of British radio.
