Nine Inch Nails (NIN) is an American industrial rock band formed by singer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and producer Trent Reznor in 1988 in Cleveland, Ohio. Reznor was the sole permanent member until Atticus Ross became an official member in 2016. The band has sold over 20 million records worldwide.

formation and early career

Trent Reznor formed Nine Inch Nails in Cleveland, Ohio, after playing keyboards for the synth-pop band Exotic Birds. While working as an assistant engineer and janitor at Right Track Studios, studio owner Bart Koster gave him free access to record demos. Inspired by Prince, Reznor played all instruments except drums, which he programmed electronically. The band's first live performance occurred on October 27, 1988, in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. After signing with TVT Records, they released their debut album Pretty Hate Machine (1989), which became one of the first independently released records to attain platinum certification.

discography and evolution

The band's discography includes 12 studio albums, one live album, five remix albums, and six extended plays. Following a debut with TVT Records, the band signed with Interscope Records to release the EP Broken (1992), the albums The Downward Spiral (1994) and The Fragile (1999). After a hiatus, they released With Teeth (2005) and Year Zero (2007). Following a feud with Interscope, the band began releasing music independently, including Ghosts I–IV (2008) and The Slip (2008). Later releases include Hesitation Marks (2013), the trilogy consisting of Not the Actual Events (2016), Add Violence (2017), and Bad Witch (2018), as well as Ghosts V: Together and Ghosts VI: Locusts (2020).

// a bit more on the live band and awards

When touring, Reznor typically assembles a live band. The current lineup consists of Reznor and Ross alongside Robin Finck, Stu Brooks, and Josh Freese. Their concerts are known for elaborate lighting, complex special effects and thematic visual elements. Nine Inch Nails has been nominated for 13 Grammy Awards, winning for the songs "Wish", "Happiness in Slavery" and "As Alive as You Need Me to Be". They were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2020.
