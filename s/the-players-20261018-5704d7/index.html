The Players refers to various entities across film, music, organizations, and sports. It is also associated with several films and television series titled "Players" or "The Players Club."

entertainment and media

There are multiple films titled The Players, including a 2012 French film (Les Infidèles), a 2020 Italian film (Gli infedeli), and a 2025 Canadian drama by Sarah Galea-Davis. Additionally, there are several productions titled "Players," such as a 1979 film starring Ali MacGraw, a 2012 Bollywood film, a 2024 American romantic comedy, and a 1997–1998 NBC crime drama. Television programs include a 2002–2004 G4 video game-related show, a 2010 Spike sitcom, and a 2022 Paramount+ mockumentary series. Other media includes the novel "Players" by Don DeLillo (1977) and a novel by Terrance Dicks based on Doctor Who, as well as an album by Too $hort.

organizations and clubs

The Players is the name of several organizations, including amateur actor's clubs in Detroit, Michigan, and a historic actors' club in New York City founded by Edwin Booth. The New York City club is also described as a private social club located in Gramercy Park, Manhattan. Other entities include the Players' Theatre in London and the Players' League, a 19th-century American baseball league.

the players club film

The Players Club is a 1998 American comedy-drama film written and directed by Ice Cube in his directorial debut. The plot follows Diana "Diamond" Armstrong, a college student and aspiring journalist who works as a stripper at the Players Club to pay for her tuition. The film's story involves conflict with the club owner Dollar Bill, a loan shark named St. Louis, and personal struggles involving Diamond's relationship with her boyfriend Lance and her cousin Ebony.
