Georges Polti is a French author who proposed a descriptive list of thirty-six dramatic situations in 1895 to categorize every possible dramatic situation that might occur in a performance or story.

the thirty-six dramatic situations

In 1895, Polti analyzed classical Greek texts as well as contemporaneous and classical French works and a handful of non-French authors to create his list. He claimed to be continuing the work of Carlo Gozzi (1720–1806), who had also identified 36 situations. The resulting list was published in a book of the same name, which contains examples and extended explanations for each situation.

influence and usage

The list has been popularized as an aid for writers, storytellers, and dramatists. It influenced George Pierce Baker, the author of Dramatic Technique, and Christina Stead. The 36 situations have been critiqued by some as being concatenations of events rather than minimal or isolable motifs.
