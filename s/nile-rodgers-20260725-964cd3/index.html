Nile Rodgers is an American guitarist and producer known for his influential 'chucking' guitar style. As a co-founder of Chic, he has written and produced records that have sold over 750 million albums and worldwide.

signature sound and techniques

Rodgers is recognized for his 'chucking' guitar style and a sound that fuses jazz, soul, and funk grooves with European-influenced melodies and lyrics. In his production work, he often centers the rhythm guitar; for example, on Daft Punk's 'Get Lucky', he created a guitar part that required the rest of the the rest of the production—including the bass part—to be re-played to fit his performance.

landmark records

Rodgers's career is marked by several canonical records. With Chic, he produced the self-titled debut 'Chic' (1977), the album 'C'est Chic' (1978) featuring the hit 'Le Freak', and 'Risqué' (1979) which contained 'Good Times'—a track that became one of the most-sampled songs of all time and influenced hits like Queen's 'Another One Bites the Dust'. He also produced David Bowie's 'Let's Dance' (1983), Madonna's 'Like a Virgin' (1984), and contributed to Daft Punk's 'Random Access Memories' (2013), winning three Grammys for the project.

collaborators and lineage

His most significant early partnership was with bassist Bernard Edwards, with whom he co-founded the band Chic and produced for artists such as Sister Sledge ('We Are Family', 1979) and Diana Ross ('Diana', 1980). Following the breakup of Chic, he worked with a variety of artists including Duran Duran, INXS, and more recently, Beyoncé (on the album 'Renaissance') and Daft Punk.

canonical status

Rodgers is a Songwriters Hall of Fame inductee and has received the Grammy Lifetime Achievement/Special Merit Award. He is cited as a 'massively influential' innovator of the guitar, with Rolling Stone placing him 7th on their list of the 250 greatest guitarists of all time.
