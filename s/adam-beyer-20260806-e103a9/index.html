Adam Beyer is a Swedish electronic dance music producer and DJ. He is the founder of Drumcode Records and was one of several Swedish EDM artists to emerge in the mid-1990s.

musical career and style

Beyer began his musical journey in the techno scene, but over the last decade he has shifted towards playing more progressive EDM and Trance styles. He hosts a weekly radio show called Drumcode Live, which is one of the most listened to weekly EDM broadcasts in the world with an audience of 11 million.

discography

As an artist, Beyer has released four albums and various EPs. His studio albums include Decoded (1996), Recoded (1997), Protechtion (1999), and Ignition Key (2002), as well as Selected Works (1996-2000) and Fabric 22 (2005). He has also worked as a producer and remixer.

personal life

Beyer was married to fellow Swedish DJ Ida Engberg, with whom he has three daughters. They are now divorced.
