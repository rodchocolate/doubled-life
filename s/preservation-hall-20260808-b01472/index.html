Preservation Hall is a jazz venue located on St Peter Street in the French Quarter of New Orleans, Louisiana. It is associated with a house band, a record label, and a non-profit foundation. The venue receives approximately 180,000 visitors annually.

Preservation Hall is a jazz venue located on St Peter Street in the French Quarter of New Orleans, Louisiana. It is associated with a house band, a record label, and a non-profit foundation. The venue receives approximately 180,000 visitors annually.

organization and education

The Preservation Hall Foundation is a 501(c)(3) organization focused on educational initiatives. These include providing private lessons to youth taught by New Orleans jazz musicians, coordinating group lessons with the Preservation Hall Junior Jazz Band, and maintaining archives. The Foundation also provides a free online learning resource for music educators and students called the Preservation Hall Foundation Brass Bandbook, which includes songs such as "Bourbon Street Parade" and "Just a Closer Walk with Thee".

preservation hall jazz band

Founded by Allan Jaffe in the early 1960s, the Preservation Hall Jazz Band derives its name from the venue. The band has toured nationally and internationally, collaborating with various artists such as the Blind Boys of Alabama, Foo Fighters, and My Morning Jacket. In 2006, Benjamin Jaffe and Sandra Jaffe accepted the National Medal of Arts on behalf of the band. In 2025, the album For Fat Man, a collaboration between the Preservation Hall Jazz Band and Preservation Brass, was nominated for a Grammy Award for Best Regional Roots Music Album.
