Madison Square Garden (MSG) is a multi-purpose venue and associated corporate entities in New York City. It encompasses an arena and a theater located beneath the main arena that hosts smaller events.

Madison Square Garden (MSG) is a multi-purpose venue and associated corporate entities in New York City. It encompasses an arena and a theater located beneath the main arena that hosts smaller events.

the theater at madison square garden

The Infosys Theater at Madison Square Garden, formerly known as the Felt Forum, Paramount Theater, WaMu Theater, and Hulu Theater, is situated beneath the main arena. It seats between 2,000 and 5,600 people and is used for concerts, shows, sports, and meetings. The theater's stage is 30-by-64 feet, with a ceiling height of 20 feet at stage level and an 8,000-square-foot lobby. No seat is more than 177 feet from the stage.

corporate history and management

The Madison Square Garden Corporation was incorporated in 1923 by Tex Rickard to operate the third Madison Square Garden. In 1959, controlling interest was sold to Graham-Paige, which later merged with the corporation in 1960. Gulf and Western Industries acquired complete control of the corporation in 1977, becoming a wholly owned subsidiary. The company later became Paramount Communications in 1989. In 1994, Viacom purchased majority ownership but sold MSG to Cablevision and ITT Corporation. In 2010, Cablevision spun off the MSG properties into the Madison Square Garden Company. In 2020, the Madison Square Garden Company rebranded as Madison Square Garden Sports Corp. and spun off its entertainment division as Madison Square Garden Entertainment.

the second madison square garden

Operating from 1890 to 1926, the second Madison Square Garden was located at 26th Street and Madison Avenue in Manhattan. Designed by architect Stanford White, it featured a Beaux-Arts structure with a Moorish feel and a 32-story tower topped by a statue of Diana. The main hall was the largest in the world at the time, with permanent seating for 8,000 people. It hosted various events including boxing matches, orchestral performances, light operas, romantic comedies, and the 1924 Democratic National Convention.

events and programming

The theater at MSG has hosted a wide range of events, including Mike Tyson's fourteenth professional fight in 1985, boxing matches between Sergio Martínez and Matthew Macklin in 2012, Vasiliy Lomachenko and Guillermo Rigondeaux in 2017, and Teófimo López and George Kambosos Jr. in 2021. It has also hosted concerts by the Grateful Dead in 1971 and a memorial concert for Phil Ochs in 1976. Musical productions such as A Christmas Carol (1994-2003), The Wizard of Oz (1997-1999), Cinderella (2001) and Annie (2024-2025) with Whoopi Goldberg as Miss Hannigan. It also hosted the 1987 stand-up comedy movie Eddie Murphy Raw, filming of tracks for the Doors' album Absolutely Live in 1970, {// a bit more detail on the other events} and WWE's NXT show.

sports history

The second Madison Square Garden hosted the first indoor professional football games during the World Series of Football in 1902 and 1903. It also hosted the laurognan marathon in 1909, an indoor marathon by Matthew Maloney, and the Millrose Games starting in 1914. The venue also had a long history of boxing and professional wrestling, including George Hackenschmidt's victory over Tom Jenkins in 1905 for the World Heavyweight Championship.
