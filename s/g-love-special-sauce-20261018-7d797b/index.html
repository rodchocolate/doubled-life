G. Love & Special Sauce is an American rock band from Philadelphia known for a "sloppy" and "laid back" sound that blends blues, hip hop, rock, and soul. The group features frontman Garrett Dutton, better known as G. Love, along with drummer Chuck Treece and bassist Jim Prescott.

the debut album was certified gold after selling 500,000 copies and was included in the book 1001 albums you must hear before you die. the philadelphia inquirer described g. love's rapping style as "ragmop" and noted that his carefree playing refracts the blues into unlikely colors. the globe and mail concluded that while it is not profound, it is "good dumb fun."

The band's studio albums include G. Love and Special Sauce (1994), Coast to Coast Motel (1995), Yeah, It's That the Easy (1997), Philadelphonic (1999), and Electric Mile (2001). Later releases include Superhero Brother (2008), Sugar (2014), Love Saves the Day (2015), Philadelphia Mississippi (2022), and Ode To R.L. (2025). G. Love's solo studio albums are The Hustle, Lemonade, Fixin' to Die, and The Juice.
