Robyn Rihanna Fenty is a Barbadian singer, songwriter, businesswoman, and actress. She is an influential figure in popular culture known for her multifaceted career, artistic reinventions, and eclectic fashion style.

early life and education

Born on February 20, 1988, in Saint Michael, Barbados, Rihanna was the daughter of Monica, an Afro-Guyanese accountant, and Ronald Fenty, a former warehouse supervisor. She grew up in Bridgetown in a three-bedroom bungalow, where she helped her father sell clothes at a street stall. Her childhood was marked by her father's alcoholism and crack cocaine addiction, which strained her parents' marriage and led to her witnessing physical abuse of her mother. As a child, she suffered from intense headaches that required multiple CT scans. After her parents divorced when she was 14, her health improved. She attended Charles F. Broome Memorial Primary School and Combermere School, where she showed an interest in poetry, dancing, and singing. At age 11, she became a cadet in Barbados's Cadet Corps. She eventually dropped out of high school at age 16 to pursue music.

music career

Rihanna began her career by forming a music trio with classmates in Barbados. After auditioning for producer Evan Rogers, who was impressed by her, recorded demo tapes in Connecticut. In 2005, she signed with Syndicated Rhythm Productions and subsequently with Def Jam Recordings after an audition for Jay-Z and Antonio "L.A." Reid. She debuted with the Caribbean-inspired albums Music of the Sun (2005) and A Girl Like Me (2006). Following these occurred her best-selling album Good Girl Gone Bad (2007), and its reissue, Reloaded (2008). After exploring personal themes on Rated R (2009), she released dance-pop albums Loud (2010) and Talk That Talk (2011), and the synth-pop set Unapologetic (2012). Later, departing from Def Jam in favor of Roc Nation, she released the eclectic Anti (2016). Her music has resulted in 14 number-one singles on the Billboard Hot 100 and uma own's total record sales exceeding 250 million records.

business ventures and accolades

Rihanna is the first female musician to achieve billionaire status. She founded the nonprofit Clara Lionel Foundation, the cosmetics brand Fenty Beauty, and the fashion brand Fenty under LVMH. In 2018, she was named an ambassador by the Government of Barbados, and in 2021, she was declared a National Hero of Barbados. She has received numerous accolades, including nine Grammy Awards, twelve Billboard Music Awards, thirteen American Music Awards, and seven MTV Video Music Awards.
