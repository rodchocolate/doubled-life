Weather Report was an American jazz fusion band active from 1970 to 1986. The group was founded in 1970 by Joe Zawinul and Wayne Shorter, along with Miroslav Vitouå, Don Alias, Barbara Burton, Alphonse Mouzon, and others.

musical style and approach

Weather Report abandoned the traditional 'soloist/accompaniment' demarcation of straight-ahead jazz, instead featuring opportunities for continuous improvisation by every member. While their work was categorized as 'jazz fusion', the band members generally distanced themselves from that term. Their sound evolved from free improvising with avant-garde and experimental electronic leanings to a more groove-oriented approach incorporating elements of R&B, funk, rock, art music, and ethnic music. This shift was steered by Joe Zawinul, who utilized latest developments in synthesizer technology and a variety of tone colors to make the band stand out. Wayne Shorter's role was an economical 'listening' style of saxophone playing, often responding to other members' improvisations rather than taking the lead.

history and discography

The band's career spanned sixteen years, producing fourteen studio albums, three live albums, eleven compilation albums, five singles, and six video albums. Their self-titled debut album in 1971 was considered a classic of early fusion and won the Down Beat 'Album of the Year' award. Other successful recordings including Mysterious Traveller (1974), Tale Spinnin' (1975), and Heavy Weather (1977), also won this same award five times in a row. The album Mr. Gone (1978) reached No. 1 on the Billboard Jazz Albums chart, and the double live album 8:30 (1979) won a Grammy Award for Best Jazz Fusion Performance. Later albums such as Procession (1983) and This Is This! (1986) marked their final recordings. The band was dissolved by Joe Zawinul in 1987 after Wayne Shorter left in February 1986.

instrumentation and members

A quintet consisting of Zawinul, Shorter, a bassist, a drummer, and a percussionist was the standard formation. Throughout their history, prominent members included bassists Jaco Pastorius, Alphonso Johnson and Victor Bailey, drummers Peter Erskine and Omar Hakim, and percussionists Airto Moreira and Alex Acuña. Jaco Pastorius popularized the use of fretless bass guitar and melodic bass soloing in the band's music. For the first eight years, the group struggled to find a permanent drummer, until Peter Erskine was recruited in 1978 with the help of Jaco Pastorius.
