Matteo Giovanelli is an Italian DJ and producer who is one half of the electronic music duo Mathame. He was trained in classical music and also studied film, which led him to work as a film director.

music career

Matteo Giovanelli, along with his brother Amedeo Giovanelli, formed the duo Mathame. The pair began their career while living in Sicily at the base of Mt. Etna volcano, where they helped run their family's bed and breakfast. Their music is described as a combination of melodic house, dub techno, and electronic music, taking influence from anime, new technology, science-fiction, and classical movie soundtracks. Rooted in cinematic techno, cinematic techno, Mathame's live Audio Visual (AV) show is an immersive performance that utilizes AI programming for visuals and some other artwork. They tour globally, performing at events such as Tomorrowland, Ultra Miami Resistance, Coachella, and Sonus Festival. Their work has been remixed by ZHU, Adriatique, and Diplo.

album work

Mathame released their debut album, Memo, on June 30, 2023, via Astralwerks. Work on the album began in 2019 and continued through the COVID-19 pandemic. The thirteen track body of work serves as a travel log of memories and emotions over three years, focusing on moving from lockdown to being able to dance and then returning to lockdown. Themes within Memo include contrast, love, synthesis, darkness and light, and science fiction. Each track's four layers are intended to be imagined as characters in a story, and the narrative order of the tracklist pays homage to Joseph Campbell's monomyth concept, Hero's Journey. AI was used for the artwork of singles such as To Hope, Come for You, and the music video and artwork for So What.
