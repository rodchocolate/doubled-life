Nigeria 70 was a band renamed from Koola Lobitos during Fela Kuti's time in the United States.

formation and influence

Fela Kuti, a musician and political activist, formed the band Nigeria 70. While in Los Angeles for ten months in 1969, Kuti discovered the Black Power movement through Sandra Smith (also known as Sandra Izsadore or Sandra Akanke Isidore), a partisan of the Black Panther Party. This experience heavily influenced his music and political views, leading him to rename the band Nigeria 70.

recording sessions

Nigeria 70 performed a quick recording session in Los Angeles that would later be released as The '69 Los Angeles Sessions.

notes

The band's existence under the name Nigeria 70 was short-lived due to legal issues. The Immigration and Naturalization Service was tipped off by a promoter that Kuti and his band were in the United States without work permits.
