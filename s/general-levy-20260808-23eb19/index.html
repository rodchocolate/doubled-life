General Levy is an English ragga deejay who is frequently employed on studio tracks by drum and bass DJs. He is most recognized for his collaboration with M-Beat on the track "Incredible", which reached number eight on the UK singles chart in 1994.

early life and musical influence

Born on April 28, 1971, in Central Middlesex Hospital in Park Royal, London, General Levy is of Trinidadian descent. He spent his formative years in Wembley and Harlesden in the London Borough of Brent. Starting in 1981, he began collecting dancehall tapes, which influenced his musical development. At age 12, he started writing lyrics and formed his first sound system, Third Dimension, with friends two years later.

career and collaborations

Levy's early releases were with the independent label Fashion Records, who had a distribution deal with London Records for re-releases. He also recorded tracks with producers Robbo Ranx and Lloydy Crucial that were hits on the underground dancehall scene. Throughout his career, he has collaborated with various artists including R&B singer Mark Morrison, Madness singer Suggs (on the track "Girl" from the Breed album), 1988-1990s sound systems like Tippertone Sound and Java One Love, and dub producer Joe Ariwa. He also briefly worked with Rocco Barker of Flesh for Lulu in a band called The Space Police. In 2018, he collaborated with artists such as Spragga Benz, Fedde Le Grand and Chase & Status.

coutroversy and recognition

In 1994, General Levy was ostracized by the UK jungle scene due to mis-reported comments regarding his collaboration with M-Beat on "Incredible". He had claimed in an interview with The Face that he "run jungle at the moment". This controversy has since faded and he is now cited as one of the 25 UK MCs who changed the game.

works

Levy's discography includes albums such as The Wickeder General (1992), Wickedness Increase (1993), New Breed (1999) and Spirit & Faith (2008). He also collaborated on albums with Capleton, Joe Ariwa, and DJ Bonnot. He self-published an autobiography titled Incredible exploring his life and career to date on November 13, 2022.
