DJ EZ (pronounced "DJ ee zed") is a British house DJ from Tottenham, North London. He was one of the earliest proponents of UK garage music and hosted a long-running radio show on Kiss 100.

radio career

DJ EZ began his career DJing on the pirate radio station Dance 93FM as Easy O. After that station was shut down, he established his own short-lived North London station called Dimension FM. In 1994, he changed his name to EZ and joined Freek FM 101.8, where he hosted a 4-hour show on Saturday and Sunday mornings. He later moved to commercial radio at Kiss 100, where he had several shows including "Saturday Satisfaction", "The Hour of Power", "Destination Weekend", and "The Bassline Mix". His tenure at Kiss ended in 2014 after 14 years on air. Most recently, he founded a record label in 2020 with his manager and hosted the syndicated radio show Nuvolve Radio.

club career and awards

DJ EZ started his own weekly club night called Club Z at the Gass Club in Leicester Square. He has been associated with raves and nights such as Pure Silk, Garage Nation, Exposure, Frisky, Sun City, Twice As Nice, and Club Sidewinder. Over the years, he has won several awards, including 'Best DJ' at the 2000 and 2002 UK Garage Awards and 'Club DJ of the Year' at the 1998 Underground Garage Awards.

production and charity

DJ EZ is known for his remix of the B-15 Project track "Feels So Good" and for compiling and mixing the Pure Garage mixed compilation series. He has released several EPs, including the 4 Track E.P. (1998) and The Next Chapter EP (2002). He also performed a 24-hour charity set on February 27–28, 2016, with proceeds going to Cancer Research UK.
