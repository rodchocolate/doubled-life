Coldplay is a British rock band known for their prolific live performances. They have visited all continents except Antarctica and have headlined eight concert tours.

live performance history

The band's official debut was held at London's Laurel Tree in 1998, though drummer Will Champion and vocalist Chris Martin had busked in Covent Garden a year prior. Their early career included performances at Ronnie Scott's - Birmingham, where they held early concerts during 1999 and 2000. Following their debut album Parachutes (2000), the band embarked on the Parachutes Tour, their first concert tour, which primarily featured performances at clubs, theatres, and festivals. This tour grossed $4,201,871 from 262,140 tickets sold.

touring growth and financials

Coldplay expanded their reach to theatres, arenas, and stadiums during the Rush of Blood to the Head (2002–2003), Twisted Logic (2005–2007), and Viva la Vida (2008–2010) runs. In North America during the 2000s, they earned $142.9 million from 2.6 million tickets sold. The Mylo Xyloto (2011) tour introduced LED wristbands, which the group is credited with popularising. The Head Full of Dreams Tour (2016–2017) became one of the highest-grossing concert runs of all time, earning over $523 million from 5.38 million tickets sold.

// a bit more on recent tours

The Music of the Spheres World Tour (2022–2025) is the most-attended tour of all time and the first by a group to surpass $1 billion, generating over $1.52 billion from 13.1 million tickets sold in its initial 223 shows. This tour features ecological initiatives to reduce CO2 emissions by 50% compared to their previous run, positioning the band as pioneers for sustainable touring. Additionally, the band has performed at the Brit Awards eight times and appeared on Saturday Night Live as musical guests eight times, a record for international acts.
