Kool FM refers to several radio stations across different regions. These include a commercial classic hits station in Phoenix, Arizona, a former pirate radio station in London, and a private radio station in Malaysia.

kool-fm (phoenix)

KOOL-FM is a commercial classic hits station based in Phoenix, Arizona, owned by Audacy, Inc. It began programming oldies music in 1971, becoming the first radio station to carry that format. The station's history includes various ownership changes and ownership by Adams Radio Group, Chancellor, Infinity Broadcasting, and CBS Radio. In September 2022, it rebranded as Big 94.5 after 62 years of using its call letters for branding. Its HD Radio signal is multiplexed, with the HD2 subchannel currently simulcasting KWSS-LP.

kool fm (london)

Originally a pirate radio station that first broadcast on 28 November 1991 from Clapton, North East London, Kool FM is regarded as instrumental in the development of the jungle music scene. It has promoted events such as Jungle Fever and Kool Skool club nights. After operating as an online station known as Kool London from August 2010, it was relaunched as Kool FM on 23 April 2023 under the management of Rinse FM.

programming and formats

The Phoenix station's format has evolved from oldies to classic hits, focusing on hits of the 1970s, 1980s, and 1990s. The London station focuses on jungle, drum and bass, and old skool music. The Malaysian station's format has changed between music-focused programming and talk radio (Suara Semasa), before shifting to Malaysian adult contemporary music from the 90s to now.
