Cream is a music promotion trio consisting of Darren Hughes, James Barton and Andy Carroll. They originally hosted a weekly house music club night from 1992 to 2002 at the Nation nightclub in Liverpool.

club history and locations

Cream's home in Wolstenholme Square, Liverpool, was located at the now-demolished Nation nightclub (formerly Snobs Disco). The club night ran in this format from October 1992 to June 2002. In 2016, the site was demolished as part of an urban regeneration project; plans for a new home for the nightclub were later removed from the planning application. Additionally, Cream hosts a weekly Thursday night clubbing season event at Amnesia in Ibiza.

festivals and awards

Cream hosts the dance music festival Creamfields, which takes place every August in Daresbury, Cheshire. Creamfields has won the Music Week award for "Festival of the Year" in 2010 and a record six times the Best Dance Event at the UK Festival Awards. In 2012, Live Nation acquired the Creamfields Festival Brand on a contract basis.

music releases

The label has released DJ mix albums through various labels including their own, Deconstruction Records (1995–98), Virgin/EMI (1998–03), WEA (2004–05), Ministry of Sound (2006–11) and New State Recordings (2011–present). Notable releases include Paul Oakenfold's Goa Mix, which was described as "groundbreaking in its cinematic two-hour scope," and Cream Anthems 2001 (2000), which reached number 1 in the UK Compilation Chart. Paul Oakenfold's Creamfields (2004) was nominated for a Grammy Award for "Best Electronic/Dance Album" in 2005.
