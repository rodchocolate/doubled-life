timbaland

Timbaland (born Timothy Zachery Mosley) is an American record producer and rapper widely acclaimed for his distinctive production work and 'stuttering' rhythmic style. He is credited with blurring the distinction between hip-hop and R&B production by applying a fast-paced, clear-drum-break hip-hop sound to R&B artists. His influence on pop trends was such that Entertainment Weekly stated in 2007 that 'just about every current pop trend can be traced back to him.'

Timbaland's trademark sound is rooted in hip-hop, characterized by a fast-paced nature and clear drum breaks. He took this hip-hop foundation and applied it to R&B, effectively blurring the distinction between the two genres' production styles. His work is noted for incorporating 'avant-garde sounds into No. 1 hits.' In his later work with Missy Elliott (specifically *Under Construction*), he attempted to rebuild hip-hop production from the ground up by covering other people's songs and re-contextualizing sonic motifs, though critics noted this sometimes resulted in a formulaic approach.

Timbaland's first major production credit was Ginuwine's *Ginuwine... the Bachelor* (1996). His mainstream breakthrough came in 1996 with the majority of Aaliyah's *One in a Million*, featuring hits like 'If Your Girl Only Knew' and 'Pony.' In 1997, he fully produced Missy Elliott's debut *Supa Dupa Fly*, continuing his electronic production style. He scored a major hit in 1999 with Jay-Z and UGK's 'Big Pimpin''. His second album, *Shock Value* (2007), featured the #1 single 'Give It To Me' and the top-3 hit 'The Way I Are.' He produced Justin Timberlake's 'Cry Me a River' (2002) and subsequently the majority of Timberlake's releases, including *FutureSex/LoveSounds* (2006).

Timbaland began his career in the Swing Mob ensemble alongside DeVante Swing, Missy Elliott, and Magoo. He was a member of the production group S.B.I. (Surrounded By Idiots) with Pharrell Williams. His long-term partner is rapper Melvin 'Magoo' Barcliff, with whom he formed the duo Timbaland & Magoo in 1989; they released three albums on Blackground Records. He founded Beat Club in 2001 and Mosley Music Group in 2006, signing artists such as Nelly Furtado, OneRepublic, Keri Hilson, Bubba Sparxxx, and Chris Cornell.

Timbaland is widely acclaimed for his distinctive production work. His influence on pop trends was highlighted by Entertainment Weekly in 2007, which noted that trends ranging from 'sultry, urban-edged R&B songstresses' to the incorporation of avant-garde sounds into No. 1 hits could be traced back to him. He has won four Grammy Awards from 22 nominations. Along with Swizz Beatz, he co-created the webcast series Verzuz in 2020 and launched Beat Club, a mentorship service for upcoming producers.
