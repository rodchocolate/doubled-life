timbaland

Timbaland, born Timothy Zachery Mosley, is an American record producer, rapper, singer, and songwriter from Norfolk, Virginia. He is widely acclaimed for his distinctive production work and 'stuttering' rhythmic style, with Entertainment Weekly stating that 'just about every current pop trend can be traced back to him'.

Timbaland's signature sound is characterized by his distinctive 'stuttering' rhythmic style, fast-paced hip-hop beats applied to R&B, and innovative use of electronic sounds. He often incorporates avant-garde sounds into pop hits.

Timbaland's first major production credit was on Ginuwine's debut album 'Ginuwine... the Bachelor' (1996), which included the hit single 'Pony'. He also produced Aaliyah's second album 'One in a Million' (1996) and Missy Elliott's debut album 'Supa Dupa Fly' (1997). His production work on Justin Timberlake's albums, including 'Justified' (2002), 'FutureSex/LoveSounds' (2006), and 'The 20/20 Experience' (2013), has been highly influential in pop music.

Timbaland's long-term collaborators include Missy Elliott, with whom he formed the production duo The Neptunes, and rapper Magoo. He has also worked extensively with Justin Timberlake, Aaliyah, Ginuwine, and Jay-Z.

Timbaland's innovative approach to music production has had a significant impact on contemporary pop, R&B, and hip-hop. His distinctive sound has influenced numerous producers and artists, making him a canonical figure in modern music.
