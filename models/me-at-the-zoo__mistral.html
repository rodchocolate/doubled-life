me at the zoo

A zoo, short for zoological garden, is a facility where animals are kept within enclosures for exhibition and often bred for conservation purposes. The term 'zoo' was first used of the London Zoological Gardens in 1847.

The predecessor of the zoological garden is the menagerie, which has a long history from the ancient world to modern times. The oldest known zoological collection was revealed during excavations at Hierakonpolis, Egypt in 2009... The modern zoo that emerged in the 19th century in the United Kingdom was focused on providing scientific study and later educational exhibits to the public for entertainment and inspiration.

First Modern Zoo The first modern zoo was the Tierpark Hagenbeck by Carl Hagenbeck in Germany.
Annual Visitors (US) Over 181 million people annually visit zoos in the United States alone.
Oldest Zoo The oldest zoo still in existence is the Schönbrunn Zoo in Vienna, Austria.
