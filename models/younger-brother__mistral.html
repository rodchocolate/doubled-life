Younger Brother is an electronic duo formed in 2003 by Simon Posford and Benji Vaughan. They are known for their albums 'A Flock of Bleeps', 'Last Days of Gravity', and 'Vaccine'.

Younger Brother was formed in 2003 by Simon Posford (Shpongle, Hallucinogen) and Benji Vaughan (Prometheus). The band's name is taken from a Kogi prophecy that regards Westerners as Younger Brother.

Their debut album 'A Flock of Bleeps' was released in 2003, followed by 'The Last Days of Gravity' in 2007, which featured vocals by Ruu Campbell. Their third album 'Vaccine', recorded with a U.S. band lineup, became available on 4 April 2011.

'A Flock of Bleeps' led to demand for live shows, which they played from London to Tokyo. They also produced 'Phoenix in Dynamite Sky' for the British cult film 'Heartless'. In 2014, they released an alternate album of remixes titled 'Vaccine Electronic' as a free stream for users who installed their app.
