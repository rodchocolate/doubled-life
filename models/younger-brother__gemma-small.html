younger brother

Younger Brother is an electronic duo formed in 2003 by Simon Posford and Benji Vaughan. Their debut album, A Flock of Bleeps, was released that same year. The group's name has origins in a Kogi prophecy.

Simon Posford (Shpongle, Hallucinogen) and Benji Vaughan (Prometheus) formed the band after making a remix for a charity record. The name 'Younger Brother' is derived from a Kogi prophecy; according to the Kogi, they regard themselves as the Older Brother, and prophesy that Westerners will be called Younger Brother when Earth and environment are destroyed. Fragments of this prophecy appear in the song "Evil and Harm."

The duo's first album, A Flock of Bleeps (2003), became a cult classic in the underground electronica scene, featuring tracks like "Crumblenaut" and "Finger." Following this success, they released Last Days of Gravity in 2007, an album that moved toward a more organic sound. This album was inspired by their live shows and featured artwork by Storm Thorgerson. Their third album, Vaccine, followed in 2011, recorded with a U.S. band lineup. In 2014, they released the alternate remix album Vaccine Electronic as a free stream via an app.

The success of A Flock of Bleeps led to live shows for Younger Brother in locations ranging from London and Tokyo to New York and Moscow. For Last Days of Gravity, they played with a 'Younger Brother Live' band that included Marc Brownstein on bass, Tom Hamilton on guitar, and Joe Russo on drums in Japan, the US, and the UK. The group also produced "Phoenix in Dynamite Sky" for the British cult film Heartless in 2009.

The name 'Younger Brother' has been used in other media. In the realm of manga, Please Excuse My Younger Brothers is a series written and illustrated by Akira Ozaki, which began serialization in January 2020. Additionally, The Younger Brothers is the title of a 1949 American Western film directed by Edwin L. Marin.
