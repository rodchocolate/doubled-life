great woods

Great Woods, originally known as the Great Woods Center for the Performing Arts, is an outdoor amphitheater located in Mansfield, Massachusetts. Opened in 1986 with a capacity of 12,000, it was expanded after 2000 to accommodate 19,900 attendees. The venue hosts primarily concerts from mid-May until late September and has been named Top Grossing Amphitheater by Billboard.

Great Woods has hosted numerous notable performances, including Aerosmith (24 times), Avenged Sevenfold (3 times), Depeche Mode (5 times), Eagles (5 consecutive nights), Flyleaf, Gin Blossoms, James Taylor (4 consecutive dates), Jimmy Buffett & The Coral Reefer Band (63 times), Korn, Nickelback, Oasis (2 DVDs), Pearl Jam (11 times), Phish (22 times), R.E.M. (at least 7 times), Rush, Santana (regular performer), Spice Girls, Stone Sour, The Cure (4 times), The Fray, The Smiths (13 songs featured on LP re-release), and The Who.

Several performances at Great Woods have been recorded and released. Notable examples include 'Live at Great Woods' by the Allman Brothers Band (1992), Grateful Dead's performances from 1973 and 1977, Phish's performance on July 8, 1994, Jerry Garcia Band's performances in 1989 and 1990, and The Allman Brothers Band featuring Jack Pearson's performance on September 19, 1998.

Great Woods primarily hosts concerts, with a focus on rock music. It has also hosted graduation ceremonies such as that of Mansfield High School. The venue's programming includes both established and up-and-coming artists, with notable performances by legendary bands like The Who and Rush, as well as contemporary acts like Phish and Pearl Jam.
