shpongle

Shpongle is a psychedelic electronic music project from England formed in 1996 by Simon Posford and Raja Ram. The duo are considered progenitors of the psybient genre, which combines world music with psychedelic trance and ambient sounds. Their musical style integrates traditional music and vocals from around the world with contemporary western synthesizer-based psychedelic music.

Shpongle formed in 1996, consisting of Simon Posford and Raja Ram, who is one of three members of The Infinity Project. Posford is generally responsible for synthesizers, studio work, and live instrumentation, while Raja Ram contributes broad musical concepts and flute arrangements. Raja Ram has stated that "Shpongle" is an umbrella term for feeling positive and euphoric emotions. Their music is heavily influenced by psychedelic experiences and frequently uses sonic textures that approximate psychedelic states, along with vocal samples relating to consciousness expansion and altered awareness. For example, the track "A New Way to Say 'Hooray'" contains a vocal sample of Terence McKenna describing the effects of DMT. Posford has described Shpongle's music as "like nothing you've ever heard before."

Shpongle's first track, "Vapour Rumours," was released on TIP Records' Infinite Excursions compilation in 1996. Their debut album, Are You Shpongled?, followed in 1998 on Twisted Records. The group has released six studio albums: Tales of the Inexpressible (2001), Nothing Lasts... But Nothing Is Lost (2005), Ineffable Mysteries from Shpongleland (2009), Museum of Consciousness (2013), Codex VI (2017), and Improvisations for Piano & Flute (2024). On 9 September 2014, Posford confirmed work on a sixth album, which was released as Codex VI on 23 October 2017. Even before this release, the duo announced they had begun working on their seventh studio album. Since June 2017, they have been releasing remasters of previous albums, focusing on special edition vinyl records.

Shpongle's first live performance was at the Solstice Music Festival in Japan in July 2001. The band has toured extensively, including a sold-out concert at London's Roundhouse venue on 31 October 2008 with a full lineup featuring Andy Gangadeen (drums), Nogera (percussion), Chris Taylor (bass), Pete Callard (guitar), Harry Escott (cello), and Dick Trevor (keyboards). In January 2011, Shpongle toured Australia and played the Electric Forest Festival with the "Shpongletron," a visual setup designed by artist Zebbler. On 10 May 2014, they performed at Red Rocks Amphitheater in Colorado with a full 12-piece band; this concert was filmed and released on DVD in October 2015. In September 2018, Shpongle announced their last full public concerts would be two consecutive nights at Red Rocks Amphitheatre on 3–4 May 2019, though they stated they would continue releasing studio recordings.
