shpongle

Shpongle is a psychedelic electronic music project from England that formed in 1996. The group includes Simon Posford and Raja Ram, who are considered progenitors of the psybient genre. Their musical style combines traditional world music with contemporary western synthesizer-based psychedelic music.

Shpongle's sound is heavily influenced by psychedelic experiences, frequently utilizing sonic textures that approximate altered states of awareness. The duo incorporates traditional music from around the world and vocals, with Posford describing the music as 'like nothing you've ever heard before.' Raja Ram stated that "Shpongle" itself is an umbrella term for feeling positive and euphoric emotions. For example, the track "A New Way to Say 'Hooray'" features a vocal sample from Terence McKenna discussing the effects of DMT.

The duo released their first track, "Vapour Rumours," on TIP Records' Infinite Excursions compilation in 1996. Their debut album was *Are You Shpongled?* (1998), followed by *Tales of the Inexpressible* (2001). They continued to release albums such as *Nothing Lasts... But Nothing Is Lost* (2005) and *Museum of Consciousness* (2013). Posford is generally responsible for the synthesizers, studio work, and live instrumentation, while Raja Ram contributes broad musical concepts and flute arrangements.

Shpongle has performed numerous live concerts, with Simon Posford frequently touring performing DJ sets. Raja Ram occasionally accompanies these sets with live flute performances. Notable appearances include a sold-out concert at The Roundhouse venue in North London on 31 October 2008, and a full ensemble performance at Camp Bisco X in New York on 8 July 2011. In 2014, the group played with a full 12 piece band at Red Rocks Amphitheater in Colorado.

Shpongle's discography includes studio albums such as *Are You Shpongled?* (1998), *Tales of the Inexpressible* (2001), and *Codex VI* (2017). They have also released EP albums, including *Divine Moments of Truth* (2000) and *Dorset Perception* (2004). The group's material is available across various formats, including remix albums and DVD releases.
