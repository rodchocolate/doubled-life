The Menemsha Inn is a historic lodging establishment located in Menemsha, Martha's Vineyard. It was owned by Robert Nixon and his wife Sarah Thorsby Guinan.

The Menemsha Inn was owned by Robert Nixon, an American film director, writer, and conservationist. He purchased the inn along with his wife Sarah Thorsby Guinan. They also own other establishments in Martha's Vineyard such as The Beach Plum Inn and Restaurant, and Home Port Restaurant.

The Menemsha Inn is located in Menemsha, Martha's Vineyard. Martha's Vineyard is an island located south of Cape Cod in Massachusetts, with a total area of 96 square miles (250 km2).
