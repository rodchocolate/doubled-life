great woods

Great Woods, now known as the Xfinity Center in Mansfield, Massachusetts, is an outdoor amphitheater that has hosted numerous notable live music performances and recordings over its decades of operation.

Great Woods has seen a wide array of legendary acts. Notably, the Allman Brothers Band recorded their 'Live at Great Woods' DVD here in 1992. Jimmy Buffett has performed there more than any other artist, with 63 shows. Depeche Mode's final show in 2009 was recorded for their live albums project. Phish played 22 times between 1992 and 2024, including a complete Gamehendge saga performance in 1994.

Several recordings exist from Great Woods performances, including the Allman Brothers Band's 'Live at Great Woods' DVD, portions of Jimmy Buffett's 'Buffett Live: Tuesdays, Thursdays, Saturdays' and 'Encores,' Depeche Mode's live albums project, and Phish's 'A Live One.' The Jerry Garcia Band also performed there in 1989, with recordings available online.

Great Woods primarily hosts concerts during the summer months. It has a diverse lineup of artists ranging from rock legends to contemporary acts. The venue's capacity and outdoor setting make it suitable for both large-scale performances and intimate shows, attracting a wide audience.
