cercle

This collection documents the use of 'Cercle' as a brand or event series name for hosting diverse musical acts across various international locations. It is particularly valuable for documenting electronic and ambient music performances, featuring artists like Godspeed You! Black Emperor, A Silver Mt Zion, Tame Impala, and Monolink. The material provides specific setlists and recordings from unique venues globally.

Godspeed You! Black Emperor performed at CAT (Cercle des Arts Traditionnels) in Bordeaux, France on January 25, 2002. A Silver Mt Zion played at Le Cercle in Québec, QC on September 24, 2010. Other notable performances include Tame Impala at Parque de Quetzalcóatl in Mexico; Monolink at Cercle Odyssey in Paris, France; ZHU at Hakuba Iwatake in Nagano, Japan; and Folamour at Cathédrale Saint-Pierre in Geneva, Switzerland. HVOB performed live at Copa del Sol in Careyes, Mexico.

Recordings are available from multiple sources: a comprehensive set of two lossless audio recordings of Godspeed You! Black Emperor's 2002 performance (Source 1 and Source 2). Other material exists as YouTube videos documenting live sets at various locations, including Plitvice Lakes National Park in Croatia, and the general availability of specific recorded performances from other artists.

The programming is characterized by diverse international electronic music events. The featured acts include ambient rock (Godspeed You! Black Emperor), experimental folk (A Silver Mt Zion), psychedelic pop (Tame Impala), and house/disco genres (Monolink, Folamour).
