Younger Brother is an electronic duo formed in 2003 by Simon Posford and Benji Vaughan. Their debut album, A Flock of Bleeps, became a cult classic in the underground electronica scene. They have since released several albums and EPs, evolving their sound to include more organic elements.

Simon Posford and Benji Vaughan formed Younger Brother after making a remix for a charity record. Their first album, A Flock of Bleeps, was released in 2003, leading to live performances around the world. They then moved towards a more organic sound with their second album, The Last Days of Gravity, which included vocals by Ruu Campbell and featured artwork by Storm Thorgerson.

Younger Brother's third studio album, Vaccine, was released in 2011 with a lineup that included Marc Brownstein, Joe Russo, and Tom Hamilton. In 2014, they developed an app for mobile devices featuring an alternate album of remixes titled Vaccine Electronic. Benji Vaughan announced he would take a break from the band in 2014.
