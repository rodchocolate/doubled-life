30 for 30

30 for 30 is the title of three distinct subjects covered in these sources: a documentary film series about sports, a popular song by SZA and Kendrick Lamar, and an early sporting cartridge designation. The documentary series highlights significant moments and figures in sports history, while the song was released as part of SZA's album *Lana*.

The 30 for 30 film series is a collection of documentaries airing on ESPN and its sister networks that highlight interesting people and events in sports history. The concept originated in 2007 from Bill Simmons, an ESPN.com columnist, and Connor Schell. Initially titled to celebrate ESPN's 30th anniversary in 2009, Volume I premiered in October 2009, chronicling 30 stories from the network’s first three decades of existence. Subsequent volumes expanded the series beyond the 'ESPN era,' and it has also included formats like Soccer Stories and audio podcasts.

"30 for 30" is a song by SZA and Kendrick Lamar, released on January 7, 2025, as a single from SZA's first reissue album, *Lana*. The track was written by both artists, along with Bobby DeBarge, Greg Williams, Anthony Jermaine White, and Michael Uzowuru. It arrived shortly after the release of Lamar’s sixth studio album, GNX, and features SZA delivering a confident rap-singing performance.

The .30-30 Winchester cartridge (also known as 7.62×52mmR or .30 WCF) was first marketed in 1895 for the Winchester Model 1894 lever-action rifle. It was one of the United States' first small-bore sporting rifle cartridges designed for smokeless powder. The cartridge remains widely used due to its reliable effectiveness in civilian hunting applications, and it is noted for leaving lower volumes of spoiled venison after a kill.
