me at the zoo

['A zoo is a facility where animals are kept within enclosures for exhibition and often bred for conservation purposes.', "The term 'zoo' originates from the Greek words meaning 'study of animals', and it has evolved significantly since its inception in ancient times."]

Zoos are institutions that house a variety of animals for public display and conservation. They serve educational purposes, allowing visitors to learn about different species and their habitats. However, they have faced criticism regarding animal welfare concerns.

The concept of zoos dates back to ancient times with royal menageries in Egypt, Assyria, China, and Rome. The modern zoo emerged in the 19th century, evolving from private collections into public institutions focused on scientific study and education.

In recent decades, there has been increased scrutiny of zoos due to concerns about animal welfare. Efforts have been made to improve living conditions and promote conservation efforts within these facilities.
