toyota 4runner

The Toyota 4Runner is a compact SUV manufactured by Toyota since 1984, marketed globally across six generations. It was initially based on the Hilux pickup truck but has since undergone significant independent development.

The 4Runner's predecessor, the Trekker, was a third-party conversion of the Toyota Hilux built by Winnebago Industries from 1981 to 1983. The first generation 4Runner (N60) was introduced in 1983, based on the Hilux pickup truck with a removable fiberglass canopy and rear seats added.

The 4Runner has evolved from being nearly mechanically identical to the Toyota Hilux to becoming a standalone SUV. The second generation (N10) introduced in 1968 was based on the Hilux pickup truck, while the sixth generation (N250) is a purpose-built SUV.
