Mad Libs is a classic word game created by Leonard Stern and Roger Price in the United States, selling over 110 million copies since its first publication in 1958. It's renowned for its comedic phrasal template format.

Mad Libs is a game where one player prompts others for words to substitute for blanks in a story before reading aloud, resulting in comedic sentences. The game's unique phrasal template format is its signature technique.

The first book of Mad Libs, published in 1958, is considered the game's landmark record. It popularized the game and led to over 70 editions being released under Price Stern Sloan.

Mad Libs was created by Leonard Stern and Roger Price. They partnered with Larry Sloan in the early 1960s to found Price Stern Sloan, which published over 70 editions of Mad Libs.
