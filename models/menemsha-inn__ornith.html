menemsha inn

The Menemsha Inn and Cottages is a property located on Martha's Vineyard owned by filmmaker Robert Nixon and his wife Sarah Thorsby Guinan. The couple also own the Beach Plum Inn and Restaurant and the Home Port Restaurant in the same area.

The Menemsha Inn is owned by Robert Nixon, an American film director, writer, and conservationist born in 1954. His career includes producing the Academy Award-nominated film Amazon Diary and co-producing Gorillas in the Mist. He also founded the Earth Conservation Corps and Wings Over America.

Nixon resides with his wife, Sarah Thorsby Guinan, in both Washington, D.C., and Martha's Vineyard. Their son Jack helped conceive the American Heroes Fishing Challenge, which was the subject of a documentary produced and directed by Nixon and Guinan.
