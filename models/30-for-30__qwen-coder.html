30 for 30

['30 for 30 is a series of documentary films airing on ESPN and its platforms, highlighting interesting people and events in sports history.', "The series includes four volumes of 30 episodes each, an additional 13-episode series, and online shorts, expanding beyond the 'ESPN era' to cover broader sports history."]

The idea for 30 for 30 originated in 2007 from ESPN.com columnist Bill Simmons and ESPN's Connor Schell. The series was initially conceived as a celebration of ESPN's 30th anniversary with 30 one-hour films by 30 filmmakers, exploring significant sports stories from the network's first three decades.

Volume I premiered in October 2009 and covered events from ESPN's founding in 1979. Subsequent volumes and online shorts expanded the series to include a wide range of sports stories beyond ESPN's history, including Soccer Stories ahead of the 2014 FIFA World Cup.

The series received mixed critical responses, with some episodes receiving high praise while others were less favorably reviewed. Despite a slow start in terms of ratings, awareness and critical acclaim led to increased viewership over time. The series has won several awards, including the 2016 Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature for 'O.J.: Made in America'.
