30 for 30

30 for 30 is a series of documentary films airing on ESPN, highlighting interesting people and events in sports history. It began as a celebration of ESPN's 30th anniversary but has since expanded beyond the 'ESPN era'.

The series consists of four volumes, each containing 30 episodes, along with additional specials and shorts. It was created by ESPN.com columnist Bill Simmons and Connor Schell in celebration of ESPN's 30th anniversary.

Volume I premiered in October 2009 and ran to December 2010, chronicling 30 stories from the 'ESPN era'. Subsequent films expanded the series beyond this timeframe. In September 2014, Schell stated that there was no shortage of incredible moments from the world of sports, enabling them to continue making quality films.

The series has received positive reviews and critical acclaim, winning awards such as the Peabody Award (2010), International Documentary Association's Distinguished Continuing Series (2010), Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Short-Format Nonfiction Program (2014), and Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature (2016) for O.J.: Made in America.
