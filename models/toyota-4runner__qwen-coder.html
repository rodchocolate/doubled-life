The Toyota 4Runner is an SUV that was initially developed as a modified version of the short-bed Toyota Hilux pickup truck. It has undergone significant development over six generations, evolving from a compact SUV to a vehicle that blends features of both compact and mid-size SUVs.

The 4Runner's history traces back to the Toyota Trekker, a third-party conversion of the Hilux truck in the early 1980s. The first generation of the 4Runner was introduced in 1983 as a modified version of the Hilux, and it has since been developed independently by Toyota.

The 4Runner is primarily sold in North America and South America. In other markets like Europe, Asia, and the Middle East, similar vehicles such as the Land Cruiser Prado or Fortuner are offered instead. The vehicle has been marketed under different names, including Hilux Surf in Japan.
