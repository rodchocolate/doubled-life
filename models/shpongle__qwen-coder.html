Shpongle is a psychedelic electronic music project from England that formed in 1996. The group includes Simon Posford and Raja Ram, who are considered pioneers of the psybient genre. Their music blends traditional world music with contemporary western synthesizer-based psychedelic sounds.

Shpongle's first track was released in 1996 on TIP Records' Infinite Excursions compilation, and their debut album, 'Are You Shpongled?', followed in 1998. Since then, they have released several studio albums, EPs, and remixes, with their most recent full-length album being 'Codex VI' in 2017.

Shpongle has played numerous live concerts worldwide since their first performance at the Solstice Music Festival in Japan in 2001. Notable performances include headlining dates at festivals like Ozora and Rainbow Serpent, as well as sold-out shows in major cities such as New York City and Melbourne.
