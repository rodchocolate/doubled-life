great woods

Great Woods, originally the Great Woods Center for the Performing Arts, is a large outdoor amphitheater in Mansfield, Massachusetts. It has served as a major regional venue for concerts and live performances since opening in 1986, making it a significant location for capturing diverse musical acts.

The venue hosted numerous notable performances, including Aerosmith (24 times), Depeche Mode (five times; the last show in 2009 was recorded for their live albums project Recording the Universe), Eagles (performing five consecutive nights on their Hell Freezes Over Tour), and Oasis (filming two DVDs here, one in 2001, and one in 2005). The Allman Brothers Band performed multiple times, including a set filmed on September 6, 1991, and subsequent sets featuring Jack Pearson on September 19, 1998. Other acts include Pearl Jam (11 times), Phish (22 times between 1992 and 2024), Rush (featuring two songs from a June 23, 1997 concert), The Who (filming the DVD The Who: Live in Boston in September 2002), and Jerry Garcia Band (performing on September 10, 1989).

The venue has been used for filming multiple live albums and concert DVDs. Specific recordings include the Allman Brothers Band's 'Live at Great Woods' (recorded September 6, 1991), which was released on VHS and LaserDisc in 1992, with later versions available on DVD. Other recorded material includes Phish performing a track from A Live One (1994) and completing their Gamehendge saga; the Allman Brothers Band's sets featuring Jack Pearson were filmed for online viewing; and The Who: Live in Boston was filmed here.

The venue is primarily an outdoor amphitheater that operates during the summer months, typically from mid-May until late September. It has hosted a wide variety of genres, ranging from rock (Aerosmith, Pearl Jam) to classic acts (Eagles, James Taylor), and jazz/jam bands (Grateful Dead, Phish). The venue's history includes being proposed as a performing arts center before becoming an amphitheater.
