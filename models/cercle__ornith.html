cercle

Cercle is a French music company that produces immersive electronic music performances filmed at iconic global locations. The project pairs artists with striking natural and architectural backdrops, creating visually driven live recordings rather than traditional concert documentation.

Cercle has produced performances by Disclosure at Plitvice Lakes National Park in Croatia, Tame Impala at Parque de Quetzalcóatl in Mexico, Monolink at Cercle Odyssey in Paris, France, ZHU at Hakuba Iwatake in Nagano, Japan, Folamour at Cathédrale Saint-Pierre in Geneva, Switzerland (in partnership with W Hotels), and HVOB at Copa del Sol in Careyes, Mexico. These performances are distributed through the official Cercle YouTube channel.

Cercle recordings exist primarily as video content on YouTube under the 'Cercle' channel. The project also operates a sub-brand called 'Cercle Odyssey,' which produced a Paris performance by Monolink. Additionally, Cercle has collaborated with W Hotels for select events.

The Cercle project emphasizes location-driven performances, featuring electronic artists at natural landmarks (Plitvice Lakes, Hakuba Iwatake), architectural sites (Cathédrale Saint-Pierre), and resort venues (Parque de Quetzalcóatl, Copa del Sol). The format prioritizes visual spectacle alongside musical performance.
