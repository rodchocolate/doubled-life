nile rodgers

Nile Rodgers is an American guitarist and co-founder of Chic, a disco band that released several hits in the late 70s. He has written, produced, and performed on records that have sold over 750 million albums and 100 million singles worldwide.

Rodgers is known for his distinctive chucking guitar style. His signature sound is characterized by a fusion of jazz, soul, funk grooves with European influences in melodies and lyrics.

'Le Freak' (1978) was Chic's biggest hit, selling over seven million singles worldwide. 'Good Times' (1979), another Chic track, became one of the most-sampled songs in music history.

Rodgers co-founded Chic with bassist Bernard Edwards and drummer Tony Thompson. They worked extensively together, producing hits for Sister Sledge ('We Are Family', 'He's the Greatest Dancer'), Diana Ross ('Upside Down', 'I'm Coming Out'), and others.

Rodgers is a Songwriters Hall of Fame inductee, Rock and Roll Hall of Fame inductee via the Award for Musical Excellence, and Grammy Lifetime Achievement/Special Merit Award recipient. Rolling Stone ranked him 7th on their list of the 250 greatest guitarists of all time.
