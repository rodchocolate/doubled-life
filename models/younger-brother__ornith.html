Younger Brother is an electronic duo formed in 2003 by Simon Posford (of Shpongle and Hallucinogen) and Benji Vaughan (of Prometheus). The band's name was taken from a Kogi prophecy, where the Kogi regarded themselves as the Older Brother and identified Westerners as Younger Brother, whom they believed would cause the destruction of the Earth. Their debut album, A Flock of Bleeps (2003), became a cult classic in the underground electronica scene. They followed this with The Last Days of Gravity (2007) and Vaccine (2011). In 2014, they released an alternate remix album titled Vaccine Electronic via a mobile app.

Please Excuse My Younger Brothers is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Akira Ozaki. It began serialization in Shueisha's shōjo manga magazine Bessatsu Margaret in January 2020. The plot follows Ito Narita, who moves after her mother remarries and discovers she has four younger brothers: Gen, Raku, Shū, and Rui. A live-action film adaptation premiered in Japanese theaters in December 2024, followed by an anime television series produced by Lay-duce that premiered in July 2026.

The Younger Brothers is a 1949 American Western film directed by Edwin L. Marin and starring Wayne Morris, Bruce Bennett, and Janis Paige. The plot centers on Cole, Jim, and Bob Younger, who ride to Cedar Creek, Minnesota, for a parole hearing in an attempt to reform from their outlaw ways.
