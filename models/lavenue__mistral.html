lavenue

Lavenue is a historic cookery school founded by Felix Lavenue, later acquired and renamed by Agnes Marshall, a prominent English culinary entrepreneur known for her work on ice cream.

The Lavenue cookery school was founded by Charles Shepperd under the pseudonym Felix Lavenue. Agnes Marshall and her husband Alfred acquired the school in 1883, renaming it the Marshall's School of Cookery. It taught a mixture of high-end English and French cuisine and became one of two major cookery schools in London.

Agnes Bertha Marshall (born Agnes Beere Smith) was an English culinary entrepreneur, inventor, and celebrity chef. She popularised ice cream in England and is often regarded as the inventor of the modern ice cream cone. Marshall wrote four cookbooks, including two devoted to ice cream and other desserts.
