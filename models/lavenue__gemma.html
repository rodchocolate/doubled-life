lavenue

Lavenue refers to the cookery school founded by Felix Lavenue, a pseudonym for Englishman Charles Shepperd. The institution was located at 67 Mortimer Street.

The Lavenue cookery school was established by Charles Shepperd, who used the name Felix Lavenue. Shepperd died in 1864, after which the school was run by his daughter, Agnes Mary Lavenue, who died a few months after selling the business in January 1883. In January 1883, the school was acquired by Agnes Bertha Marshall and her husband Alfred William Marshall. Following the purchase, the Marshalls renamed the institution as the Marshall's School of Cookery.

Shortly after the acquisition in 1883, the school was moved from its original location at 67 Mortimer Street to 30 Mortimer St because the building at 67 Mortimer Street was scheduled for demolition.
