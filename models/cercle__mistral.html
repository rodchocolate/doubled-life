Cercle Brugge KSV is primarily known as a Belgian football club based in Bruges. However, it has also hosted notable musical performances and events under the name 'Cercle'.

Godspeed You! Black Emperor performed at Cercle des Arts Traditionnels (CAT) in Bordeaux, France on 2002-01-25. The performance was recorded and is available on Archive.org with two different lossless audio recordings. A Silver Mt Zion performed at Le Cercle in Québec, Canada on 2010-09-24. The performance was recorded by 'emmanuel' and is also available on Archive.org. Cercle has hosted unique musical events in various locations around the world, such as Disclosure performing at Plitvice Lakes National Park in Croatia, Tame Impala at Parque de Quetzalcóatl in Mexico, Monolink live at Cercle Odyssey in Paris, France, ZHU at Hakuba Iwatake in Nagano, Japan, Folamour at Cathédrale Saint-Pierre in Geneva, Switzerland, and HVOB live at Copa del Sol in Careyes, Mexico.

The performances by Godspeed You! Black Emperor and A Silver Mt Zion have been preserved as audio recordings on Archive.org. The unique musical events hosted by Cercle are available as videos on YouTube.
