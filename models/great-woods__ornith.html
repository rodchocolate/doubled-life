great woods

Great Woods, now known as the Xfinity Center in Mansfield, Massachusetts, is an outdoor amphitheatre that opened in June 1986 and has hosted nearly 80 concerts per season at its peak. It matters for live recordings because it served as a filming location for multiple commercial releases (Oasis, The Who, Aerosmith) and a recording site for official live albums by Depeche Mode, the Eagles, and Jimmy Buffett. The venue also sits inside a robust fan-trading ecosystem, with soundboard recordings of Phish, the Jerry Garcia Band, and the Allman Brothers Band circulating on YouTube and archive.org.

Jimmy Buffett & The Coral Reefer Band hold the venue record with 63 shows; portions of Buffett Live: Tuesdays, Thursdays, Saturdays (1999), Encores (2008), and the Live in Mansfield, MA Soundboard album (2003) were captured there. Phish performed at Great Woods 22 times between 1992 and 2024; a track from A Live One (1994) was recorded on July 8, 1994, the same night they played their complete Gamehendge saga (a full recording of that show is available on YouTube). The Allman Brothers Band filmed Live at Great Woods Amphitheater on September 6, 1991 for Japanese TV, released on VHS/LaserDisc in 1992 and DVD in 1998/2014/2021. Aerosmith played the venue 24 times; their live performance was filmed there for the movie Be Cool. Depeche Mode performed here five times, with the last show in 2009 recorded for their Recording the Universe live albums project. The Who filmed The Who: Live in Boston at Great Woods in September 2002. Oasis filmed two DVDs here (2001 and 2005). Pearl Jam played 11 times; on July 11, 2003 they delivered their longest show up to that point.

Commercial releases captured at Great Woods include the Allman Brothers Band's Live at Great Woods (1992 VHS/LaserDisc, 1998/2014/2021 DVD), Depeche Mode's Recording the Universe project (2009 show), the Eagles' second live album from five consecutive nights on their Hell Freezes Over tour (1994), Jimmy Buffett's Live in Mansfield, MA Soundboard (2003) plus portions of two other Buffett DVDs, Oasis's two concert DVDs (2001 and 2005), The Who: Live in Boston (2002), the Family Values (2006) DVD featuring Flyleaf, Korn, and Stone Sour, Aerosmith's Be Cool performance, Gin Blossoms' "As Long as It Matters" video (WBCN River Rave, June 8, 1996), Nickelback's "Rockstar" video, The Smiths' 2017 re-release of The Queen is Dead featuring thirteen songs from August 5, 1986, Rush's Different Stages album with two songs including a full "2112" from June 23, 1997, and the Jerry Garcia Band's September 10, 1989 soundboard release. Fan recordings of Phish (July 8/9, 1994) and the Allman Brothers Band (September 19, 1998) are also available on YouTube.

Great Woods is an outdoor amphitheatre in Mansfield, Massachusetts, within 40 miles of Providence, Boston, Worcester, and Cape Cod. It opened June 13, 1986 with Yo-Yo Ma and the Pittsburgh Symphony Orchestra, was expanded after 2000 to a capacity of 19,900 (7,000 reserved, 7,000 lawn, 5,900 general admission), and runs a summer season from mid-May through late September. It was named Top Grossing Amphitheater by Billboard in 2010 and averages about 36 events per season today after regional competition from the Xfinity Theatre (Hartford) and Leader Bank Pavilion (Boston). Artists with deep ties to the venue include Aerosmith (24 shows), Phish (22 shows between 1992–2024), Pearl Jam (11 shows), Depeche Mode (5 shows), Jimmy Buffett (63 shows), R.E.M. (at least 7 shows including a three-night stand in 1989), and Santana (regular performer since 1986). The venue also hosted the Spice Girls on July 8, 1998 as part of their Spiceworld Tour.
