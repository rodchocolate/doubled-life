10 ski mountains as first-person explorers built from public data — USGS lidar terrain, OpenStreetMap runs and lifts, SNOTEL snow by day — each with the swatch packet and deep research behind it. Walk: WASD or thumb-pad; drag to look; tap the map to travel; M for the big map; the date picker runs the real 2024–25 season.
